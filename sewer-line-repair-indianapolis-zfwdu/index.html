Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of sewer line problems do you handle?

We address a range of issues, including pipe cracks and breaks, root intrusion, drain field failures, clogs, backups, and general sewer line deterioration specific to properties in the Indianapolis area.

Q: How quickly can you respond to an emergency sewer line issue?

Emergencies are our priority. We aim to dispatch a team promptly to assess the situation and begin necessary repairs as quickly and safely as possible, recognizing the urgency of sewer line failures.

Q: Do you offer sewer line camera inspections?

Yes, we utilize sewer line camera inspections to accurately diagnose the source and extent of potential problems within your specific sewer line at your property.

Q: What are the signs of a failing sewer line?

Common signs include foul odors near plumbing fixtures or the yard, recurring drain clogs, unusually green grass over the pipe's location, wet spots, gurgling sounds from drains, and sewage backups.

Q: How long does a typical sewer line repair take?

The duration depends on the specific issue, its complexity, and the chosen repair method. We provide a clear timeline after inspection and discuss it thoroughly with you before starting any work.
