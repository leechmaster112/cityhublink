FAQs

Q1: How long does a typical helicopter tour flight last?

A1: Our standard sightseeing flights usually last approximately 20 minutes, providing a perfect balance of aerial adventure and sightseeing time over Miami Beach.

Q2: Can I get a private tour?

A2: Yes, we offer private helicopter tours departing from our base at 1626 Collins Ave. These personalized experiences allow you to customize your flight and enjoy exclusive views of Miami Beach.

Q3: What is included in the tour price?

A3: The standard tour price includes a pre-flight briefing, the helicopter flight itself, and a professional pilot. We also offer the option to add champagne or other refreshments.

Q4: What are the cancellation policies?

A4: We require 24 hours notice for any flight changes or cancellations. In case of flight cancellation due to weather conditions, we will reschedule your flight free of charge or provide a full refund.

Q5: Are there weight restrictions for passengers?

A5: Yes, our helicopters have weight and height restrictions for safety. Passengers must be under 250 pounds and fit within the designated seating area. Children under 5 may fly without a seat, but must be accompanied by an adult.
