Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of transmission repairs do you offer at your Springfield location?

We offer a comprehensive range of transmission repairs at our Springfield shop, including diagnostic services, fluid and filter replacements, transmission rebuilds, and repairs for issues like slipping gears, delayed shifting, and transmission leaks.

Q2: How often should I get a transmission fluid change for my vehicle?

While manufacturer recommendations vary, we generally recommend transmission fluid and filter changes every 50,000 miles or 5-7 years, whichever comes first. Regular changes help maintain transmission performance and longevity.

Q3: Can you rebuild transmissions from all makes and models?

Yes, our skilled technicians at Ace Transmission Service in Springfield are experienced with rebuilding transmissions from virtually all makes and models. We stay updated with the latest technology to handle modern transmission systems effectively.

Q4: What is the typical turnaround time for a transmission repair at your shop?

The turnaround time depends on the complexity of the repair. Simple fluid flushes might be completed the same day, while more extensive repairs or rebuilds could take several days. We will provide you with a specific timeline once the diagnosis is complete.

Q5: Do you offer mobile transmission services in Springfield?

No, Ace Transmission Service currently operates as a fixed-location shop at 2610 W. Kearney St in Springfield. We recommend bringing your vehicle to our facility for the most accurate diagnosis and repairs.
