Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does a new pool installation typically take?

A: The timeline for a new pool installation can vary depending on the complexity of the design, weather conditions, and site preparation. Generally, the process takes between 3 to 6 weeks from the initial design phase to the final completion of the pool.

Q2: What types of pool surfaces do you offer?

A: We offer a variety of high-quality pool surface options, including popular choices like Pebble Tec, which provides a textured, durable finish, as well as options like plaster and tile for a more traditional look.

Q3: How often should I have my pool water tested?

A: For optimal pool maintenance, water should be tested at least twice a week during the swimming season. Regular testing ensures proper chemical balance, preventing issues like algae growth and equipment damage.

Q4: Can you repair an old or damaged pool liner?

A: Yes, we can often repair older pool liners to extend their lifespan. The extent of the damage and the age of the liner will determine the best course of action, which could range from patching minor tears to recommending a liner replacement.

Q5: What is included in a typical pool maintenance package?

A: Our standard maintenance packages typically include skimming the surface, cleaning filters, testing and balancing water chemistry, checking equipment operation, and addressing any minor issues to keep your pool running smoothly.
