Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of custom homes do you build?

We build a variety of custom residential properties, including single-family homes, additions/remodels, and specialty projects, all designed to meet the specific needs of our Austin clients.

Q2: How long does a custom home project typically take?

The timeline varies based on project complexity, but typically ranges from 6-12 months for standard custom home construction in the Austin area.

Q3: Can I make changes during construction?

Absolutely! We encourage client involvement and welcome design modifications throughout the construction process to ensure your vision is realized.

Q4: What is your process for selecting materials?

We guide clients through material selection, offering expert advice on quality, durability, and aesthetics while balancing budget considerations.

Yes, our design-build approach allows us to handle both the design and construction phases under one contract, providing streamlined project management for our Austin clients.
