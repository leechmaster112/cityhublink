Our Paint Protection Film Services

PPF, often called "bumper guards," is a clear urethane film applied to vulnerable areas of your vehicle. This revolutionary protection shields your car's factory paint from chips, scratches, rock chips, and environmental damage. At our Syracuse location, we offer precise application to contours like front bumpers, side rocker panels, hood, and fenders. The film seamlessly integrates with your vehicle's factory paint, maintaining its original appearance while providing a built-in repair system that fixes minor damage instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does PPF installation typically take?

A: Most PPF installations at our Syracuse location take 1-3 days depending on the vehicle size and complexity. We schedule appointments to minimize inconvenience and ensure proper curing time between applications.

Q2: Can ceramic coating be applied over existing wax?

A: Yes, our ceramic coating adheres well to properly prepared surfaces including wax, sealants, and factory coatings. However, we recommend a thorough surface decontamination before application for optimal results.

Q3: How long does ceramic coating last?

A: With proper maintenance, our ceramic coating typically lasts 2-5 years. The longevity depends on driving conditions, washing frequency, and environmental factors specific to the Syracuse area.

Q4: What's the difference between PPF and clear bra?

A: These terms are interchangeable – clear bra is simply another name for Paint Protection Film. The film is clear and designed to protect and blend with your vehicle's factory paint.

Q5: How should I prepare my vehicle for PPF application?

A: We recommend washing your vehicle thoroughly before your appointment. Remove all personal items and clean the interior completely. Our technicians will handle all surface preparation at our Syracuse facility.
