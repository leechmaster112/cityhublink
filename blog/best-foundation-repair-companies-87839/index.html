Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I know if I need foundation repair services?

Common signs include visible cracks in walls or floors, sticking doors or windows, uneven floors, bowing basement walls, and water intrusion. If you notice any of these, it's wise to consult with foundation repair companies.

Q2: What are the most common types of foundation repairs?

The most common types include piering (push piers and helical piers) for settling foundations, carbon fiber reinforcement for bowing walls, crack injection for sealing cracks, and mudjacking or slab jacking for lifting sunken concrete slabs.

Q3: How much does foundation repair typically cost?

The cost varies significantly depending on the type and severity of the issue, the repair methods used, and the geographic location. Simple crack repairs might cost a few hundred dollars, while major structural stabilization can run into tens of thousands.

Q4: What is the difference between foundation repair and waterproofing?

Foundation repair focuses on addressing structural issues and ensuring the stability of the foundation itself. Waterproofing focuses on preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space, which often helps prevent future foundation damage and can resolve issues caused by water saturation.

Q5: Are foundation repair warranties transferable?

Some companies offer transferable warranties, which can be a significant selling point if you plan to sell your home in the future. It's essential to inquire about the warranty details when getting estimates from foundation repair companies.

Q6: How long does foundation repair take?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the repair. Minor crack repairs can often be completed in a day, while extensive underpinning or structural stabilization projects can take several days to a few weeks.

Q7: Should I get multiple quotes from foundation repair companies?

Yes, it is highly recommended to get at least three detailed quotes from reputable foundation repair companies. This helps you compare pricing, understand different proposed solutions, and gauge the expertise of each contractor.

Q8: What questions should I ask potential foundation repair companies?

Ask about their experience with your specific problem, the materials they use, their warranty policies, licensing and insurance, and if they provide a detailed explanation of the repair process and cost breakdown.

Q9: Can I repair my foundation myself?

For minor cosmetic cracks, some DIY solutions might exist. However, for any signs of structural distress, sinking, or significant bowing, it is crucial to hire professional foundation repair companies. Improper DIY repairs can exacerbate the problem and lead to more costly fixes.

Q10: How do I choose the best foundation repair company for my needs?

Consider their experience, specialization, customer reviews, warranty offerings, transparency, and whether they offer integrated solutions like waterproofing if needed. A company like Cornerstone Foundation Repair & Waterproofing excels in offering a complete package.
