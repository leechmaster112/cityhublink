What are the signs I need a new roof in Austin?
Common signs you need a new roof in Austin include missing or cracked shingles, granule loss in gutters, sagging areas of the roof, algae or moss growth (especially after rainy periods), and an increase in energy bills due to poor insulation. Regular inspections by a professional, such as from Altair Austin Roofing Company, can help identify issues early.
Is metal roofing a good option for Austin homes?
Yes, metal roofing is an excellent option for homes in Austin. It's durable, energy-efficient (reflecting heat), and can withstand the Texas climate, including strong winds and hail. Many companies, including Altair Austin Roofing Company, offer various metal roofing styles and colors to suit different aesthetics.
