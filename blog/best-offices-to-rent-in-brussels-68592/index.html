2. Betacowork

Betacowork is a well-established coworking space known for its vibrant community and extensive network. Located in Brussels, it provides a dynamic environment for freelancers, startups, and small to medium-sized businesses. The space emphasizes collaboration and knowledge sharing, making it an excellent hub for innovation and growth. With a variety of membership plans, Betacowork offers flexibility for individuals and teams.

  • Pros: Strong community focus, excellent networking events, flexible membership options, well-equipped common areas, strategic location with good transport links.
  • Cons: Can be noisy at times due to the open and collaborative nature, private office availability might be limited.
  • Who it's best for: Freelancers, digital nomads, startups seeking a collaborative atmosphere, and small teams looking for cost-effective office solutions with networking opportunities.

3. AAB Business Center

AAB Business Center offers a professional and fully serviced office environment designed to support businesses of all sizes. Situated in a convenient part of Brussels, it provides a range of private offices, meeting rooms, and virtual office services. The focus is on providing a seamless operational experience for tenants, with all essential business services included.

  • Who it's best for: Established SMEs, corporate branches, legal and financial firms, and businesses that require a professional and serviced office environment with minimal operational hassle.

4. Silversquare
