FAQ

What is the most popular type of exotic car to rent in Las Vegas in 2026?

Sports cars and supercars, such as Lamborghini, Ferrari, and McLaren, are extremely popular. However, luxury SUVs and sedans are also in demand.

How much does it cost to rent an exotic car in Las Vegas?

The cost varies greatly depending on the car model, rental duration, and time of year. Expect to pay a few hundred dollars per day for some models to several thousand dollars per day for the most exclusive vehicles.

What do I need to rent an exotic car in Las Vegas?

You typically need a valid driver's license, proof of insurance, a major credit card, and to meet age requirements (usually 25 years or older). Some companies may also require a security deposit.

Are there any age restrictions for renting exotic cars in Las Vegas?

Yes, most rental companies require renters to be at least 25 years old. Some may consider renters between 21 and 24, but usually with additional fees or restrictions.

How far in advance should I book my exotic car rental?

It's always a good idea to book as far in advance as possible, especially if you have a specific model in mind or are renting during peak season. A few weeks or even months in advance is recommended.

Do exotic car rental companies in Las Vegas offer delivery services?

Many companies offer delivery services to hotels, the airport, or other locations within the Las Vegas area for added convenience.

What kind of insurance do I need to rent an exotic car?

You’ll usually need to provide proof of your own insurance. The rental company might also offer additional insurance options for damage and liability.

What is the best way to choose the right exotic car rental company?

Consider the fleet selection, customer reviews, pricing, and included services. Reading reviews and comparing different companies will help you make the best decision. Consider the experience that you want to have and select the company that best aligns with that experience.

Where can I rent an exotic car in Las Vegas?

You can find several specialized exotic car rental companies, as well as some larger rental agencies that offer exotic car collections. Research online to compare your options.

Can I rent an exotic car for a special occasion in Las Vegas?

Absolutely! Exotic car rentals are popular for weddings, bachelor/bachelorette parties, corporate events, and other special occasions in Las Vegas. Many companies offer packages for events.

What happens if I damage the rented exotic car?

The rental agreement will outline your responsibilities in the event of damage. It is crucial to understand the terms of your insurance coverage and the policies of the rental company.
