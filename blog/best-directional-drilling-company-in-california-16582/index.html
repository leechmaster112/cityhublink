Frequently Asked Questions

What is directional drilling?

Directional drilling, also known as horizontal directional drilling (HDD), is a trenchless method of installing underground infrastructure such as pipelines, conduits, or cables along a prescribed bore path with minimal surface disruption. It allows for the installation of utilities under roads, rivers, existing structures, and environmentally sensitive areas.

What services does a directional drilling company typically provide?

A full-service directional drilling company offers a range of services, including project planning, permitting assistance, site preparation, pilot hole drilling, hole enlargement, product installation, and site restoration. Hydro excavation services are often offered for precise and safe potholing and daylighting of existing utilities to avoid damage during drilling.

How do I choose the right directional drilling company?

Consider factors such as the company's experience, their project portfolio and references, the equipment they use, their safety record, and their commitment to environmental responsibility. Assess their understanding of local regulations, their ability to deliver on time and within budget, and whether they offer comprehensive services.

What are the benefits of using directional drilling?

The benefits include minimal surface disturbance, reduced environmental impact, cost-effectiveness (compared to open-cut methods in certain situations), the ability to cross obstacles, reduced traffic disruption, and enhanced safety.

What types of projects require directional drilling?

Directional drilling is used for various projects, including the installation of utilities (water, gas, electric, fiber optic cables), sewer and drain lines, pipelines, and the construction of tunnels.

What are the environmental considerations of directional drilling?

Environmental considerations include the potential for inadvertent returns (frac-outs), which is the release of drilling fluid into the surrounding soil or water. Companies should follow specific protocols to prevent or mitigate environmental impact.

How long does a directional drilling project take?

The duration varies depending on the project's complexity, the distance of the bore, the soil conditions, and any obstructions. Simpler projects may take a few days, while complex ones may take several weeks or months.

What is hydro excavation and why is it important?

Hydro excavation uses high-pressure water and a vacuum system to precisely excavate and expose underground utilities. It is essential for verifying utility locations, reducing the risk of hitting existing infrastructure, and for safe potholing prior to drilling.

What are some important safety considerations for directional drilling?

Safety considerations include proper training, the use of personal protective equipment, careful site preparation, utility location, adherence to industry standards, and emergency response planning.

How can I get a quote for a directional drilling project?

Contact multiple directional drilling companies (including Devco Development and Engineering Directional Drilling and Hydro Excavation) directly, provide detailed information about your project, and request a detailed quote. Be sure to consider their experience when making a final decision.

What are the costs associated with directional drilling?

Costs depend on project complexity, bore distance, equipment, and other factors. However, directional drilling can often be more cost-effective than traditional open-cut methods.
