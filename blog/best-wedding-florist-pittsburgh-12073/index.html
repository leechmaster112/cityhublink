Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions to guide you during your search:

What services do the best wedding florists in Pittsburgh offer?

The best wedding florists in Pittsburgh typically offer a range of services, including custom bouquet design, ceremony arrangements, reception centerpieces, and delivery and setup. Some also provide additional services like floral crowns, corsages, and personal flowers for the wedding party. Planning consultations are a standard part of most florist services.

How far in advance should I book a wedding florist in Pittsburgh?

It's best to book your wedding florist as early as possible, ideally 6-12 months before your wedding date, especially if your wedding is during peak season (May-October) in 2025. This ensures you'll be able to work with your preferred florist and secure your desired date.

What is the average cost of wedding flowers in Pittsburgh?

The cost of wedding flowers varies widely depending on the size and complexity of your arrangements, the types of flowers you choose, and the florist's pricing structure. On average, you can expect to spend anywhere from $2,000 to $10,000 or more for wedding flowers, not including delivery fees.

How do I choose the right wedding florist for my Pittsburgh wedding?

Consider your style, budget, and the level of service you need. Research florists in Pittsburgh, look at their portfolios, read reviews, and schedule consultations. Ask plenty of questions about their experience, flower selection, and design process. Make sure to assess their communication style, as well.

What questions should I ask a potential wedding florist?

Ask about their experience with weddings, their design process, flower availability, pricing structure, and cancellation policies. Inquire about their experience with your preferred wedding venue, as well. Also, ask for examples of past wedding work similar to the vision you have in mind.

Do wedding florists in Pittsburgh offer consultations?

Yes, most reputable wedding florists in Pittsburgh offer consultations. This is an opportunity to discuss your vision, budget, and preferences, and for the florist to provide a quote and preliminary design ideas. During the consultation, you can share your inspiration images, color palette, and any specific requests.

What types of flowers are best for a Pittsburgh wedding?

The best flowers depend on your personal preferences, but popular choices include seasonal blooms like roses, peonies, hydrangeas, and dahlias. Consider the overall style of your wedding and the color scheme you've chosen when making your flower selections.

How can I make my wedding flowers sustainable?

You can ask your florist to source flowers locally, use seasonal blooms, avoid floral foam, and choose eco-friendly packaging. Consider donating your flowers to a nursing home or other organization after the wedding. Discuss composting options, as well.

Which Pittsburgh wedding florist is best for a unique style?

Lilac & Fawn, Allison McGeary Flowers, and possibly Hens & Chicks are all known for their unique floral design styles and ability to craft custom arrangements tailored to individual tastes. Consider researching their portfolios to see their past work.

Should I rent or buy vases and containers for my wedding flowers?

Many florists offer vase rentals, which can be a more cost-effective and convenient option. Discuss your needs with your florist so they can help determine the best vase options for your budget and style.

How do I know if my wedding florist is a good fit?

Choose a florist whose design style aligns with your vision, who communicates well, and who understands your budget. Pay attention to how the florist responds to your ideas and if they seem enthusiastic about making your floral dreams come to life.
