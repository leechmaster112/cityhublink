FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions About TMS Therapy in Norwood MA

1. What is TMS therapy and how does it work?

TMS therapy, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a noninvasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. These pulses target specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, aiming to alleviate symptoms of depression and other mental health conditions. In Norwood, MA, providers like Onward Psychiatry utilize this technology.

2. Is TMS therapy a painful procedure?

TMS therapy is generally well-tolerated and is not considered painful. Patients may experience a mild tapping sensation on the scalp during the treatment, but discomfort is usually minimal. Your provider in Norwood, MA, will ensure your comfort throughout the process.
