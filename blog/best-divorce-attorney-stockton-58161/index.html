What is the average timeline for a divorce in Stockton, California?

In California, there is a mandatory six-month waiting period from the date of service of the divorce petition before a divorce can be finalized. However, many divorces, especially those involving complex issues like asset division or child custody, can take much longer—often a year or more. Attorneys like those at Davalos Law Firm PC work to streamline the process where possible.

What is a retainer fee for a divorce attorney?

A retainer fee is an upfront payment made to a divorce attorney, which goes into a trust account. The attorney then bills against this retainer for their time and services. Any unused portion of the retainer is typically returned to the client at the end of the representation. Davalos Law Firm PC will discuss their retainer policies during your initial consultation.

How does spousal support work in California divorces?

Spousal support (alimony) in California is determined by various factors, including the length of the marriage, the income and earning capacity of each spouse, the standard of living during the marriage, and the needs of each party. The goal is often to help a lower-earning spouse become self-supporting. Experienced attorneys like those at Davalos Law Firm PC can guide you through this process.

What are common grounds for divorce in Stockton?

California is a no-fault divorce state, meaning you don't need to prove wrongdoing by the other spouse. The two grounds for divorce are irreconcilable differences and incurable insanity. Most divorces are filed based on irreconcilable differences, the most common and straightforward ground.

How is child custody determined in Stockton courts?

In Stockton, like all of California, child custody decisions are based on the "best interests of the child." Courts consider factors such as the child's physical and emotional well-being, each parent's ability to provide care, any history of domestic violence, and the child's wishes if they are of sufficient age and maturity. Davalos Law Firm PC prioritizes child-centered resolutions.

What is community property in a Stockton divorce?

Community property in California refers to assets and debts acquired by a married couple during the marriage, regardless of who earned or paid for them. This typically includes income, real estate, vehicles, and investments. Community property is generally divided equally between the spouses in a divorce. Navigating complex property division is a specialty of firms like Davalos Law Firm PC.

Do I need to hire a divorce attorney if my divorce is amicable?

Even in amicable divorces, it is highly recommended to consult with or hire a divorce attorney. An attorney can ensure that all legal requirements are met, that agreements are fair and legally sound, and that your rights are protected, especially concerning property division, support, and child custody.
