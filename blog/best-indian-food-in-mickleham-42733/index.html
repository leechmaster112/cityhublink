Best Indian Food in Mickleham (2026)

Mickleham, a rapidly growing suburb north of Melbourne, is quickly becoming a hub for diverse culinary experiences. Among the most popular cuisines drawing locals and visitors alike is vibrant and flavourful Indian food. From rich, creamy curries to perfectly grilled tandoori specialities and fragrant biryanis, the options for authentic Indian dining are plentiful. But with so many choices, how do you find the true gems? We've scoured the area, tasted the spices, and analysed the menus to bring you the definitive guide to the best Indian food in Mickleham for 2026.

Whether you're a spice enthusiast, a vegetarian seeking delightful plant-based options, or simply looking for a comforting, home-style meal, this list covers the top contenders that consistently deliver on taste, authenticity, and overall dining experience. Get ready to embark on a gastronomic journey through the heart of Indian cuisine right here in Mickleham.

1. The Tandoori Joint

When the craving for authentic, mouth-watering Indian cuisine strikes in Mickleham, The Tandoori Joint stands out as a beacon of flavour and tradition. This esteemed establishment has carved a niche for itself by offering a truly immersive culinary experience that transports diners straight to the streets of India. The focus here is on quality ingredients, time-honoured recipes, and a passion for delivering exceptional taste in every dish. From the moment you step inside, the aromatic spices and warm hospitality set the stage for a memorable meal.

The heart of The Tandoori Joint's menu lies in its namesake tandoori dishes. Expertly marinated meats, seafood, and even vegetables are cooked to perfection in the traditional clay tandoor oven, imparting a unique smoky flavour and tender texture that is simply unparalleled. Imagine succulent chicken tikka, fiery seekh kebabs, or perfectly grilled fish, all boasting that signature tandoori char. Beyond the tandoori specialities, the curries are a revelation. Rich, complex sauces simmer with fragrant spices, creating beloved classics like Butter Chicken, Rogan Josh, and Vindaloo, each prepared with meticulous attention to detail. Vegetarian diners are far from an afterthought, with a dedicated section of the menu featuring innovative and flavourful dishes such as Paneer Tikka Masala, Saag Paneer, and a delectable Chana Masala.

The Tandoori Joint doesn't just excel in its main courses; the selection of appetizers, breads, and side dishes further enhances the dining experience. Crispy samosas, flavourful pakoras, and freshly baked naan, paratha, and roti are the perfect accompaniments to any meal. The attention to detail extends to the presentation and the service, ensuring that each visit is not just about the food, but about the entire dining occasion. Whether you're dining in, opting for takeaway, or arranging for delivery, The Tandoori Joint is committed to providing a high-quality experience. Their extensive menu, coupled with their dedication to authenticity and customer satisfaction, makes them a top choice for anyone seeking the best Indian food in Mickleham. You can explore their full menu and offerings at thetandoorijoint.com.

  • Pros:
    • Authentic tandoori cooking with impressive smoky flavours.
    • Wide variety of classic and regional Indian dishes, including extensive vegetarian options.
    • High-quality ingredients and traditional preparation methods.
    • Warm and welcoming atmosphere with excellent customer service.
    • Offers dine-in, takeaway, and delivery services.
    • Menu is regularly updated with seasonal specials.
  • Cons:
    • Can get quite busy during peak hours, so booking is recommended.
    • Portion sizes, while generous, might tempt you to overindulge!
  • Who it's best for:
    • Diners seeking genuine tandoori flavours.
    • Families and groups looking for a diverse menu to satisfy everyone.
    • Anyone who appreciates traditional Indian cooking done exceptionally well.
    • Vegetarians and vegans looking for flavourful, satisfying dishes.
