Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What types of services do financial advisors for lottery winners typically offer?
A: Advisors often provide investment management, tax planning, estate planning, retirement planning, philanthropic guidance, and risk management.

Q: How do I find a financial advisor who specializes in working with lottery winners?
A: Look for advisors with experience in managing substantial wealth, inquire about their experience with lottery winners, and review client testimonials.

Q: What are the key features to consider when choosing a financial advisor?
A: Consider their experience, expertise in lottery winner finances, fee structure, services offered, and client service approach.

Q: How much does a financial advisor cost?
A: Fees vary, but can be a percentage of assets under management, hourly fees, or a flat fee for specific services.

Q: Why is it important to work with a financial advisor after winning the lottery?
A: Advisors provide expertise in managing large sums of money, minimizing taxes, and creating a financial plan to secure your long-term financial well-being.

Q: What questions should I ask a financial advisor before hiring them?
A: Ask about their experience, investment philosophy, fee structure, services offered, and how they work with lottery winners.

Q: How do financial advisors help lottery winners with tax planning?
A: Advisors help minimize tax liabilities through strategic planning and investment choices and work closely with CPAs.

Q: What is the difference between a financial advisor and a financial planner?
A: Financial advisors often provide a broad range of services, including investment management and financial planning. Financial planners focus specifically on creating financial plans.

Q: Can a financial advisor help with charitable giving after a lottery win?
A: Yes, advisors can help structure charitable donations and advise on the most tax-efficient giving strategies.

Q: How do I know if my financial advisor is a fiduciary?
A: Fiduciaries are legally obligated to act in your best interest. Always confirm that your advisor is a fiduciary and understands the unique issues facing lottery winners. This is standard in 2026.

Q: How can I protect my lottery winnings from fraud or scams?
A: Work with a reputable financial advisor who understands fraud prevention and take steps to maintain privacy. Always take time to carefully review any investment or offer, and be skeptical of any investment promise that seems too good to be true.

Q: What should I expect in the first meeting with a financial advisor?
A: In the first meeting, the advisor will likely gather information about your financial situation, goals, and risk tolerance, and discuss how they can help you manage your winnings.
