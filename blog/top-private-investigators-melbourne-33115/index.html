Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What types of investigations do private investigators in Melbourne undertake?

Melbourne private investigators handle a wide variety of cases, including infidelity investigations, background checks, surveillance, missing persons searches, fraud detection, asset tracing, and corporate investigations.

How do I choose the best private investigator in Melbourne?

Consider factors such as experience, specialization, reputation, client testimonials, and the range of services offered. Ensure the agency is licensed and has a proven track record. It is also good to have a consultation.

Are private investigators in Melbourne licensed?

Yes, all practicing private investigators in Melbourne must be licensed by the relevant authorities. Verify the license before engaging their services.

How much does it cost to hire a private investigator in Melbourne?

The cost varies depending on the type of investigation, time required, and resources used. Costs often range from hourly rates to flat fees, or customized packages. Get a quote.

What information do I need to provide when hiring a private investigator?

You'll need to provide as much detail as possible about the case, including the names of the subjects, any relevant addresses, dates, and background information about the parties involved.

What is the process of hiring a private investigator in Melbourne?

The process typically involves an initial consultation to discuss your needs, a signed contract outlining services and fees, and the investigation itself. You will then receive regular updates and a final report.

How long does an investigation typically take?

The duration varies depending on the complexity of the case. Some investigations may take a few days, while others can last weeks or months. The investigator will give you a timeline.

Is the information I provide to a private investigator confidential?

Yes, reputable private investigators maintain strict confidentiality. All information provided is kept secure and only used for the investigation purposes outlined in the contract.

Can private investigators in Melbourne conduct surveillance?

Yes, surveillance is a common service offered by private investigators in Melbourne. They use various techniques, including physical and electronic surveillance, to gather evidence.

What kind of evidence can a private investigator collect?

Private investigators can collect a wide range of evidence, including photographic and video evidence, documentation, witness statements, and public records, all within the legal framework.

What are the legal limitations on private investigators in Melbourne?

Private investigators must operate within the law. They cannot trespass, break into properties, or conduct illegal surveillance. All evidence must be obtained legally and ethically. They can't violate privacy laws.
