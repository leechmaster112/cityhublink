2. Zingerman's Delicatessen

Zingerman's Delicatessen in Ann Arbor is a legendary establishment. Known for its sandwiches, they offer a vast array of high-quality ingredients and flavors, making each sandwich an experience in itself.

  • Description: A renowned deli famous for its overstuffed sandwiches, Zingerman's is a true Michigan institution.
  • Pros: Wide selection of quality sandwiches and ingredients.
  • Cons: Can get very crowded; known for long wait times.
  • Who it's best for: Sandwich lovers and those looking for a classic deli experience.

4. Selden Standard

Selden Standard in Detroit is known for its delicious food, and its commitment to local sourcing enhances the dining experience. The atmosphere is casual, yet polished.

  • Description: Offers a diverse menu of contemporary American fare with a focus on local ingredients.

  • Who it's best for: Diners who appreciate great food and a lively atmosphere.
