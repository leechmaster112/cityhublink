Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • What should I do immediately after a car accident in Calgary?

    • After a car accident in Calgary, ensure everyone's safety by checking for injuries and calling emergency services if needed. Exchange information with the other driver, document the scene, and report the accident to the police. Seek medical attention and contact a car accident lawyer as soon as possible.

  • How much does a car accident lawyer in Calgary cost?

    • Most car accident lawyers in Calgary work on a contingency fee basis. This means they only get paid if they win your case, taking a percentage of the settlement or court award. The percentage varies but is typically around 25-40%.

  • What types of compensation can I claim after a car accident?

    • After a car accident in Alberta, you can claim compensation for various losses, including medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and other related expenses.

  • What is the statute of limitations for car accident claims in Alberta?

    • In Alberta, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims, including car accidents, is generally two years from the date of the accident. It is essential to file your claim within this timeframe to avoid losing your right to compensation.

  • Do I need a lawyer for a minor car accident?

    • While you can handle a minor car accident claim on your own, it's often beneficial to consult with a car accident lawyer. They can assess your situation, advise you on your rights, and help you navigate the complexities of dealing with insurance companies, even for seemingly minor incidents.

  • How can a car accident lawyer help me with my claim?

    • A car accident lawyer can investigate the accident, gather evidence, handle communication with insurance companies, negotiate settlements, and represent you in court if necessary. They can help maximize your compensation by ensuring all damages are considered.

  • What if the other driver was uninsured or underinsured?

    • If the other driver was uninsured or underinsured, you may still have options. Your own insurance policy may provide coverage for uninsured or underinsured motorists. A car accident lawyer can advise you on your coverage and help you pursue a claim.

  • How long will it take to resolve my car accident case?

    • The time it takes to resolve a car accident case varies depending on the complexity of the case, the severity of the injuries, and whether the case goes to court. Minor claims can often be resolved within a few months, while more complex cases may take a year or longer.

  • Where can I find the best car accident lawyers in Calgary?

    • You can find the best car accident lawyers in Calgary through online searches, referral services, or recommendations from friends and family. Check the lawyers' websites for experience and previous case results. This article offers several recommendations.

  • Is it worth it to hire a car accident lawyer?

    • Hiring a car accident lawyer is often worth it. They provide expertise and will negotiate on your behalf. They level the playing field when dealing with insurance companies. They often increase the amount of compensation you receive, making it well worth the fee they charge.
