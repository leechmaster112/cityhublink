FAQ: Best Luxury Exotic Car Rental in Los Angeles 2026

  1. What is the average cost to rent an exotic car in Los Angeles?

    The cost of renting an exotic car in Los Angeles in 2026 varies widely depending on the make and model, the rental duration, and the time of year. Expect to pay anywhere from a few hundred dollars per day to several thousand dollars for the most exclusive vehicles.

  2. What kind of insurance is required to rent a luxury car?

    Most luxury car rental companies in Los Angeles require renters to have valid auto insurance. They may also offer their insurance coverage, which is usually included in the rental agreement. Be sure to understand the coverage and the deductible amounts before renting.

  3. What models of cars are typically available for rent?

    The models available change depending on the rental company and seasonality; it is common to find vehicles from brands like Ferrari, Lamborghini, Rolls Royce, Bentley, and Porsche. Some rental companies may offer more niche exotic models as well like the MCLaren.

  4. How far in advance should I book my luxury car rental?

    It's always a good idea to book your luxury car rental as far in advance as possible, especially during peak seasons or for specific models. Popular vehicles can sell out quickly.

  5. Are there any age restrictions for renting an exotic car?

    Yes, many rental companies have age restrictions, usually requiring renters to be at least 25 years old, though it may vary. Some companies may not rent to drivers under 30. Check the specific rental company's policies.

  6. What are the mileage limitations on exotic car rentals?

    Rental agreements often include mileage limitations. Be sure to understand your mileage allowance and the per-mile overage fee to avoid extra charges.

  7. What are the payment options?

    Most luxury car rental companies in Los Angeles accept major credit cards. Some might also accept debit cards or other forms of payment. Always confirm available payment methods when making your reservation.

  8. Can I rent an exotic car for a special occasion, like a wedding?

    Yes! Exotic car rentals are very popular for weddings, anniversaries, and other special events. Be sure to explicitly communicate your needs to the rental company. Some offer specialized packages for events.

  9. Where in Los Angeles can I pick up or have the car delivered?

    Rental companies often offer delivery to airports, hotels, and residences throughout the Los Angeles area. Delivery policies should be discussed in advance to ensure the best possible experience. Make sure to choose a trusted company, such as 777 Exotics.

  10. How do I ensure the car is in good condition?

    Always inspect the car thoroughly upon pickup and note any existing damage on the rental agreement. Take photos or videos for your records. Notify the rental company immediately about any items not meeting expectations or safety concerns.

  11. Do I need any special license to drive an exotic car?

    You only need a regular valid driver's license. With that said, luxury car rental companies might have additional requirements, such as a driving record check.
