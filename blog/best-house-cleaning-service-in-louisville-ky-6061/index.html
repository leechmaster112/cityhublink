Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of house cleaning in Louisville, KY?

The average cost can vary greatly depending on factors such as the size of your home, the frequency of cleaning, and the type of cleaning services. Generally, prices in Louisville range from $75 to $250+ per cleaning, but it is best to get a quote from several different companies to get a more accurate idea.

Are house cleaning services in Louisville, KY, insured?

Reputable house cleaning companies in Louisville are typically insured. Insurance protects both the homeowner and the cleaning service in case of any damage or accidents during the cleaning process. Always ask about insurance coverage when considering a cleaning service.

How often should I have my house cleaned by a professional in Louisville?

The frequency of professional house cleaning depends on your lifestyle and preferences. Many people choose weekly or bi-weekly service to maintain a consistently clean home. Others may opt for monthly cleaning, or a one-time deep clean. Scrub Lou offers flexible scheduling options to fit individual needs.

What services are typically included in a standard house cleaning in Louisville?

A standard house cleaning generally includes dusting, vacuuming, mopping floors, cleaning bathrooms (toilets, showers, sinks), wiping kitchen surfaces, and emptying trash. Deep cleaning services may include additional tasks like cleaning inside appliances, scrubbing baseboards, and cleaning windows.

How do I prepare my home for a house cleaning service?

To help the cleaning process, it's helpful to declutter surfaces, pick up any large items off the floor, and secure any valuables. This allows the cleaning professionals to work more efficiently and effectively. For initial cleans, especially, it can be beneficial to give the cleaners clear access to all areas you want cleaned.

What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning service in Louisville, KY?

Most reputable cleaning services, like Scrub Lou, offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the service, contact the company immediately. Many will return to re-clean areas or provide a refund or discount on future services.

Does a house cleaning service in Louisville provide their own cleaning supplies?

Yes, most professional cleaning services in Louisville provide their own cleaning supplies and equipment. This includes cleaning solutions, vacuum cleaners, mops, and other tools. Clients should confirm this before booking a service, but it's generally standard practice.

How do I choose the right house cleaning service in Louisville?

Consider the company's reputation, services offered, pricing, and customer reviews. Check for insurance and bonding. Make sure the service is willing to address your specific needs and preferences. Look closely at the online presence and reviews of any service.

What are the benefits of hiring a house cleaning service in Louisville?

Hiring a house cleaning service saves you time and energy, allowing you to focus on other priorities. A clean home also promotes a healthier living environment. Professional cleaners have expertise and can provide a more thorough clean than many homeowners can achieve. Plus, there's a certain feeling of ease that comes from a clean home!

What’s the difference between a standard and deep cleaning service?

A standard cleaning service focuses on maintaining the cleanliness of your home with routine tasks, such as dusting and mopping. Deep cleaning services are comprehensive and include detailed cleaning of baseboards, inside appliances, and other areas that are not usually addressed during standard cleanings.
