FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about dental implants in Langley:

  1. What are dental implants? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically made of titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth.
  2. Are dental implants right for me? The best way to determine if you're a good candidate is to consult with a qualified dentist who can assess your oral health and discuss your goals.
  3. How much do dental implants cost in Langley? The cost of dental implants varies depending on several factors, including the type of implant, the complexity of the procedure, and the dentist's fees. It's best to get a personalized quote from a dental professional.
  4. What is the dental implant procedure like? The procedure typically involves several steps, including consultation, implant placement, healing, and restoration (attaching the artificial tooth).
  5. How long does it take to get dental implants? The entire process can take several months, as the implant needs time to fuse with the jawbone. This process is called osseointegration.
  6. What are the benefits of dental implants? Dental implants offer numerous benefits, including improved chewing ability, enhanced aesthetics, and long-term oral health. Implants help to prevent bone loss, helping maintain the structure of your face.
  7. Are dental implants painful? The implant procedure itself is typically performed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn't feel pain during the surgery. After the surgery, you may experience some minor discomfort, which can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  8. How do I choose the best dentist for dental implants in Langley? Look for a dentist with experience in implant dentistry, a strong reputation, and access to advanced technology, and who utilizes technologies like 3D imaging. Read patient reviews and schedule a consultation to discuss your specific needs.
  9. What is the success rate of dental implants? Dental implants have a very high success rate, often exceeding 95% over the long term.
  10. What kind of aftercare is required for dental implants? Proper oral hygiene, including regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups, is essential for the long-term success of dental implants.
