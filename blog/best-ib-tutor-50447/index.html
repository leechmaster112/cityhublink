Frequently Asked Questions About IB Tutors

What is the best way to find a good IB tutor?

The best way to find a good IB tutor is to look for platforms specializing in IB support, like Top IB Tutors, or to thoroughly research individual tutors. Look for verified experience with the IB curriculum, strong subject matter expertise, positive testimonials, and a personalized approach to teaching.

How much does an IB tutor typically cost in 2026?

The cost of an IB tutor in 2026 can vary significantly, ranging from $30 to over $150 per hour. Specialized platforms like Top IB Tutors may be at the higher end due to the expertise of their tutors, while general tutoring marketplaces might offer more budget-friendly options. Factors influencing price include tutor experience, subject complexity, and session length.

Is it worth hiring an IB tutor for the Extended Essay?

Yes, it can be highly worth it. The Extended Essay (EE) is a significant component of the IB Diploma Programme, requiring extensive research, critical analysis, and writing skills. A specialized IB tutor can guide students through the process, including topic selection, research methodology, structuring the essay, and academic referencing, greatly improving the quality of the final submission.

When should I start looking for an IB tutor?

It’s best to start looking for an IB tutor early in the academic year or even before the IB program begins. This allows ample time for assessment, planning, and finding the right tutor. For specific projects like the Extended Essay or Internal Assessments, starting at least a few months in advance is recommended.

What qualifications should I look for in an IB tutor?

Ideal qualifications include experience teaching or tutoring the IB curriculum, a strong academic background in the subject they teach, and an understanding of IB assessment criteria. Many top IB tutors are former IB examiners or teachers. Look for evidence of successful student outcomes and a pedagogical approach that suits your learning style.

Can an online IB tutor be as effective as an in-person tutor?

Absolutely. Modern online tutoring platforms offer interactive whiteboards, screen sharing, file sharing, and video conferencing, creating a dynamic and engaging learning environment. For many students, the convenience and access to a wider pool of specialist tutors make online IB tutoring highly effective.

What subjects are most commonly tutored for IB?

The most commonly tutored IB subjects often include Mathematics (Analysis & Approaches, Applications & Interpretation), Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English A (Language & Literature, Literature), History, Economics, and Psychology. Support for Theory of Knowledge (TOK) and the Extended Essay (EE) is also highly sought after.

How can a tutor help with IB Internal Assessments (IAs)?

An IB tutor can provide crucial guidance on the Internal Assessments (IAs) by helping students understand the assessment criteria, refine their research questions or experimental designs, plan their investigations, develop their analytical skills, and interpret their findings effectively. They can also assist with the structure and presentation of the IA report.
