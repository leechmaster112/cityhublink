Q6: What are the risks of driving after putting in the wrong fuel?

Driving after putting in the wrong fuel can lead to severe engine damage. Symptoms might include engine sputtering, loss of power, rough idling, or complete engine failure. Prolonged driving can cause irreversible damage to the fuel pump, injectors, and catalytic converter, leading to extremely costly repairs. It is always recommended to turn off the engine immediately and call for professional assistance.

Q8: What is involved in a mobile wrong fuel drain and flush service?

A mobile wrong fuel drain and flush service involves a technician coming to your location. They then typically flush the fuel lines and may clean or replace the fuel filter to remove any residual contaminants. Q10: Is it better to get a tow or have fuel rescued on-site?

For wrong fuel incidents, on-site rescue is usually the preferred and more cost-effective option. Professional wrong fuel rescue services are designed to be performed roadside.
