Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q: How long does it take to set up a bounce house?

A: Typically, our team can set up most standard bounce houses within 30-60 minutes upon arrival. Combo units or larger inflatables may take a bit longer, but we always work efficiently to get everything ready for your guests.

Q: Are your bounce houses safe for all ages?

A: While our inflatables are designed for fun, they do have weight limits and age recommendations for safety. We provide clear guidelines with each rental and train our setup team on safety procedures. Children under a certain age often require adult supervision near the bounce house.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: We accept cash, all major credit cards, and debit cards. A deposit may be required at the time of booking, with the remaining balance due upon setup. Please call us at (513) 999-5867 for specific payment details based on your rental duration.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?

A: We understand that plans can change. If you need to cancel or reschedule your rental, please notify us as soon as possible. We require a minimum 48 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling to receive a full refund or rebooking credit, subject to availability.

Q: Do you offer delivery outside of Cincinnati?

A: Absolutely! While we primarily serve the Cincinnati area, we are often able to accommodate rentals in surrounding communities. Please contact us at (513) 999-5867 or check our website at https://cincinnatibounce.com/ for specific delivery boundaries and any potential fees for distant locations.
