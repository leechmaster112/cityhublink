K-9 Rooster Dog Park

K-9 Rooster Dog Park is a secure off-leash area in northwest Wichita honoring fallen police K-9 Officer Rooster, with separate grassy sections for small and large dogs. Amenities include shaded shelters, benches, drinking fountains for dogs and humans, a dog wash station, waste stations, and newly planted trees following a recent remodel. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.
