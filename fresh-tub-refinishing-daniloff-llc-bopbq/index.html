Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly does bathtub refinishing involve?

We begin by thoroughly cleaning and preparing the surface, removing any loose paint or debris. Then, we apply specialized refinishing compounds that bond with the surface, followed by a durable clear coat for protection. The result is a seamless, waterproof finish that looks brand new.

Q2: How long does a typical refinishing project take?

Most refinishing projects can be completed in just one day, depending on the size and complexity of the job. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home and schedule.

Q3: Is the refinishing process safe for my family?

Absolutely. Our processes use only safe, low-VOC materials that are free from harsh chemicals and solvents. We take precautions to ensure a clean work environment during the refinishing process.

Q4: How long does the finished refinishing job last?

With proper care, a professionally refinished surface can last anywhere from 5 to 10 years or even longer. It effectively protects against future damage and maintains its beautiful appearance.

Q5: Can you refinish surfaces other than bathtubs?

Yes, our expertise includes refinishing sinks, shower pans, toilets, and various other bathroom surfaces. We can discuss your specific needs and determine if refinishing is the right solution for you.
