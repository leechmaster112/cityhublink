Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Hamden location?

We provide a comprehensive range of treatments including sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation, and vein surgery when necessary. Our Hamden center offers both medical and cosmetic vein care options.

Q: How long is the recovery period after vein treatment at your facility?

Recovery varies depending on the procedure performed. Most minimally invasive treatments allow patients to return to normal activities within a few days, with full recovery typically completed within two weeks.

Q: Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at your Hamden center?

While referrals are not required, we welcome patients who have questions or concerns. Our team can provide guidance on whether a vein evaluation is appropriate for their specific situation.

Q: What conditions do you treat at your Whitney Avenue location?

Our Hamden center specializes in treating conditions such as varicose veins, spider veins, chronic venous insufficiency, venous pain, and other venous disorders affecting the legs.

Q: How often should I schedule follow-up appointments after completing treatment?

We recommend follow-up visits within 4-6 weeks after treatment to monitor healing and address any concerns. Our team will develop a personalized care plan based on individual needs.
