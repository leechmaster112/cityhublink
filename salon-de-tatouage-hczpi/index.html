FAQs About Salon De Tatouage

Q: How do I book an appointment at Salon De Tatouage?

You can book an appointment by visiting our website at https://nolatattoos.com/ or by calling us directly at (504) 502-0900. We recommend booking in advance to secure your desired time slot.

Q: What styles of tattoos do you offer?

At Salon De Tatouage, we specialize in a variety of tattoo styles including traditional, neo-traditional, realism, geometric, and fine-line. Our artists can create custom designs tailored to your preferences.

Q: Do you offer consultations before tattooing?

Yes, we offer complimentary consultations where you can discuss your ideas, choose an artist, and finalize your design. This is a great opportunity to ask any questions you may have.

Q: How long does a typical tattoo session last?

The duration of a tattoo session depends on the size and complexity of the design. Your artist will provide an estimate during your consultation. Larger or more detailed tattoos may require multiple sessions.

Q: Do you provide aftercare instructions?

Absolutely! We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure your tattoo heals properly. Following these guidelines is essential for maintaining the vibrancy and longevity of your tattoo.
