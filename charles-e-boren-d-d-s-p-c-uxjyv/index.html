Boren combines advanced diagnostic and treatment technologies with personalized patient care to deliver exceptional results. The practice specializes in a comprehensive range of services including cosmetic dentistry, dental sleep medicine, and migraine prevention therapy, making it a unique destination for patients seeking transformative dental care and overall wellness solutions under one roof. Dr. Boren's expertise in cosmetic dentistry encompasses state-of-the-art treatments such as Lumineers (ultra-thin porcelain veneers), all-porcelain crowns, ClearCorrect clear aligners, professional teeth whitening with Opalescence, and custom smile makeovers. Beyond cosmetic enhancements, Dr. Boren offers specialized dental sleep medicine services for patients suffering from sleep apnea and snoring, utilizing custom oral appliance therapy developed in collaboration with sleep specialists to improve sleep quality and overall health. What truly distinguishes Charles E. Boren D.D.S., P.C. is the practice's patient-centered approach and commitment to comprehensive wellness care. With over three decades of experience in migraine prevention, Dr. Boren provides complimentary evaluations and personalized treatment solutions using the innovative NTI-tss device to help patients find relief from chronic migraine pain. The practice welcomes new patients, walk-ins, and emergency dental cases, offering flexible scheduling and sedation dentistry options to ensure a comfortable, stress-free experience.
