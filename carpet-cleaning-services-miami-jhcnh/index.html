Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of carpets do you clean?

We clean a wide range of carpet types, including but not limited to Berber, nylon, polyester, olefin, and wool. Our techniques and equipment are adapted to handle different carpet materials effectively and safely.

Q2: How long does a typical carpet cleaning job take?

The duration depends on the size of the area and the level of soiling. Generally, a standard residential carpet cleaning can take anywhere from 1 to 3 hours. We will provide a more accurate estimate after assessing your carpets.

Q3: Will the cleaning leave my carpets wet?

Modern cleaning methods, like hot water extraction (steam cleaning), do involve saturating the carpet. However, our process includes effective drying techniques to minimize moisture and ensure your carpets are ready for use as quickly as possible, typically within 4-8 hours.

Q4: Do you offer stain removal services?

Absolutely. We specialize in removing common stains such as spills, ink, wine, coffee, pet accidents, and more. Prompt attention significantly increases the chances of successful stain removal.

Q5: Are your cleaning products safe for homes with children or pets?

We use professional-grade cleaning solutions that are effective yet safe. While we recommend vacuating the room during the cleaning process for optimal results, our products are designed to minimize wait time and are safe once fully dried. We can discuss specific product choices if you have concerns.
