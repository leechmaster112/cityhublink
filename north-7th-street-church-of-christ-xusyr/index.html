North 7th Street Church of Christ

We warmly invite you to join us in worship, fellowship, and spiritual growth at the North 7th Street Church of Christ, a welcoming Christian congregation in the heart of Harlingen. Our hearts and doors are open to all who seek to deepen their faith, grow closer to God, and study the Bible. As a vibrant community dedicated to New Testament restoration, we emulate the purity, unity, and devotion of the early church. Our mission is to foster a sacred space for worship services, Bible study, and Christian fellowship, where believers can connect meaningfully with God’s Word and one another. Join us for Sunday worship, prayer, and community outreach as we strive to live out the teachings of Jesus Christ and build a faithful, loving church family committed to spiritual growth and service.

Location

Welcome to the North 7th Street Church of Christ, a place where faith, fellowship, and community converge right here in Harlingen. Located at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550, we are dedicated to providing a welcoming environment for individuals seeking spiritual growth, meaningful worship, and authentic Christian fellowship.

At the North 7th Street Church of Christ, our mission is rooted in the core principles of our faith. We believe in the power of God's Word to transform lives and guide individuals on their journey of faith. Our services are designed to be accessible, engaging, and spiritually enriching for people of all ages and backgrounds.

Worship Services

Our primary focus is on worship, where we gather each week to praise and honor God. Services are held regularly, allowing our congregation and visitors to come together in song, prayer, and the study of the Bible. The atmosphere is warm and inviting, encouraging participation from everyone who attends.

During our services, you can expect traditional elements of worship, including scripture readings, prayer, and teaching based on biblical principles. We strive to create a space where the Word of God is central, offering guidance and inspiration to those seeking a deeper connection with Him.

Fellowship and Community

Beyond the formal services, the North 7th Street Church of Christ emphasizes fellowship among its members. We believe that building relationships with fellow believers is an essential part of the Christian experience. Regular fellowship opportunities allow us to support one another, share our faith, and grow together in our understanding of God's Word.

Our fellowship extends to various activities and events held throughout the week, providing chances for members to connect, serve, and engage with the church community. These gatherings are open to everyone, fostering a sense of belonging and unity among those who share our faith.

Location and Accessibility

Our church building at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550 is easily accessible by car, with ample parking available for visitors and members. The facility is designed to accommodate a comfortable gathering, providing a peaceful and focused environment for worship and study.

We encourage everyone in the Harlingen area to visit us and experience the welcoming atmosphere firsthand. Whether you are a long-time member or a curious visitor, you are invited to join us as we gather to worship and serve God.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What days and times are services held?

Answer: Our regular worship services are held every Sunday morning at 10:30 AM. We invite you to join us for a time of fellowship, worship, and spiritual growth.

2. How can I get to the church?

Answer: The church is located at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550. Detailed driving directions and a map can be found on our website at https://harlingenchurch.com/.

3. Are there programs for children during services?

Answer: Yes, we offer children's programs during our Sunday worship services to accommodate families with young ones. Our children's ministry focuses on teaching biblical principles in a fun and engaging way.

4. What should I wear to church?

Answer: We encourage comfortable attire for our services. There is no specific dress code, so visitors can wear whatever makes them feel most at ease while worshipping with us.

5. How can I learn more about joining the congregation?

Answer: You can learn more about becoming a part of the North 7th Street Church of Christ by visiting our website at https://harlingenchurch.com/ or contacting us at (956) 320-7755. We offer opportunities for visitors to connect with our members and learn more about our beliefs and practices.
