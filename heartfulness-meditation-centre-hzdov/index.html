Heartfulness Meditation Centre

Heartfulness Meditation Gurugram offers free meditation training for everyone. Visitors are invited to experience guided Heartfulness meditation sessions with a certified trainer at no cost. We also hold Sunday group meditations at convenient morning hours at our Shri Ram Chandra Mission centre, Salapur Road, Sector 21E, Palam Farms, Gurugram. As part of the global Heartfulness movement, affiliated with SRCM, HFI, FBH, and Sahaj Marg, our Gurugram centre is connected to Kanha Shanti Vanam, Hyderabad, the world headquarters. Heartfulness meditation is offered in 160+ countries, and all are welcome to begin their journey of peace and inner balance here in Gurugram.
