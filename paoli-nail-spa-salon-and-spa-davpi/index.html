Our Services at Paoli Nail Spa

We specialize in a range of nail care services and basic spa treatments designed for our clients at Paoli Shopping Center.

Nail Services:

Manicures: Experience our signature manicure or choose from express options. We offer classic polish applications, French manicures, and meticulous shaping and filing. Soak-offs are available for removing previous polish. We use high-quality polishes and base/top coats.

Pedicures: Indulge your feet with our relaxing pedicure services. Options include basic shaping and polish, deluxe with soak and file, and specialized treatments like paraffin wraps for soft, smooth skin.

Nail Enhancements: Elevate your natural nails with professional enhancements. We offer nail tips for length and strength, as well as classic and hybrid acrylic nail systems, sculpted gels, and dip powder applications. Regular maintenance is key to keeping your enhancements looking perfect.

Spa Services:

Facials: Pamper your skin with our basic facial treatments. These include a consultation, cleanse, steam, treatment application (like masks), massage, and moisturizer, tailored to address your skin's specific needs.

Waxing: Achieve smooth skin with our professional waxing services for the face and body (areas like legs, arms, back, etc.). We use high-quality wax in a safe and efficient manner to minimize discomfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you offer gift certificates or gift cards?

A1: Yes, we do offer gift certificates and gift cards for various service packages and individual treatments. They are perfect for clients or as thoughtful gifts for friends and family. You can inquire about them at our salon or over the phone.

Q2: How long does a standard manicure or pedicure take?

A2: A standard manicure typically takes around 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the chosen service and if nail polish removal is needed. A standard pedicure usually takes about 45 minutes to an hour. Deluxe or specialized treatments will take longer.

Q3: Can I schedule an appointment online?

A3: While we primarily manage appointments over the phone, our website may provide contact information or links to scheduling platforms. You can also call us directly at (610) 407-0653 to book your appointment. We recommend calling ahead, especially for specific services or during busy times.

Q4: What brands of nail polish and products do you use?

A4: We utilize high-quality products, often including well-known brands like OPI, CND Shellac, Deborah L. Needleman, and others, depending on the specific service and product line chosen. We focus on using products that deliver great results and are beneficial for nail health.

Q5: Are there any special offers or package deals available?

A5: We periodically offer specials and package deals on various services. You can check our website or call us at (610) 407-0653 to inquire about current promotions and package options available at our Paoli Nail Spa.
