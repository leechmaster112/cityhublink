Victor, a Cal State East Bay Computer Science graduate and former member of the multi-platinum band BOSTON, brings a unique blend of technical expertise and creative marketing insight, having grown the agency from its first project for a Saratoga day spa into a trusted partner for local, regional, and national clients, delivering standout results like a 200% organic traffic surge for Danville Music and a $212,000 Google Ads revenue boost with a 10x return on ad spend. With a concierge-style approach, a team of seasoned experts, and a commitment to transparency and tailored strategies, Boomcycle empowers businesses to dominate search rankings, enhance their digital presence, and achieve lasting success.
