Our Inspection Services

Our standard home inspection typically includes a detailed examination of the following key areas:

Structural Components: We carefully assess the foundation, walls, ceilings, floors, and overall structural integrity of the home.

Roofing System: Our inspectors evaluate the roof's condition, including shingles, flashing, gutters, and downspouts.

Exterior Features: We inspect the siding, decks, patios, fencing, and landscaping surrounding the property.

Interior Systems: This includes checking walls, ceilings, floors, stairs, and interior fixtures for any signs of damage or wear.

Plumbing Systems: We examine both the water supply lines and the drainage systems, including the water heater and sump pump.

Electrical Systems: Our team reviews the electrical panel, wiring, outlets, and circuit breakers to ensure safety and compliance.

Refrigeration and HVAC: We assess the functionality and condition of the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.

Fireplace and Wood-Burning Stoves: If applicable, we inspect the chimney, flue, and fireplace structure.

Insulation and Ventilation: We evaluate the attic insulation and ventilation systems for energy efficiency.

Basement and Foundation: We check for signs of moisture, cracks, and other potential issues.

Appliances: We perform a functional check of major appliances like the refrigerator, oven, dishwasher, and disposal.

2. How long does a typical home inspection take?

A standard home inspection usually takes between 2.5 to 3.5 hours to complete thoroughly. The duration can vary slightly depending on the size and age of the property.

3. When should I schedule a home inspection?

Typically, a home inspection is scheduled after the purchase agreement is signed and the home has been made accessible to our inspector. It's best to schedule it before proceeding with any demolition or renovation work.
