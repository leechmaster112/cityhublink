Location

Kildare Road Medical Centre, located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, NSW, 2148, has been a cornerstone of healthcare in the Blacktown community since its doors opened in April 2010. Following a comprehensive refurbishment of the historic building, the centre was established with a commitment to providing high-quality, patient-focused medical services within a comfortable and modern environment.

Our dedicated team of experienced medical practitioners is available to meet the diverse health needs of our patients residing in and around Blacktown. We pride ourselves on delivering comprehensive primary healthcare, focusing on preventative medicine, accurate diagnosis, and effective treatment plans tailored to individual requirements.

Our Services

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, we offer a wide range of general medical services designed for adults and children. Our consultations cover everything from routine check-ups and minor ailments to the management of chronic conditions and health promotion advice.

We provide:

• General Medical Consultations: Comprehensive health assessments and ongoing medical care.

• Minor Ailment Management: Treatment for common illnesses like colds, flu, sore throats, and skin infections.

• Chronic Disease Management: Ongoing care for conditions such as asthma, diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease.

• Health Screenings and Preventative Care: Guidance on healthy lifestyle choices, vaccination programs, and health risk assessments.

• Minor Procedures: We can perform simple procedures like wound care, suturing, and skin lesion removal (requiring prior arrangement).

• Referrals and Coordinations: Appropriate referrals to specialists or diagnostic services when required.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of clinical practice and patient confidentiality. Our friendly and professional staff ensure a welcoming atmosphere, making your visit comfortable and stress-free.

Located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, our centre is easily accessible and conveniently situated to serve the local community. We understand the importance of accessible healthcare and strive to provide prompt and efficient services.

For more information about our services or to schedule an appointment, please visit our website or call our dedicated line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of medical services do you offer at Kildare Road Medical Centre?

We provide comprehensive primary healthcare services for patients living in Blacktown. This includes general health check-ups, management of acute and chronic illnesses, preventative health advice, minor procedures, and referrals to other healthcare professionals as needed.

Q2: How do I book an appointment at Kildare Road Medical Centre?

Appointments can be booked by calling our office directly at (02) 8822 3000. We recommend booking in advance to ensure availability, especially for regular health assessments or consultations for specific concerns.

Q3: Do you offer after-hours medical care?

While we primarily operate during standard business hours, we can provide information on local after-hours medical services available to residents of Blacktown. Our reception staff can assist with this information during our opening hours.

Q4: Can I request a specific doctor during my appointment?

While we strive to accommodate patient preferences where possible, appointments are generally allocated based on doctor availability and the nature of the consultation requested. Our team will do their best to connect you with a doctor they believe is most suitable for your needs.

Q5: What is the process for managing chronic conditions like diabetes or asthma?

Our approach to managing chronic conditions involves regular monitoring, medication management (if applicable), lifestyle advice, and education for both the patient and their family. We work collaboratively with patients to develop personalised care plans aimed at optimising health outcomes and quality of life, tailored specifically for individuals living in Blacktown.
