Jackson Bark

Jackson Bark is the largest fully enclosed dog park on Chicago's south side, built on a repurposed tennis-court surface in Jackson Park with over 50 agility obstacles, cooling pools, and mist stations. Community-maintained with free parking, it offers water stations, a Little Free Library, and separate areas for dogs with mismatched temperaments. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Location

Jackson Bark stands as a singular destination on Chicago's south side, offering an unparalleled indoor haven for canine adventurers.

This expansive, fully enclosed dog park, ingeniously built on a repurposed transport hub at 6000 S DuSable Lake Shore Dr, Chicago, IL 60637, provides a secure and stimulating environment for dogs to socialize and play, regardless of the weather outside.

Forget the usual limits of urban dog spaces; Jackson Bark is dedicated to providing a vast, climate-controlled playground where your furry companions can truly stretch their legs and engage in boundless fun.

Discover the difference a dedicated, indoor space makes for your dog's well-being and happiness.
