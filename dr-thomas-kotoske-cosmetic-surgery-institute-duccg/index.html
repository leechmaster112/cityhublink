Our Services: Tailored Aesthetic Solutions

Body by Kotoske offers a comprehensive range of cosmetic surgery and non-surgical aesthetic services, all meticulously planned and performed within our state-of-the-art facility at 19636 N 27th Ave #101. We pride ourselves on providing a full spectrum of options to address diverse body and facial concerns:

Surgical Procedures:

Body Contouring: Say goodbye to stubborn areas of fat and excess skin. We offer advanced surgical liposuction techniques, including SmartLipo™, designed to sculpt and redefine various body parts such as the abdomen, thighs, buttocks, back, and arms, yielding smoother, more contoured results specific to your body type.

Facelift Surgery (Rhytidoplasty): Reveal a more youthful facial appearance by addressing sagging skin, jowls, and neck bands. Our surgical facelift techniques focus on lifting and tightening the facial tissues for a refreshed look.

Breast Surgery: Whether you seek breast augmentation to enhance your figure, breast reduction for comfort and proportion, or a mastopexy (breast lift) to restore firmness, our experienced team provides personalized surgical solutions tailored to your anatomy and aesthetic desires.

Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck): Restore your pre-pregnancy body or address significant weight loss changes with a comprehensive tummy tuck. This procedure removes excess skin and fat from the abdomen and tightens the underlying muscles for a flatter, more toned profile.

Non-Surgical Procedures:

Injectables: Achieve facial rejuvenation with minimal downtime using Botox® and Dysport® for dynamic wrinkle reduction, and dermal fillers like Juvederm® and Restylane® to restore volume, enhance lips, and sculpt facial contours.

Laser Skin Treatments: Improve skin texture, tone, and appearance with our range of laser therapies, including laser skin resurfacing for wrinkles and scars, laser hair removal for long-term hair reduction, and vascular laser treatments for conditions like rosacea and spider veins.

Other Aesthetic Options: We may also offer other minimally invasive procedures like CoolSculpting® for non-invasive fat reduction, thread lifts for facial support, and skin tightening treatments, all selected for their efficacy and safety in our Phoenix practice.
