Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What areas does Laser Bar Barrie treat for laser hair removal?

A1: At our Barrie location, our laser hair removal services target common areas including the legs, arms, back, face, upper lip, chin, and bikini line. A consultation at 535 Bryne Dr will determine the best course of treatment for your specific hair growth patterns.

Q2: How many sessions of microneedling are typically needed at Laser Bar?

A2: The number of microneedling sessions required depends on your individual skin concerns and desired results. Generally, clients at our Barrie clinic see optimal outcomes after a series of 3-6 sessions, spaced several weeks apart.

Q3: Is IPL facial treatment at Laser Bar Barrie suitable for all skin tones?

A3: Our IPL technology at 535 Bryne Dr is suitable for a wide range of Fitzpatrick skin types, but results can vary. A patch test is recommended during your consultation to ensure suitability for your skin tone and specific concerns.

Q4: What is the downtime after laser hair removal at Laser Bar?

A4: Immediately following laser hair removal at our Barrie clinic, you may experience mild redness and warmth in the treated area, similar to a sunburn. This typically subsides within a few hours to a couple of days. Larger areas or more sensitive skin might experience slightly more noticeable side effects.

Q5: Can I combine laser hair removal with other treatments at Laser Bar?

A5: Yes, our team at 535 Bryne Dr can often combine laser hair removal with other services like microneedling or IPL facials, depending on your goals and skin condition. Discuss your desired treatment combination during your initial consultation.
