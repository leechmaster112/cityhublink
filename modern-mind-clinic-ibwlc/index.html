Location

Modern Mind Clinic is a mental health practice located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. We provide a modern approach to mental wellness, offering advanced treatments for individuals seeking relief from depression and other mental health conditions. Our clinic is designed to be a calm, welcoming space where patients can focus on their journey toward healing.

At our Southfield location, we specialize in two key, evidence-based treatments: Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy) and Spravato (esketamine) treatment. TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, specifically targeting areas involved in mood regulation. It is an FDA-cleared treatment for Major Depressive Disorder, particularly for patients who have not experienced satisfactory improvement from antidepressant medications. Spravato is a nasal spray medication, also FDA-approved for treatment-resistant depression, that works differently than traditional antidepressants. Both treatments represent a modern shift in psychiatric care, offering new hope for lasting relief.

In addition to these specialized interventions, Modern Mind Clinic offers comprehensive telehealth services. This allows patients to connect with our providers for consultations, therapy sessions, and follow-up care from the comfort and privacy of their own homes. Our integrated model ensures that whether you are visiting us in person at our Southfield clinic or connecting virtually, you receive consistent, high-quality, and personalized care.

The philosophy of Modern Mind Clinic is built on the principle of modern, patient-centered care. We understand that each individual's experience with mental health is unique. Our team of dedicated professionals takes the time to conduct thorough assessments and create tailored treatment plans that align with your specific needs and goals. We are committed to utilizing the latest advancements in mental health treatment to provide effective and compassionate care to our community here in Southfield.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What mental health services are offered at the Southfield clinic?

At our Southfield location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, we specialize in TMS Therapy and Spravato treatment for depression. We also provide telehealth services for therapy and psychiatric consultations, offering a modern and integrated approach to mental health care.

2. How do I know if TMS Therapy is right for me?

TMS Therapy is typically recommended for adults with Major Depressive Disorder who have not found sufficient relief from antidepressant medications. The best way to determine if TMS is a suitable option for you is to schedule a consultation with our clinical team at our Southfield clinic. We will conduct a comprehensive assessment to discuss your history and treatment goals.

3. Where do I go for my Spravato treatment sessions?

All Spravato treatments are administered in person at our clinic located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. This is a requirement as the treatment is supervised by our medical staff in a comfortable, private setting to ensure your safety and comfort throughout the session.

4. Can I use telehealth for all my appointments?

Telehealth is available for many of our services, including initial consultations, therapy sessions, and follow-up appointments. However, TMS Therapy and Spravato treatment require in-person visits to our Southfield clinic due to the nature of the procedures. Your treatment plan will outline which appointments can be conducted via telehealth.

5. How can I schedule an appointment at the Modern Mind Clinic?

To schedule an appointment at our Southfield location, you can call us directly at (248) 607-7190. You can also learn more about our services and submit a contact form through our website at https://www.modernmindclinic.com/. Our team will guide you through the initial steps.
