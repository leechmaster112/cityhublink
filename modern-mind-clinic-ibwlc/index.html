Location

Welcome to Modern Mind Clinic, a dedicated mental health practice located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. Our name reflects our commitment to providing contemporary, evidence-based psychiatric care. We believe in a modern approach to mental wellness, one that integrates advanced medical treatments with compassionate, personalized support. At our Southfield clinic, we focus on creating a serene and professional environment where individuals can take meaningful steps toward healing and improved mental health.

Modern Mind Clinic specializes in cutting-edge treatments for depression and other mental health conditions. Our core services include Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy) and Spravato (esketamine) treatment. TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, offering hope for individuals who have not found success with traditional antidepressant medications. Spravato treatment, an FDA-approved nasal spray, provides a rapid-acting option for treatment-resistant depression, administered under medical supervision right here in our Southfield office suite. For continued care and accessibility, we also offer comprehensive telehealth services, allowing patients to connect with their provider from the comfort of their own homes while maintaining a consistent treatment plan.

Our practice is built on the principle that every individual's journey is unique. The team at Modern Mind Clinic takes the time to understand each patient's specific needs and goals. From your initial consultation at our Southfield location, we work with you to develop a tailored treatment strategy. We are not a general practice; we are a specialized clinic focused on providing these modern interventions to the community we serve directly from 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750.
