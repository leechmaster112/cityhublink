Location

Modern Mind Clinic provides advanced mental health treatment from our professional facility at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. We specialize in cutting-edge, evidence-based interventions designed for individuals seeking transformative care. Our clinic is dedicated to offering innovative solutions that address complex mental health conditions with precision and compassion.

At our Southfield location, our core service is Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy). This non-invasive, FDA-cleared treatment is a cornerstone of our practice, offering new hope for those with Major Depressive Disorder who have not found success with traditional antidepressant medications. This FDA-approved medication, offered in conjunction with comprehensive support, is available for qualified individuals at our Southfield clinic. All Spravato sessions are conducted in our specially designed treatment rooms, ensuring a safe and monitored environment for this important therapy.

To complement our in-clinic services, we provide dedicated telehealth mental health support. This allows for continuous care and accessibility for our patients connected to the Southfield practice, facilitating therapy sessions and follow-up consultations from the comfort of home. Our integrated approach ensures that every patient at our Nine Mile Road location receives a personalized and cohesive treatment plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS Therapy and how is it administered at your Southfield clinic?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our Southfield location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, the treatment is delivered by our clinical team while you are seated comfortably in one of our private treatment rooms. It does not require anesthesia and you can drive yourself home immediately after each session.

2. Who is a candidate for Spravato treatment at your Southfield facility?

Spravato is FDA-approved for treatment-resistant depression. Candidates are those who have tried at least two antidepressant medications without sufficient relief. Eligibility is determined through a comprehensive assessment conducted by our medical providers at the Modern Mind Clinic in Southfield to ensure it is a safe and appropriate option.

3. Are all appointments conducted in-person at your Southfield address?

No. While our TMS and Spravato treatments are administered in-person at our clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075, we also offer telehealth services for therapy sessions and consultations. This provides flexibility and continuous care for our patients.

4. How do I begin treatment at Modern Mind Clinic in Southfield?

Starting treatment begins with a consultation. You can reach out to our Southfield office via phone at (248) 607-7190 or through our website to schedule an initial evaluation. This first step allows our team to understand your needs and determine the most effective treatment plan for you.

5. What measures are in place for patient safety at the Southfield location?

Patient safety is our utmost priority. Our Southfield clinic adheres to the highest medical standards. For Spravato treatment, patients are monitored by our clinical staff in a dedicated recovery room for the required time post-administration. All equipment is maintained and operated according to strict safety protocols.
