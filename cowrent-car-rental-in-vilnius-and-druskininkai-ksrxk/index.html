Cowrent - Car rental in Vilnius and Druskininkai

ADTRANSPORT in collaboration with Cowrent is a leading car and minibus rental company in Lithuania, offering comprehensive mobility solutions for both individual travelers and corporate clients. Established in 2010, the company has built a solid reputation for reliability, transparency, and customer-focused service, continuously adapting to the evolving needs of modern transportation. Operating across the entire country, ADTRANSPORT provides vehicle pickup and delivery in all major Lithuanian cities, including Vilnius and Druskininkai, ensuring customers enjoy maximum convenience wherever they are. Whether you need a compact city car, a comfortable business-class sedan, or a spacious minibus for group travel, the company’s diverse and well-maintained fleet is designed to meet every requirement. Each vehicle is regularly inspected and equipped with modern safety and comfort features, guaranteeing a smooth and secure driving experience. Through its collaboration with Cowrent, ADTRANSPORT integrates advanced digital solutions, allowing users to easily book, manage, and modify their reservations online. The booking platform emphasizes clarity and ease of use — customers can compare vehicles, choose flexible rental durations, and benefit from transparent pricing without hidden fees. Additional services such as free insurance, 24/7 technical assistance, and responsive customer support further enhance the rental experience. ADTRANSPORT and Cowrent share a commitment to sustainability and efficiency, offering newer, eco-friendly models like hybrid and electric vehicles for clients seeking greener travel options. Their mission is simple yet ambitious — to make transportation across Lithuania as easy, safe, and comfortable as possible. With years of experience, a trusted network, and a customer-first approach, ADTRANSPORT and Cowrent continue to set the standard for quality vehicle rental services in Lithuania.
