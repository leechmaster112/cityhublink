Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is a Precious Metal IRA?

A: A Precious Metal IRA is a type of self-directed Individual Retirement Account that allows you to invest your retirement funds in physical precious metals, such as gold, silver, platinum, or palladium, held in an approved depository. This diversifies your retirement portfolio beyond traditional stocks and bonds.

Q2: How do I set up a Precious Metal IRA at your Miami Beach location?

A: We offer personalized consultations at our office located at 1521 Alton Road #90. We can discuss your financial goals, explain the process, determine if a Precious Metal IRA is suitable for you, and guide you through the funding and setup procedures.

Q3: Can I roll over funds from my existing retirement account into a Precious Metal IRA?

A: Yes, we specialize in handling rollover and transfer services. Our team at 1521 Alton Road #90 can assist with transferring funds from other retirement accounts, such as 401(k)s or traditional IRAs, into your new Precious Metal IRA seamlessly.

Q4: Where are the precious metals stored?

A: Your precious metals investments held within your Precious Metal IRA are securely stored in IRS-approved depositories. We work exclusively with reputable facilities that adhere to strict security and insurance standards.

Q5: Do you offer ongoing management services for Precious Metal IRAs?

A: While we provide expert guidance and support, the ownership of your precious metals remains with you. We focus on education and the administrative aspects of the IRA, including custodial services. We do not actively trade your metals on your behalf unless specifically arranged as part of a separate advisory agreement.
