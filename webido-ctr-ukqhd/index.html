What is Click-Through Rate (CTR) Optimization?

CTR is the percentage of people who click on your ad after seeing it. A higher CTR generally signals that your ad is relevant and appealing to your target audience, which search engines like Google interpret as a positive signal. Webido CTR focuses on analyzing your current ad campaigns, identifying elements that may be deterring clicks (such as irrelevant keywords, weak ad copy, or unoptimized landing pages), and implementing strategic changes to increase the proportion of users who engage with your advertisements. Our goal is to drive more qualified traffic to your website or landing page, improving not just clicks, but ultimately contributing to better overall campaign performance and return on investment (ROI).
