Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy

Articulate Kids provides expert speech and language assessment and therapy for children from our two clinics: our main base in Petts Wood, Orpington (BR5 1NU), and our prestigious Harley Street clinic in Central London. We support families across Bexley, Dartford, Sidcup, Crayford, Eltham, Bromley, Beckenham, West Wickham and Shirley. Many also travel from Sevenoaks, Belgravia, Mayfair, Marylebone, Kensington, Chelsea, Fitzrovia, Bloomsbury and Regent’s Park. Our evidence-based, child-centred therapy helps with late talking, stammering, language delays and social communication. We work closely with parents to build confidence and clarity at home and school, whether you’re local or visiting from Central London.

Location

Welcome to Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy, located at Scotsdale Cl, Petts Wood, Orpington BR5 1NU. We are dedicated to providing expert assessment and therapy services for children within our local community.

Our team of qualified and experienced Speech and Language Therapists are passionate about supporting children's communication development. We understand that navigating challenges with speech, language, or communication can be a stressful time for both children and parents. Our goal is to provide compassionate, professional, and effective support tailored to the unique needs of each child we serve in Petts Wood and the surrounding Orpington area.

We offer comprehensive assessments to identify specific areas of difficulty. Based on the assessment findings, our therapists design individualised therapy programmes. These programmes are designed to be practical, fun, and engaging, focusing on skills crucial for your child's development and success in daily life. Our interventions address a wide range of needs, including:

Speech sound disorders (difficulty saying specific sounds clearly) Language delays and disorders (understanding and using spoken language) Stammering or other fluency disorders Pragmatic language difficulties (using language appropriately in social situations) Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) assessment and support Difficulty with literacy skills (reading and writing)

At our Petts Wood centre, we pride ourselves on creating a supportive and motivating environment where children feel comfortable to participate and make progress. We work closely with parents and caregivers, providing regular updates and practical strategies you can use at home to support your child's development.

If you believe your child could benefit from our speech and language therapy services, please contact us today. We are committed to helping children in our local community reach their full communication potential.

For more information or to book an initial consultation, please visit our website or call our dedicated line.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do you only serve clients from Petts Wood and Orpington?

A1: Yes, Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy at Scotsdale Cl, Petts Wood, Orpington BR5 1NU focuses exclusively on serving clients within this specific location.

Q2: How do I book an appointment for a speech and language assessment?

A2: You can book an appointment by calling our phone number, +44 20 3858 0765, or by contacting us through our website at https://articulatekids.co.uk/. We will arrange a convenient time for an initial assessment session.

Q3: What does a typical therapy session involve?

A3: Therapy sessions are tailored to the child's specific needs. They may include activities designed to improve speech sounds, language comprehension and expression, communication skills, or literacy. Sessions are typically held at our Petts Wood centre.

Q4: How long does the assessment process take?

A4: The initial assessment usually involves two sessions. The first session is an information gathering meeting with parents. The second session involves formal assessments with the child. We will discuss the results and recommendations following the assessment.

Q5: Can I have a consultation before starting therapy?

A5: Yes, we offer an initial consultation session. This is an opportunity for us to meet with you and your child, discuss concerns, explain our assessment and therapy process, and determine if our services are suitable for your child's needs in Petts Wood.
