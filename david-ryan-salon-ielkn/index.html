Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services does David Ryan Salon offer?

We specialize in luxury haircare and styling, including precision haircuts, coloring services, keratin treatments, deep conditioning masks, keratin smoothing, hair extensions, and specialized styling for all hair types.

Q2: Are there any special offers or packages available?

We periodically offer promotions and packages, especially for first-time clients or during seasonal events. You can inquire about current specials when booking your appointment or by contacting us directly.

Q3: How long does a typical hair appointment take?

Appointment times vary depending on the service requested. A standard haircut and styling typically takes around 60-90 minutes, while coloring and keratin treatments may require longer sessions, often scheduled over two visits.

Q4: Do you offer consultations before major services like coloring?

Absolutely! We believe in a thorough consultation process, especially for services like coloring. We discuss your desired outcome, undertones, and skin tone compatibility to ensure the best possible result.

Q5: What is the cancellation policy?

We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for any appointment cancellations or rescheduling to accommodate all our valued clients. Please call us at (817) 290-6557 to make changes.
