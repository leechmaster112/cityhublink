Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of eye examinations do you offer at your Mississauga location?

We provide comprehensive eye examinations that assess both vision and eye health. Our exams include tests for refractive errors (nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism, presbyopia) and screenings for conditions like glaucoma, cataracts, diabetic retinopathy, and macular degeneration.

Q2: Can you help me find the right pair of glasses for my prescription?

Absolutely! Our optical team at 6005 Erin Mills Pkwy #104, Mississauga will work with you to find frames and lenses that perfectly match your prescription. We offer a wide selection of frames and advanced lens options, including progressives, bifocals, and photochromic lenses.

Q3: Do you provide blue light filtering lenses?

Yes, we offer blue light filtering lenses designed to protect your eyes from harmful blue light emitted by digital devices. These lenses can be incorporated into single vision, bifocal, or progressive lenses.

Q4: How often should I get an eye examination?

The frequency of eye examinations depends on your age, overall health, and risk factors. Generally, adults between the ages of 18 and 60 should have an eye exam every 1-2 years, while those over 60 may need annual examinations. Children should have their first eye exam around age 6 and follow-up exams as recommended by their optometrist.

Q5: Can I schedule an appointment for a contact lens fitting?

Yes, we offer contact lens fittings and consultations at our Mississauga location. During a fitting, our optometrist will determine the best type of contact lenses for your eyes and lifestyle, ensuring optimal comfort and vision correction.
