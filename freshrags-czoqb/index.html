Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What types of printing do you offer?

A: We specialize in both screen printing (great for bulk orders and vibrant colors) and Digital Direct-to-Garment (DTG) printing (ideal for detailed designs, photos, and smaller quantities).

Q: How do I order custom t-shirts?

A: You can order online via our website https://www.freshrags.com/, where you can upload your design or request a quote. Alternatively, you can call us at (513) 732-9690, and our team will guide you through the process, answer your questions, and help you place your order.

Q: What is your minimum order quantity?

A: We strive to be flexible! While we can handle small orders, the minimum quantity depends on the printing method (screen vs. DTG) and the design complexity. Please contact us at (513) 732-9690 or use our website quote form for specific inquiries based on your needs.

Q: How long does it take to get my custom t-shirts?

A: Turnaround time varies based on the order size, complexity, and the time of your order. Generally, we offer options ranging from a few days to one week. Rush services are often available for urgent projects. Get specific estimates by contacting us!

Q: Do you help with design creation?

A: Yes! While we encourage customers to bring their own designs, our team is happy to offer design consultation and suggestions. We can also recommend resources or graphic designers if you need assistance creating your artwork from scratch.
