Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of sewer line problems do you repair?

We handle a variety of sewer line issues, including cracks, breaks, tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, drain field problems, slow drains, clogs, and backups.

Q2: How long does a typical sewer line repair take?

The duration depends on the severity of the problem and the chosen repair method. Minor repairs might take a few hours, while full pipe replacement could take one to two days. We will provide you with a specific timeline after inspecting your sewer line.

Q3: Do you offer emergency sewer repair services?

Yes, sewer line emergencies can cause significant damage. We understand the urgency and are prepared to send our team out promptly to address critical issues like sewer backups.

Q4: How do you determine if my sewer line needs repair or replacement?

We begin with a thorough inspection, often using our camera systems to locate the exact problem. Based on the inspection results, our experts will advise whether repair or replacement is the most effective and economical solution for your specific situation.

Q5: What should I do if I suspect a sewer line problem?

If you notice signs like slow drains, gurgling sounds, foul odors, or wet spots near your foundation, it's best to contact us immediately. Avoid using water fixtures excessively and do not attempt to flush items that could cause clogs. Our team can assess the situation and advise you on the next steps.
