Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of scans do you offer at your OKC location?

A1: At our 3209 NW Expressway facility in OKC, we primarily offer full body scans and heart scans. These advanced imaging services utilize sophisticated technology to provide detailed views of internal structures.

Q2: Are these scans diagnostic? Can they replace a doctor's visit?

A2: Our scans are designed for preventative health assessment and early detection. They are not diagnostic tools and cannot replace a consultation with your primary care physician. We strongly recommend discussing any findings with your doctor.

Q3: How long does a typical scan take at your OKC center?

A3: Scan times vary depending on the specific type of scan (full body vs. heart) and individual patient factors. Generally, a full body scan might take around 15-30 minutes, while a heart scan is typically quicker, often completed in 10-15 minutes.

Q4: Is any special preparation required before my scan?

A4: Preparation instructions may vary slightly depending on the specific scan. For most full body scans, we may ask you to fast for a few hours beforehand. For heart scans, specific preparation related to calcium scoring might be required. Our staff will provide detailed instructions when you schedule your appointment.

Q5: Who should consider getting a heart scan at your OKC facility?

A5: A heart scan is particularly beneficial for individuals at higher risk for cardiovascular disease. This includes people with a family history of heart disease, smokers, individuals with high blood pressure or cholesterol, and those with other risk factors. However, anyone interested in assessing their heart health can discuss this option with us at our 3209 NW Expressway location.
