Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a cutting-edge therapeutic technology used primarily for the treatment of major depressive disorder. Unlike traditional antidepressant medications, TMS targets specific areas of the brain identified as underactive through neuroimaging. This non-invasive procedure uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, potentially helping to restore balance and improve symptoms.

At our Raleigh location, we utilize this advanced technology to offer a treatment option for patients who may not have found sufficient relief with other approaches. Our team is focused on providing a comprehensive evaluation to determine if TMS is an appropriate treatment option for each individual patient.

The TMS procedure at our Raleigh facility is typically well-tolerated and does not require sedation. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is TMS therapy?

A: Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression and other conditions.

Q2: Is TMS therapy effective?

A: TMS has been cleared by the FDA for treating major depressive disorder and is supported by numerous clinical studies demonstrating its effectiveness for many patients.

Q3: Is TMS therapy safe?

A: TMS is generally well-tolerated with minimal side effects, which are typically mild and temporary. It does not require anesthesia or sedation.

Q4: How long does a typical TMS session last?

A: A standard TMS session typically lasts between 25 to 45 minutes. Most patients find they can return to their normal activities immediately after a session.

Q5: What conditions does TMS treat at your Raleigh location?

A: TMS of the Carolinas at our Raleigh location primarily offers TMS therapy for the treatment of major depressive disorder. We focus on providing this specific service to our Raleigh patients.
