Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Union center?

We provide a range of advanced, minimally invasive treatments for conditions like spider veins, varicose veins, chronic venous insufficiency, and others. Our treatments include sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and more, tailored to your specific condition.

2. Is the treatment at your Union location painful?

We utilize modern techniques and local anesthesia to ensure your comfort during procedures. While some mild discomfort or tenderness may be felt after treatment, our goal is to minimize pain and maximize your comfort throughout the process.

3. How long does a typical vein treatment procedure take at your Union clinic?

Most minimally invasive procedures performed at our Union center take between 30 minutes to a few hours, depending on the complexity and the specific treatment required. Your doctor will provide a more accurate estimate during your consultation.

4. What is the recovery time after vein treatment at your Union facility?

Recovery varies based on the procedure, but most patients can return to light activities within a day or two. We provide detailed post-operative care instructions to ensure a smooth recovery. You should avoid strenuous activities for a specified period as advised by your physician.

5. Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at your Union center?

While a referral is not always mandatory, we recommend discussing your symptoms with your primary care physician first. You can also schedule an initial consultation directly with us at our Union location to discuss your concerns and determine the best course of action.
