Landmark Tavern at Stone House

Nestled in 10 acres of meticulously lush gardens in the stunning Watchung Mountains, Stone House is a picturesque backdrop for your next celebration. Our two distinct event spaces are where luxury meets rustic contemporary. Classic tavern with a modern twist - think locally sourced ingredients, craft cocktails, bold flavors, and comforting favorites reimagined. The restaurant is equally welcoming and attractive with open kitchen views, large outdoor patio dining, a visually striking wine tower, and soul-warming hearth fireplace. Landmark Taverns is thrilled to bring you three locations: Logan Inn in New Hope, PA, Stone House in Warren, NJ, and Ryland Inn in Whitehouse Station, NJ.
