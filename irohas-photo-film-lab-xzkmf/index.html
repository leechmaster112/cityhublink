Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What film formats do you develop at your Haymarket location?

A1: We develop a wide range of 35mm film formats, including standard 35mm negatives and slides, medium format films (4x5, 645, 6x6, 6x7, APS, various 110/116 formats), and large format films. Please specify the film type when placing your order for the best results.

Q2: How long does film processing typically take at your Sydney CBD lab?

A2: Standard film processing usually takes 2-3 business days from the time we receive your films at our 398-402 Sussex St location. Rush services are often available for an additional fee, so please inquire when submitting your order.

Q3: Do you offer film scanning services for clients in the vicinity of your Haymarket studio?

A3: Yes, we offer professional film scanning services for 35mm, medium format, and large format films. This service digitises your negatives or slides, creating high-resolution digital files. You can request film scanning when you submit your film for development.

Q4: Can you print photographs for customers located near 398-402 Sussex St?

A4: Absolutely. We provide professional photo printing services using high-quality photographic papers and state-of-the-art printers. We offer various paper types, sizes, and finishes to suit different needs and aesthetic preferences. Prints can be ordered alongside your film processing.

Q5: How can I send my film to Irohas Photo Film Lab in Haymarket?

A5: You can send your film via standard mail or courier services. We provide pre-paid labels for courier pickups if you prefer. Alternatively, you can drop off your film at our location during business hours. Please ensure your order form includes all necessary details and contact information for tracking and delivery confirmation.
