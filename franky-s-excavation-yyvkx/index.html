Franky's Excavation

Franky's Excavation is a local, family-operated contractor that provides a wide range of excavation, demolition, and land clearing services to clients throughout Washington's Pierce and King Counties. As a community-focused business, they handle both residential and commercial projects in numerous local cities, including Tacoma, Puyallup, Federal Way, Lakewood, Parkland, and Spanaway. Their reputation is built on delivering professional, high-quality work to prepare properties for new construction, landscaping, or renovation projects. The company offers a comprehensive suite of services to manage all aspects of site preparation. Their primary expertise lies in excavation, which includes digging for new home foundations and basements, as well as trenching for utility lines like water and sewer pipes. For properties that need to be cleared, they provide thorough land clearing services, removing trees, thick brush, and stubborn stumps to create a clean slate. In addition to clearing and digging, Franky's Excavation is also equipped to handle demolition projects. Their team can safely tear down old structures and buildings, and they also specialize in concrete removal, breaking up and hauling away old driveways, patios, and foundations. Once the land is cleared, they perform expert grading and leveling to ensure the ground is perfectly flat and stable for whatever project comes next. They also offer services for laying gravel and loose fill, creating functional driveways and pathways.
