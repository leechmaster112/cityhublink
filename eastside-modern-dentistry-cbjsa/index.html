Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Eastside Modern Dentistry offer?

Eastside Modern Dentistry provides a wide range of dental services, including dental examinations, cleanings, cosmetic dentistry (such as teeth whitening and veneers), restorative procedures (like crowns, bridges, and dental implants), oral surgery, and orthodontic care (including Invisalign). We are committed to offering comprehensive dental care tailored to each patient's unique needs.

How can I schedule an appointment?

Scheduling an appointment with Eastside Modern Dentistry is easy. You can call our office at (425) 472-1044, or you can book online through our website at https://www.eastsidemoderndentistry.com/. We strive to accommodate your schedule and provide convenient appointment times.

Does Eastside Modern Dentistry treat dental emergencies?

Yes, we understand that dental emergencies can arise unexpectedly. While walk-in appointments are not guaranteed, we will do our best to assist you in a timely manner. You can contact our office at (425) 472-1044 during business hours, and our team will guide you on the best course of action.

Does Eastside Modern Dentistry accept insurance?

We accept most dental insurance plans and are committed to helping you understand your coverage. Our team will work with you to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs. Please contact our office for detailed information about insurance and payment options.

What technology does Eastside Modern Dentistry use?

Eastside Modern Dentistry utilizes state-of-the-art technology to enhance the quality and comfort of our dental care. This includes digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, dental lasers, and computer-aided design and manufacturing (CAD/CAM) for same-day crowns. Our modern equipment allows us to provide precise, efficient, and minimally invasive treatments.
