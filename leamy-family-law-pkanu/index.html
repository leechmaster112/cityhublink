Leamy Family Law

Leamy Family Law is a Calgary, Alberta‑based family law firm providing comprehensive legal services for individuals and families navigating separation and related legal matters. The firm’s website outlines its focus on a full range of family law areas, including divorce and separation, parenting and child custody, child and spousal support, property division for married and unmarried couples, and the drafting of separation, prenuptial, and cohabitation agreements. The firm also offers services for adoption and alternative dispute resolution such as mediation, lawyer‑assisted negotiation, and legal coaching. The site includes detailed information about how the firm approaches client matters, its consultation process, independent legal advice, billing and fees policies, and general contact details. Leamy Family Law emphasizes tailored legal solutions designed to minimize emotional stress and cost while achieving fair and informed outcomes for clients.
