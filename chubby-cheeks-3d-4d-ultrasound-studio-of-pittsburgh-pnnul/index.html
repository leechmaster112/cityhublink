Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the difference between 3D and 4D ultrasound?

A: Both 3D and 4D ultrasounds use advanced technology to create detailed images. 3D provides a still, three-dimensional view, often focusing on facial features. 4D builds upon this by adding motion, providing real-time, live video of your baby's movements and expressions.

Q2: Is 3D/4D ultrasound safe?

A: Yes, the sound wave technology used in 3D and 4D ultrasounds is the same as the standard 2D ultrasound used for medical care. It is considered safe for both mother and baby when performed by trained professionals following established guidelines.

Q3: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound?

A: A 3D or 4D ultrasound typically requires a minimum gestational age of 13 weeks for optimal image clarity. Scheduling your appointment around this time frame often yields the best results.

Q4: Do I need a prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound?

A: While a 3D or 4D ultrasound is an elective service and not typically required for medical reasons, we may request basic information about your pregnancy. A prescription from your obstetrician is generally not needed unless medically indicated.

Q5: How long does a typical 3D/4D ultrasound appointment take?

A: Appointments usually last between 45 minutes to an hour. This allows sufficient time for the imaging session and for you to view the images with our staff.
