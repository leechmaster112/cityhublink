Our Services in Bellaire, TX:

Our Bellaire location offers four key types of advanced imaging services:

Preventive Heart Scan: This non-invasive, radiation-free test evaluates your coronary artery calcium (CAC) score. It provides valuable insight into the presence of calcified plaque in your coronary arteries, a key indicator of heart disease risk. Knowing your CAC score can be a powerful motivator for adopting healthier lifestyle choices and discussing preventative strategies with your doctor.

Lung Scan: Our lung scan service is crucial for early detection of lung conditions, including lung cancer and chronic lung diseases. This vital imaging helps doctors assess lung function, diagnose abnormalities, and monitor the progression of existing conditions. Regular lung scans can be an essential part of proactive respiratory health management for Bellaire residents.

Body Scan: Our comprehensive body scan offers a detailed look at various internal structures. Depending on the specific needs, this can include multi-detector computed tomography (MDCT) scans designed to assess multiple organ systems simultaneously. It's a versatile tool for preventative health checks and evaluating various bodily functions.

Virtual Colonoscopy (CT Colonography): This advanced imaging technique provides a detailed, three-dimensional view of the colon and rectum using computed tomography. It's a less invasive alternative to traditional colonoscopy for initial screening and follow-up examinations, offering comfort and convenience while still providing high-quality diagnostic information.
