After ensuring safety, call 911 if necessary, exchange information with other drivers, document the scene (photos, witness contact info), and seek medical attention promptly, even for seemingly minor injuries.

It's advisable to contact a lawyer as soon as possible after the accident. Prompt action helps preserve evidence and protect your legal rights.
