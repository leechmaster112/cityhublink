Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What is paint protection film (PPF) and how does it benefit my car?

Paint protection film (PPF) is a transparent, durable film applied to the painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as a sacrificial layer, protecting your car's original paint from damage caused by rock chips, scratches, scuffs, insect acids, and environmental contaminants. This helps maintain your vehicle's pristine appearance and resale value.

Q2: How long does a ceramic coating last on my vehicle?

The longevity of a ceramic coating can vary depending on the specific product used, application quality, and maintenance. However, professionally applied ceramic coatings from DPS Automotive typically offer protection for several years, providing a durable, hydrophobic barrier that repels water and dirt, making your vehicle easier to clean and maintain its shine.

Q3: Can window tinting be applied to all types of vehicles?

Yes, window tinting can be applied to virtually all types of vehicles, including cars, SUVs, trucks, and vans. Our team at DPS Automotive in Arlington Heights uses high-quality films and precise application techniques to ensure a perfect fit and a professional finish for your specific vehicle model.

Q4: Will PPF or ceramic coating affect the original paint color or finish of my car?

No, both paint protection film and ceramic coatings are designed to be clear and virtually invisible. PPF is a transparent film that preserves the original look of your paint, while ceramic coatings enhance the gloss and depth of your paint without altering its color.

Q5: How do I maintain my vehicle after having PPF or ceramic coating applied?

Maintaining your vehicle after these protective services is generally easier. For PPF, simply wash and dry your vehicle as you normally would, avoiding abrasive materials. For ceramic coatings, we recommend using pH-neutral car soaps and avoiding harsh chemicals or automatic car washes with abrasive brushes. We can provide specific care instructions tailored to the services you receive.
