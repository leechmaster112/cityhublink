Insa Dodam

Welcome to InsaDodam. The best Korean restaurant in Seoul, Korea. We are located on Insadong street, offering dishes such as bibimbap, bulgogi, makgeolli, etc. Our place has been renovated from a traditional hanok to retain its authentic charm while providing a tidy environment. Lastly, we are confident that all of our menus paired with our makgeolli will take you to unforgettable moment.

Welcom to InsaDodam

Welcome to InsaDodam, your authentic Korean dining destination nestled in the heart of Seoul's vibrant Jongno District. We take immense pride in offering an unparalleled culinary experience right here at our location, 5-1 Insadong 16-gil.

Insadong is world-renowned for its rich history, traditional architecture, and, most importantly, its concentration of authentic Korean culture. Our restaurant, InsaDodam, is a testament to this heritage, providing a cozy and welcoming space where you can immerse yourself in the true essence of Korean flavors.

At InsaDodam, we specialize in serving the best Korean cuisine, prepared with the finest ingredients and traditional techniques. Our menu features a carefully curated selection of classic Korean dishes, from comforting staples like Japchae (Korean rice cakes) and Dakbokki (spicy chicken rice cakes) to hearty Bibimbap (mixed rice) and savory Jjigae (stew). We also offer a delightful variety of Korean side dishes (banchan) and refreshing Korean teas to complement your meal.

Whether you're a local resident exploring the charms of Jongno or a visitor seeking an authentic taste of Korea, InsaDodam at 5-1 Insadong 16-gil provides a perfect setting. Our attentive staff is dedicated to ensuring your visit is memorable, offering warm hospitality and expert guidance to enhance your dining experience.

Step into our restaurant and feel the authentic atmosphere of Seoul's culinary heart. We invite you to discover the rich flavors and warm hospitality that define InsaDodam. Experience the best of Korean cuisine in the heart of Jongno, right here at our Insadong location.

For reservations or more information about our menu and services, please visit our website or call us directly.
