Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you only serve customers in Portland?

A: Yes, Pure Clean NW operates exclusively out of our Portland location at 1327 SE Tacoma St, #162. We focus our services entirely on the specific needs of the Portland area.

Q2: How long does a typical roof cleaning project take?

A: The duration depends on the size of the roof and the extent of the buildup. Generally, a standard residential roof cleaning can take anywhere from 2 to 4 hours. We'll provide a more accurate estimate after your initial consultation.

Q3: What cleaning methods do you use?

A: We primarily use low-pressure washing techniques combined with specialized eco-friendly cleaning solutions designed for roof surfaces. This approach is effective for removing dirt, moss, and algae while being gentle on the roofing materials. High-pressure methods are used cautiously and only where appropriate.

Q4: Do you handle moss control, or just removal?

A: We offer comprehensive moss management. While we can remove existing moss, we also provide preventative treatments designed specifically for the Portland climate to inhibit regrowth and protect your roof's integrity.

Q5: Can you clean gutters and downspouts as part of your service?

A: Yes, we often recommend cleaning gutters and downspouts as part of a comprehensive roof care package. Debris in gutters can contribute to roof staining and drainage issues. Please mention this interest when scheduling your appointment.
