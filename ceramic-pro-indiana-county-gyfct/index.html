Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What exactly is Ceramic Pro and how does it protect my vehicle?

Ceramic Pro is a revolutionary nanoceramic coating that forms a strong, semi-permanent bond with your vehicle's paint. It creates a hard, protective layer that shields your car from scratches, chemical stains, UV damage, and environmental contaminants, while also enhancing its gloss and making it easier to clean.

Q2: How long does Ceramic Pro protection last?

The longevity of Ceramic Pro coatings is exceptional. Depending on the specific package applied and vehicle maintenance, our coatings can last for many years, offering durable protection far beyond traditional waxes or sealants.

Q3: Will Ceramic Pro prevent my car from getting scratched?

Ceramic Pro coatings add a significant layer of hardness to your vehicle's paint, making it more resistant to light scratches and swirl marks that can occur during washing or from everyday use. While it doesn't make your paint indestructible, it greatly reduces the likelihood of minor abrasions.

Q4: Is Ceramic Pro difficult to maintain after application?

No, quite the opposite. Ceramic Pro creates a hydrophobic surface, meaning water and dirt bead up and slide off easily. This makes washing your vehicle much simpler and faster, and it helps to keep your car cleaner for longer periods.
