Free SRCM Heartfulness Meditation Centre Erode

Heartfulness Meditation Centre in Erode welcomes all to explore inner peace through free guided meditation training. Visitors can attend Heartfulness meditation sessions with a certified trainer at no cost and join our Sunday group meditations held every week at the Shri Ram Chandra Mission Ashram, Erode. Part of the global Heartfulness movement and affiliated with SRCM, HFI, FBH, and Sahaj Marg, the Erode centre is connected to Kanha Shanti Vanam, Hyderabad, the world headquarters. Heartfulness meditation is offered in 160+ countries, inviting everyone to begin their journey toward calmness, clarity, and inner balance here in Erode.
