Smith Playground Dog Park

Smith Playground Dog Park at 235 Western Avenue in Allston is a secure fenced off-leash area within the larger Smith Playground complex. It features separate areas for large and small dogs, water amenities, shade, and enrichment elements like mounds and platforms. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Location

Smith Playground Dog Park offers a dedicated, secure haven for your canine companions to roam and play leash-free within its fenced-off grounds.

This Allston-based park, located at 235 Western Avenue, is specifically designed as a safe, enclosed space where dogs can exercise, socialize, and simply be dogs without the constraints of leashes.

The primary service here is providing a controlled environment, ensuring peace of mind for owners observing their pets enjoy unrestricted freedom in a designated outdoor area.

What makes Smith Playground Dog Park unique?

Its specific design as a secure, fenced off-leash zone within the existing Smith Playground, offering a dedicated space for dog activity.

Is the dog park area separate from the main playground?

Yes, it is a distinct, fenced-off section within Smith Playground, ensuring a safe and contained environment for dogs.

What type of supervision is expected at the park?

Owners are expected to supervise their dogs at all times while they are utilizing the off-leash area.

While not explicitly stated, it is always recommended to ensure your dog is up-to-date on vaccinations for the safety of all park visitors.
