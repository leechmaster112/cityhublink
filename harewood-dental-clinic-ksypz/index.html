Comprehensive Dental Services

At Harewood Dental Clinic, we provide a wide array of dental services designed to cater to all your oral health needs. Our commitment to excellence ensures that every patient receives personalized attention and effective treatment. Below is an overview of the services we offer:

Dental Check-ups and Cleanings

Regular dental check-ups and professional cleanings are the cornerstone of maintaining good oral health. At our Nanaimo clinic, our skilled dental hygienists perform thorough cleanings to remove plaque and tartar buildup, followed by a comprehensive examination to assess your oral health. Early detection of potential issues is key to preventing more serious problems down the line.

Fillings and Restorations

If you experience a tooth decay or damage, our team can provide effective fillings using modern materials to restore the function and appearance of your teeth. We utilize advanced techniques to ensure minimal discomfort and a natural-looking result.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

For more extensive restorations, we offer dental crowns and bridges. Crowns are custom-made coverings that protect damaged teeth, while bridges can replace missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent healthy teeth. These restorative options help restore your ability to eat comfortably and speak clearly.

Dental Implants

Missing teeth can significantly impact your quality of life. At Harewood Dental Clinic, we offer dental implants, a revolutionary solution for tooth replacement. Dental implants provide a durable and natural-looking alternative to traditional dentures or bridges, offering a permanent and functional restoration.

Root Canal Therapy

When a tooth's pulp becomes infected or inflamed, root canal therapy may be necessary to save the tooth. Our experienced endodontists perform this procedure with precision and care, ensuring minimal discomfort and a successful outcome.

Oral Surgery

In some cases, minor oral surgeries may be required to address complex dental issues. Our clinic is equipped to handle procedures such as tooth extractions and gum surgery, ensuring patient safety and comfort throughout the process.

Dental Emergencies

Even with the best care, dental emergencies can occur. At Harewood Dental Clinic, we understand that unexpected dental issues require prompt attention. While we encourage scheduling regular appointments, we can often accommodate urgent dental needs to provide immediate relief.

Kids' Dental Care

We believe that establishing good dental habits early on is crucial for a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our team is experienced in providing gentle and child-friendly dental care, ensuring that children feel comfortable and at ease during their visits. We offer treatments tailored to the unique needs of pediatric patients.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of dental services does Harewood Dental Clinic offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services, including dental check-ups, cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants, root canal therapy, oral surgery, and emergency dental care.

2. How often should I schedule a dental check-up?

We recommend scheduling a dental check-up at least twice a year for most patients. However, the frequency may vary depending on individual needs, so it's best to consult with our team.

3. Does Harewood Dental Clinic offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand that dental care can be a significant investment, and we offer various payment options to help make treatment accessible for our patients.

4. Are dental crowns and bridges covered by insurance?

Dental insurance coverage varies depending on the plan. We can assist you in understanding your benefits and discuss any out-of-pocket costs associated with these services.

5. What should I do in case of a dental emergency outside of office hours?

While we strive to accommodate dental emergencies, please contact our office during business hours. For urgent needs outside of office hours, we recommend reaching out to a local dental emergency service.
