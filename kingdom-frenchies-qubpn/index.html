Location

Welcome to Kingdom Frenchies, your trusted source for exceptional French Bulldog puppies nestled in the heart of Millersburg, Ohio. Located at 4392 Co Rd 160, Millersburg, OH 44654, we are proud to serve the families right here in our community and surrounding areas of Ohio.

Kingdom Frenchies is dedicated to responsible and ethical breeding practices. We are committed to producing healthy, well-socialized, and happy French Bulldog puppies that make wonderful companions. Our focus is on breeding dogs with desirable temperaments, sound structure, and importantly, a commitment to health, ensuring our puppies have the best start in life.

Our journey with French Bulldogs began with a passion for the breed and a desire to contribute healthy genetics to the community. We carefully select our breeding pairs based on extensive health testing, temperament evaluations, and adherence to breed standards. We believe that responsible breeding is about more than just producing puppies; it's about creating a legacy of healthy dogs and providing dedicated support to their new owners.

At Kingdom Frenchies, we understand that bringing a puppy home is a significant decision. That's why we prioritize transparency and open communication with all inquiries. We take the time to answer questions thoroughly, provide detailed information about our breeding program, and ensure potential puppy owners are making informed choices. We are here to guide you through the process, from understanding the responsibilities of French Bulldog ownership to finding the perfect puppy match for your family.

Our primary goal is the health and well-being of our breeding dogs and the puppies they produce. We conduct thorough health screenings for common French Bulldog issues, including Brachycephalic Airway Syndrome (BAS), Patellar Luxation, Hip Dysplasia, and others. This commitment to health testing helps us minimize the risk of passing on genetic predispositions and ensures the puppies we place are robust and resilient. We strive to breed French Bulldogs that are not only beautiful but also sturdy, active, and free from severe health concerns.

Temperament is equally important to us. We raise our puppies in a nurturing and socialized environment, starting from birth, to ensure they develop into confident, friendly, and well-behaved companions. We believe a French Bulldog's personality should be as delightful as their appearance. Our puppies are exposed to various sights, sounds, and gentle handling to prepare them for their new homes and the diverse experiences they will encounter.

While our primary focus is on the health and temperament of our puppies, we also take pride in our customer service. We are committed to building lasting relationships with the families who adopt our puppies. This includes providing detailed health records, answering questions long after the puppy is placed, and offering guidance on training, care, and puppy development. We want you to feel supported and confident in your decision to bring a Kingdom Frenchie into your life.

Our small-scale, family-run operation allows us to give each litter the attention and care it deserves. We do not prioritize quantity over quality. Instead, we limit the number of litters per year to ensure our dogs are not over-bred and can enjoy a good quality of life alongside their duties as breeding stock. This dedication to our dogs' welfare reflects in the healthy, happy puppies we produce.

If you are located in the Millersburg area or nearby towns in Ohio and are considering adding a French Bulldog to your family, we encourage you to reach out. Please visit our website at https://kingdomfrenchies.com/ to learn more about our current litters, view photos, and read about our breeding standards. You can also contact us directly by phone at +13304734057.

We look forward to connecting with you and potentially welcoming a future Kingdom Frenchie into your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you only ship puppies within Ohio?

A1: While our primary focus is on serving families in the Millersburg, OH area, we are open to placing puppies with qualified homes in other parts of Ohio. Please contact us directly via our website or phone number to discuss your specific situation and location.

Q2: What health guarantees do you offer with your puppies?

A2: We stand behind the health of our puppies. We provide a health guarantee that covers potential congenital defects for a specified period after placement. Our guarantee details are outlined in our contract and litter information. We emphasize responsible breeding practices and health testing to minimize potential issues.

Q3: How long do you keep the puppies with you before placing them?

A3: We typically keep our puppies with us for a minimum of 8 weeks, ensuring they are fully weaned, socialized, and ready for their new homes. Puppies are placed on a first-come, first-served basis once they reach this age, provided they have had their initial health checks and vaccinations.

Q4: Do you require a home visit before placing a puppy?

A4: Yes, we strongly recommend a home visit (if feasible) or a detailed phone/skype/virtual call before placing a puppy. This allows us to ensure the prospective owner is prepared for the commitment of French Bulldog ownership and provides an opportunity for us to answer any specific questions you may have.

Q5: How can I stay updated on your current litters?

A5: The best way to stay informed about our current litters is to regularly check our website at https://kingdomfrenchies.com/. You can also sign up for our email list through the website contact form to receive notifications when new litters are expected. You can also call us directly at +13304734057.
