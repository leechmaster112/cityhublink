Plumb Creek Dog Park

Plumb Creek Dog Park is one of Knoxville's largest dog parks, spanning 8 acres with separate fenced areas for large and small dogs. The 7-acre large dog area includes a walking path, water fountain, and shaded shelter, while the 1-acre small dog area features its own water fountain and shelter. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.
