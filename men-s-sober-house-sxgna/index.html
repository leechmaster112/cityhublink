A Community Within A Community

Located at 1501 S Newkirk St, Philadelphia, PA 19146, Men's Sober House provides a dedicated space for men navigating the challenging path of recovery. We understand that the journey towards sobriety requires more than just abstaining from substances; it demands a supportive environment, structure, and a strong sense of community.

Our mission at Men's Sober House is to provide the best care possible for men committed to achieving and maintaining long-term sobriety right here in our neighborhood. We foster a "Community Within A Community," creating a safe and understanding environment where men can share experiences, offer mutual support, and build meaningful connections with peers who truly understand the struggles and triumphs of recovery.

We offer a structured living environment designed to promote healthy habits and positive lifestyle changes. Our focus is on peer support, accountability, and personal growth. While we do not provide medical detoxification or licensed clinical treatment, our sober living home provides a crucial transitional step for men moving towards independent, substance-free living.

At 1501 S Newkirk St, we emphasize:

A drug-free and alcohol-free environment – Uphold by our residents and staff. Mutual respect and understanding – Treating each resident with dignity and empathy. Structure and routine – Daily schedules promoting sobriety, wellness, and personal responsibility. Opportunities for personal development – Support for recovery goals and life skills building. A focus on community – Connecting with others in recovery for shared support.

We are committed to being a stable resource for men in Philadelphia seeking a sober lifestyle. Our dedicated team is available to assist residents with daily living needs and provide guidance focused on maintaining sobriety.
