Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do you offer jet ski rentals for individuals?

A1: Yes, we offer individual jet ski rentals for riders of all skill levels. Perfect for a quick adventure!

Q2: Can beginners learn to jet ski?

A2: Absolutely! We provide beginner lessons for all ages. Our certified instructors will teach you the basics safely and confidently.

Q3: What is included in a guided tour?

A3: Our guided tours include safety instructions, a high-performance jet ski, life jackets, and a professional guide who will take you to the best spots in the area.

Q4: Are there age restrictions for jet skiing?

A4: Riders must be at least 16 years old and possess a valid driver's license. Minors must be accompanied by an adult.

Q5: What safety measures do you have in place?

A5: Safety is our top priority. All our jet skis are equipped with life jackets, and our guides are trained professionals. We also provide safety instructions before each ride.
