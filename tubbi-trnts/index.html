The Tubbi Difference: Professional Reglazing

Reglazing, also known as refinishing, is a cost-effective and environmentally friendly alternative to replacing your bathtub or tiles. Instead of tearing out old fixtures, which can be a messy and expensive undertaking, Tubbi applies a durable, high-gloss coating that restores the surface to a like-new condition. This process not only enhances the aesthetic appeal of your bathroom but also extends the lifespan of your fixtures, saving you money and reducing waste.

Our team of skilled technicians at Tubbi is dedicated to meticulous craftsmanship. We begin by thoroughly preparing the surface, ensuring it is clean, free of any damage, and properly etched for optimal adhesion of the new coating. This preparation is crucial for a long-lasting and beautiful finish. We then apply multiple layers of our specialized refinishing material, creating a smooth, seamless, and resilient surface that is resistant to stains, chips, and scratches.
