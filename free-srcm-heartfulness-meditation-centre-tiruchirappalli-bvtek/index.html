Free SRCM Heartfulness Meditation Centre Tiruchirappalli

Heartfulness Meditation Tiruchirappalli invites individuals to experience inner peace, clarity, and balance through free guided meditation sessions. Certified Heartfulness trainers offer personalized one-to-one and group meditations at no cost, with weekly group sessions held every Sunday at the Shri Ram Chandra Mission Ashram in Tiruchirappalli. As part of the global Heartfulness movement, affiliated with Shri Ram Chandra Mission (SRCM), Sahaj Marg, and connected to Kanha Shanti Vanam, Hyderabad, our Tiruchirappalli centre is part of a worldwide network offering free meditation in 160+ countries. Begin your journey toward mindfulness and inner transformation here in Tiruchirappalli.
