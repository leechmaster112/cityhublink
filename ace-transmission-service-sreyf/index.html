Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does a typical transmission repair or rebuild take?

A: The duration depends heavily on the specific issue, the type of transmission (automatic or manual), and whether it's a repair or a rebuild. Minor repairs might be completed in a day or two, while a full rebuild can take several days to a week or more. We will provide you with a specific timeline once we've diagnosed the problem.

Q: Should I repair or rebuild my transmission?

A: This depends on the severity of the damage and the age/condition of the transmission. Minor slipping or leaks might be fixed with a repair. However, if internal parts are severely worn, damaged, or leaking significantly, a rebuild might be more cost-effective and durable than a simple repair. Our diagnostic process will help determine the best course of action for your specific situation.

Q: Do you use original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts?

A: We strive to use high-quality parts. While we often utilize genuine OEM parts for their reliability, we also source competitive aftermarket parts that meet or exceed industry standards. The choice often depends on the specific component, availability, and the recommendation based on the repair. We will discuss part options with you.

Q: Do I need to make an appointment for transmission service?

A: While we can often accommodate walk-ins, scheduling an appointment is highly recommended, especially for more complex repairs or rebuilds. You can call us at 417-831-9390 to book your appointment or check our website https://acetransmissionservice.com/ for scheduling information.

Q: What kind of warranty do you offer on your transmission work?

A: We stand behind the quality of our work. Typically, we offer a warranty on both parts and labor for transmission repairs and rebuilds. The specific details of the warranty (duration and coverage) can vary depending on the service performed and parts used. Our service advisor will discuss the warranty terms with you when you drop off your vehicle.
