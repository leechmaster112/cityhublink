Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get my deck or driveway pressure washed?

The frequency depends on various factors like weather conditions, foot traffic, and proximity to trees or other sources of debris. Generally, decks benefit from cleaning 1-2 times per year, while driveways might need cleaning every 6-12 months, or sooner if stains appear.

Is pressure washing safe for all surfaces?

While pressure washing is powerful, our trained technicians are experienced in using the appropriate pressure settings and techniques for different surfaces. We always conduct a pre-inspection and adjust our methods accordingly to ensure safety and prevent damage to your property.

Do I need to prepare anything before your arrival?

Generally, minimal preparation is needed. We ask you to move potted plants, outdoor furniture, toys, and lawn equipment away from the work area. This allows us to work efficiently and ensures thorough cleaning without obstructions.

How long does a typical pressure washing job take?

The duration varies significantly based on the size of the area, the extent of the cleaning required, and the specific services requested. A small driveway might take 30-60 minutes, while a full house wash and deck cleaning could take several hours. We provide a more accurate estimate after your initial consultation.

Are you available year-round for pressure washing services?

We strive to be available year-round, but we generally recommend scheduling exterior cleaning projects during the warmer months (typically spring through fall) for optimal drying conditions and comfort during the job. However, we can often accommodate requests outside these seasons based on weather permitting.
