Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services do you offer at your Trophy Club location?

We specialize in luxury hairstyling and coloring services at our Trophy Club salon. Our services include precision haircutting, color correction and enhancement, signature color services (balayage, ombre, fashion colors), luxury keratin smoothing treatments, advanced keratin therapies, deep conditioning and repair treatments, and comprehensive styling consultations.

Q2: Can I book an appointment for a keratin treatment?

Absolutely! We recommend booking your keratin treatment appointment well in advance, especially for our popular Luxury Keratin Smoothing Treatment and Advanced Keratin Therapy. You can schedule your appointment by calling us at (817) 290-6557 or booking online through our website.

Q3: Do you offer color services for blonde hair?

Yes, we offer a full range of blonde services at our Trophy Club location. Our expert colorists specialize in creating dimensional, natural-looking blonde shades that complement your skin tone and hair texture.

Q4: What is the difference between your Keratin Smoothing Treatment and Keratin Therapy?

Our Luxury Keratin Smoothing Treatment provides significant frizz reduction and lasts approximately 3-5 months. Our Advanced Keratin Therapy is a longer-lasting alternative, offering results that typically last 6-8 months, making it ideal for maintaining smooth, frizz-free hair between regular treatments.

Q5: Are consultations available before major services like coloring or keratin treatments?

Yes, we highly recommend consultations for significant services like coloring and keratin treatments. During a consultation, you can discuss your desired outcome, address any concerns, and ask questions about the process. You can schedule a consultation by calling our Trophy Club location at (817) 290-6557.
