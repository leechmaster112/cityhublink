Transmission Rebuild Services

When a transmission is beyond simple repair but not quite at the point of needing a costly replacement, a rebuild can be an excellent alternative. Ace Transmission Service offers professional transmission rebuilds. This process involves disassembling the transmission, inspecting all components, replacing worn-out parts, and reassembling it according to factory specifications or better. A rebuild often restores the transmission to like-new condition, offering improved performance and fuel efficiency at a fraction of the cost of a new unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the most common transmission problems you see in Springfield vehicles?

Answer: We frequently encounter issues like transmission slipping (especially under load), delayed or erratic shifting, rough idling or shifting, transmission fluid leaks, and harsh downshifts. Regular maintenance can often prevent these problems.

2. How often should I get a transmission fluid change?

Answer: While manufacturer recommendations vary, many experts suggest a transmission fluid and filter change every 50,000 to 100,000 miles. We recommend checking your owner's manual and discussing a maintenance schedule tailored to your vehicle.

3. What's the difference between transmission repair and a rebuild?

Answer: Repair typically addresses specific damaged parts within the transmission. A rebuild involves completely disassembling the transmission, replacing all worn or damaged components, and reassembling it, often resulting in better performance and longevity than a simple repair.

4. Do you offer mobile transmission services in Springfield?

Answer: Currently, we provide our services primarily from our shop at 2610 W. Kearney St in Springfield. We recommend bringing your vehicle to our location for the best service environment.

5. What kind of warranty do your transmission repairs and rebuilds come with?

Answer: We stand behind the quality of our work and typically offer warranties on both our repair and rebuild services. The specific warranty details will depend on the scope of the work performed, so be sure to ask our technicians during your consultation.
