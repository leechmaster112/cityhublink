Location

The Ruby On Johnson, located at 642A Johnson St, Victoria, BC V8W 1M6, is more than just a place to eat; it's a vibrant hub for those seeking exceptional brunch experiences right in the heart of downtown Victoria. This funky spot brings together creativity, community, and delicious food, offering a unique atmosphere where locals and visitors alike can gather to enjoy a memorable meal.

Nestled on the lively Johnson Street, The Ruby On Johnson has quickly become a favourite destination for its distinctive character and commitment to quality. The interior is designed to be welcoming and energetic, reflecting the creative spirit of the menu. It's the perfect place to start your day right, offering a diverse selection of brunch items that cater to various tastes and dietary preferences.

The Ruby On Johnson prides itself on sourcing fresh, locally-inspired ingredients whenever possible, supporting the local food scene while delivering innovative dishes. Their creative brunch menu features a range of options, from hearty classics to lighter fare, all prepared with care and presented thoughtfully. Signature items often include unique takes on eggs benedict, inventive avocado toast variations, creative pastries, and refreshing beverages to complement the meal.

Whether you're catching up with friends, enjoying a weekend breakfast with your partner, or hosting a small gathering, The Ruby On Johnson provides the ideal setting. The focus is on quality ingredients, creative preparation, and a relaxed yet stylish vibe that makes every visit special. It's a space designed for connection, enjoyment, and indulging in delicious food.

Operating primarily as a brunch establishment, The Ruby On Johnson offers a warm and inviting environment for lunch and early dinner options as well, depending on the day and season. Their knowledgeable staff are always ready to assist with recommendations and ensure a pleasant dining experience for all guests.
