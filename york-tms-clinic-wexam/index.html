Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy is a non-surgical procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Specifically, it targets areas of the brain that are underactive in individuals experiencing depression. These magnetic pulses are delivered through a device that is placed near the scalp. The stimulation is thought to help regulate mood and alleviate depressive symptoms by increasing activity in key brain regions. Unlike electroconvulsive therapy (ECT), TMS does not require anesthesia and has fewer side effects, making it a more accessible and comfortable option for many.

Q3: What can I expect during a TMS treatment session at 1806 N George St, York, PA 17404?

A5: A typical course of TMS therapy at our York, PA facility involves daily sessions, usually five days a week, for a period of 4 to 6 weeks.
