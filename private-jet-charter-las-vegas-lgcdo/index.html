Q4: What is included in a typical private jet charter package for travel to Las Vegas?

A4: A standard private jet charter includes positioning the aircraft at the destination airport, positioning back at your point of origin, block hours (the time from engine start until shutdown at the destination), fuel consumption as per the flight plan, and the flight crew. Depending on the package, it may or may not include inflight catering, alcoholic beverages, and specific ground services like airport transfers. We provide a detailed quote outlining exactly what is included before you book.
