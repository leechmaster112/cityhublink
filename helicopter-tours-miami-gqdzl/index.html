Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What does the New York helicopter tour include?

We provide a dedicated helicopter for your exclusive tour. The flight typically lasts around 30-45 minutes and includes a certified pilot and views of key New York landmarks, depending on the specific tour package chosen.

Q2: How far in advance should I book my New York helicopter tour?

We recommend booking your New York tour as far in advance as possible, especially during peak travel times or weekends. However, last-minute availability may sometimes exist, so calling us directly is always best.

Q3: Are New York helicopter tours safe?

Absolutely. Safety is our top priority. We operate modern, well-maintained helicopters and employ experienced, certified pilots who adhere strictly to safety regulations for all New York flights.

Q4: What should I wear for the New York flight?

Comfortable clothing is fine. We suggest wearing closed-toe shoes. While light aircraft activity may cause slight movement, our focus is on smooth flying for optimal sightseeing. Dress comfortably for the weather conditions expected during your flight.

Q5: What is the cancellation policy for New York tours?

Please check the specific terms for the tour package you choose, as policies may vary slightly. Generally, we require 24-48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling to receive a full refund or rebooking credit for New York flights.
