Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What conditions qualify for medical cannabis in Pennsylvania?

A: Pennsylvania law recognizes several qualifying medical conditions, including cancer, HIV/AIDS, hepatitis C, epilepsy, glaucoma, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, PTSD, and others. Please consult with your healthcare provider to determine if you meet the criteria.

Q2: How do I become a registered medical cannabis patient?

A: To become a registered patient, you need a physician certification confirming your qualifying condition. Once you have this, you can apply for a Pennsylvania Medical Marijuana Card online through the state's portal.

Q3: What types of cannabis products do you offer at your Wilkes-Barre location?

A: Ethos Wilkes-Barre offers a variety of products, including flower, edibles, concentrates, topicals, and pre-roll cigarettes. Our selection is carefully curated to provide effective and safe options for our patients.

Q4: Are there any restrictions on how much medical cannabis I can purchase?

A: Patients with a valid Pennsylvania Medical Marijuana Card can purchase up to 24 ounces of cannabis product every 28 days. There are also limits on the amount of cannabis oil you can purchase at one time.
