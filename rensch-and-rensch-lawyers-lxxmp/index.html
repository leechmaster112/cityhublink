Frequently Asked Questions

Question: Do you only handle personal injury cases in Omaha?

Answer: Yes, Rensch & Rensch Law exclusively handles personal injury cases. Our firm is located at 7602 Pacific St Suite 300, Omaha, NE 68114, and we serve clients throughout Omaha.

Question: How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Omaha?

Answer: The time limit to file a personal injury claim in Nebraska, including Omaha, is typically three years from the date of the injury. It is crucial to consult with our attorneys promptly to ensure your claim is filed within this statute of limitations.

Question: What types of injuries do you handle?

Answer: We handle a wide variety of injuries resulting from the negligence of others. This includes, but is not limited to, car accident injuries, workplace accidents, medical malpractice, slip and fall injuries, burns, traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord injuries, and other serious or complex injuries.

Question: Is there a cost to consult with your firm?

Answer: Yes, we offer free initial consultations to our clients at our Omaha law office. There is no obligation, and we will use our consultation to understand your case and advise you on the best course of action.

Question: How long does a personal injury case typically take?

Answer: The duration of a personal injury case can vary significantly depending on the complexity of the case, the extent of the injuries, the willingness of the insurance company to settle, and whether the case goes to trial. Simple cases might be resolved in a few months, while more complex cases can take a year or longer. Our team will keep you informed about the progress of your case.
