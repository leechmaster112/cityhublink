Our Specialized Cleaning Techniques

At Carpet Cleaning Mississauga, we pride ourselves on utilizing state-of-the-art equipment and proven cleaning methods to ensure your carpets are thoroughly cleaned, decontaminated, and protected. Our process is designed to be effective yet gentle, ensuring we don't damage your valuable carpet investment while achieving outstanding results. Here’s a glimpse into the specialized techniques we employ right here in Mississauga:

Hot Water Extraction (Steam Cleaning): This is our most popular and effective method for deep cleaning carpets. Using high-temperature water extraction with powerful suction, we loosen and remove embedded dirt, dust mites, allergens, and even tough stains from the carpet fibers. This method is excellent for both wool and synthetic carpets, providing a deep clean that leaves your carpets feeling fresh and revitalized. The process also helps in drying the carpet relatively quickly, minimizing disruption to your home or office schedule.

CARBONA® Enclosed Cleaning System: We utilize the powerful Carbona Enclosed Cleaning machine, known for its effectiveness in removing tough soils and stains. This machine uses a controlled amount of water and specialized detergents under pressure, followed by powerful extraction. The enclosed nature of the system helps protect your floors and surrounding areas from excess water damage. This method is particularly effective for removing embedded dirt and is excellent for maintaining the integrity and appearance of your Mississauga-area carpets.

Enclosed Buffer Cleaning: Ideal for smaller areas or as a preventative maintenance measure, this method uses a buffer attached to a cleaning solution tank. The buffer gently agitates the carpet while the solution is applied, loosening dirt before it is extracted. This method is less invasive than steam cleaning and is perfect for regular cleaning to keep your carpets looking their best between deep cleans. It's a great option for maintaining the cleanliness of your carpets in your Mississauga home or business.

Pre-Treatment Solutions: Before applying our main cleaning agents, we always pre-treat visible stains and heavily soiled areas. Our specialized pre-treatment solutions are designed to break down and lift stains from the carpet backing without damaging the surface. This step is crucial for tackling spills and marks effectively, ensuring they are addressed before the main cleaning process begins, giving your carpets a thorough clean right here in Mississauga.

Optional Carpet Protection Spray: As part of our service, we offer a protective spray application after cleaning. This helps repel future spills and makes your carpets easier to clean should accidents happen. It adds an extra layer of care for your investment, ensuring your carpets stay cleaner for longer in your Mississauga home or office.
