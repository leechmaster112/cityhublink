Frequently Asked Questions What exactly is a Gold IRA Rollover?

A Gold IRA Rollover involves transferring funds from a traditional IRA, 401(k), or other eligible retirement plan into a self-directed Individual Retirement Account (IRA) that specifically allows investments in physical precious metals like gold, silver, platinum, and palladium. This rollover facilitates diversification away from traditional stocks and bonds.

Are there tax implications for a Gold IRA Rollover?

Generally, a direct rollover from one retirement plan to another IRA is tax-deferred, meaning you don't owe taxes immediately on the funds transferred. However, it's crucial to understand that distributions from a traditional IRA are taxed as income when you withdraw them during retirement. Roth IRA conversions (if applicable) have different tax rules. We advise consulting with a tax professional for advice specific to your situation.
