Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it typically take to install a pool fence?

A: The installation time depends on the size and complexity of the fence, but most residential pool fences can be completed in one to two days. We will provide you with a specific timeline during your free consultation.

Q: What types of materials do you use for pool fences?

A: We primarily use high-quality vinyl and aluminum for our pool fences. These materials are durable, low-maintenance, and resistant to the Las Vegas climate, ensuring longevity and minimal upkeep.

Q: Do you offer custom design options for pool fences?

A: Yes, we work closely with you to design a fence that fits your specific needs and aesthetic preferences. We can discuss various styles, heights, and gate configurations to create a solution that's both functional and beautiful.

Q: Is a pool fence required by law in Las Vegas?

A: Yes, local regulations in Las Vegas typically require a compliant pool fence around any swimming pool, spa, or hot tub on the property. Our installations are designed to meet or exceed these requirements.

Q: How do I maintain my new pool fence?

A: Maintenance for vinyl and aluminum pool fences is minimal. Regular cleaning with mild soap and water is usually sufficient. Avoid using harsh chemicals or abrasive tools that could damage the surface.
