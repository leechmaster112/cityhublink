Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do you locate a pool leak in Orlando?

A: We employ a combination of specialized techniques including Flow Check (monitoring water loss rate), dye testing (introducing colored dyes into the pool water), pressure testing (isolating plumbing lines), and sometimes electronic leak detection (using sensitive instruments to pinpoint sound or vibration). The specific method depends on the suspected location and nature of the leak.

Q2: Is it always necessary to be home during the leak detection process?

A: Not necessarily. While dye testing often requires access to the pool's return jets and skimmer, and pressure testing requires access to plumbing connections, many Flow Check and electronic methods can be performed while you are away. Our technician will discuss the specific access requirements for your situation.

Q3: How long does a typical pool leak detection visit take?

A: The duration varies depending on the complexity of the pool, the suspected leak location, and the method used. A simple Flow Check might take 30-60 minutes, while more involved dye tests or pressure tests could take several hours. We always provide a clear estimate.

Q4: What is the typical cost for finding a pool leak in Orlando?

A: Leak detection costs vary based on the size and complexity of the pool, the suspected location of the leak, and the diagnostic methods required. We offer transparent pricing and will provide a detailed estimate before starting any work. You can learn more about our pricing structure on our website.

Q5: Once the leak is found, who handles the repairs?

A: Dr. Pool Leak Detection specializes in leak detection and diagnosis. While we identify the problem accurately, the actual repair work (patching concrete, replacing plumbing, etc.) is typically handled by the pool's original manufacturer, builder, or a separate qualified pool repair contractor. We provide the precise location and cause of the leak to facilitate a quick and effective repair.
