Location

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - SouthPark, located at 4914 Old Sardis Rd C5, Charlotte, NC 28211, is Charlotte's newest destination for exceptional veterinary care for your beloved pets. We are Charlotte's first Petfolk clinic in the SouthPark neighborhood, established by a dedicated team of expert veterinarians committed to providing the highest standard of care right here in our community.

At our SouthPark location, we understand that pets are cherished family members. That's why we offer a comprehensive range of veterinary services, combining thorough medical care with genuine compassion. Our expert team is dedicated to addressing all your pet's health needs, from routine wellness examinations and vaccinations to more complex medical issues and immediate urgent care.

Our veterinary services are designed to keep your pet healthy and happy throughout their life. We emphasize preventative care as the cornerstone of pet health, which is why we provide thorough wellness examinations tailored to your pet's specific needs, age, and lifestyle. These regular check-ups allow us to monitor your pet's health proactively, catch potential issues early, and adjust their preventative healthcare plan accordingly.

We also offer essential veterinary vaccinations to protect your pet against common and serious diseases. Our knowledgeable staff will guide you on the recommended vaccination schedule for your pet, ensuring they receive the necessary protection throughout their life. Furthermore, we provide crucial fecal testing services to diagnose and treat internal parasites, contributing to your pet's overall health and preventing potential zoonotic diseases.

For pets requiring more intensive medical attention, our facility is equipped to handle a wide range of conditions. We offer comprehensive diagnostic services, including in-house laboratory testing, allowing us to quickly analyze samples and provide prompt diagnoses. Our commitment to thorough diagnostics ensures that your pet receives the most accurate assessment of their health condition.

Our urgent care services are available for pets experiencing sudden illness or injury outside of regular business hours or when routine care is needed. We understand that emergencies can arise unexpectedly, and we are dedicated to providing timely and effective care for your pet during these stressful times. Our experienced veterinarians are adept at managing a variety of urgent situations, offering peace of mind to pet owners in the Charlotte area.

At Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - SouthPark, we are dedicated to providing personalized, compassionate care for your pets.
