Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How quickly can you respond to water damage in Lexington?

We understand the urgency of water damage. Our standard emergency response time for properties in Lexington is typically within 2 hours of receiving a call, and we work around the clock to begin the drying process promptly.

Q2: What types of water damage do you handle?

We handle all types of water damage originating from any source. This includes clean water damage (like from broken water lines), gray water damage (from sources like dishwashers with overflow or leaking washing machines), and black water damage (from flooding involving sewage or contaminated water). Our process adapts to the specific type of water encountered.

Q3: How do you prevent mold growth after water damage?

Mold prevention is a critical part of our restoration process. After water extraction, we utilize powerful dehumidifiers and air movers to drastically reduce humidity levels in the affected area. We typically aim to bring moisture content in building materials back below 15% to prevent mold growth. Our rapid drying process is key to mold mitigation.

Q4: Do you offer emergency services outside of regular business hours?

Absolutely. Water damage doesn't adhere to a schedule, and neither do we. We provide 24/7 emergency services for residents and businesses in Lexington experiencing urgent water damage situations.

Q5: What should I do immediately after water damage occurs in my Lexington home or business?

First, ensure everyone's safety. If there's electrical involvement, turn off the power. Evacuate the area if necessary. Then, call us immediately at (859) 327-1899. While waiting for our team, you can also remove personal belongings from the wet area and move furniture away from walls to prevent further damage.
