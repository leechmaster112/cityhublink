Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Union location?

A1: At our Union center, we provide a range of advanced, minimally invasive treatments for various venous conditions. These include sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and others, tailored to address issues like varicose veins and spider veins.

Q2: Are the treatments at your Union clinic painful?

A2: While some discomfort may be felt during the procedure, our team at Metro Vein Centers Union uses local anesthesia and advanced techniques to ensure patient comfort. Most patients report minimal pain and describe the recovery as relatively comfortable.

Q3: How long does it take to recover from vein treatment at your Union facility?

A3: Recovery time varies depending on the specific treatment and individual factors. Generally, patients can return to light activities immediately after their procedure at our Union center. We provide detailed post-treatment care instructions during your visit.

Q4: Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at your Union center?

A4: While a referral is not always required, we welcome patients who self-refer. You can schedule your initial consultation at our Union location by calling our phone number provided.

Q5: What should I expect during my first visit to your Union clinic?

A5: During your first visit to our Union center, a vascular specialist will conduct a thorough examination, possibly including an ultrasound, to diagnose your condition. We will then discuss the most appropriate treatment options and answer any questions you have.
