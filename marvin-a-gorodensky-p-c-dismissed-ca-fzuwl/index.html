Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment law issues do you handle from your Toronto office?

A1: Our practice at 45 St Clair Ave W focuses exclusively on employment law matters for employees in Toronto. This includes claims related to wrongful dismissal, constructive dismissal, human rights violations, pay deductions, workplace harassment, and failure to accommodate.

Q2: How long do I have to file a claim after being dismissed from my job in Toronto?

A2: Time limits for employment claims in Ontario are strict. Generally, you have two years from the date you became aware of the dismissal or the alleged human rights violation to commence legal proceedings. It is crucial to consult with us promptly after an incident occurs.

Q3: Do I need to have worked for a certain period before I can claim wrongful dismissal?

A3: There is no minimum employment period required to claim wrongful dismissal in Ontario. However, the notice period (the length of time you must be given before being dismissed without cause) is calculated based on your length of service according to the Employment Standards Act. Our office at 45 St Clair Ave W can advise you on your specific situation.

Q4: Can I make a claim if my employer retaliated against me for complaining about harassment?

A4: Yes, retaliation for reporting harassment or participating in an investigation is itself a form of workplace discrimination and a potential employment law claim. We handle such cases regularly from our Toronto office at 45 St Clair Ave W.
