Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are the most common foundation problems in San Antonio?

Foundations in San Antonio can face various challenges, including soil movement due to expansive clay, water table fluctuations, poor drainage, and aging infrastructure. Our team is experienced in identifying and addressing these common local issues.

Q2: How long does a typical foundation repair project take?

The duration of a foundation repair project depends on the specific issue and the chosen repair method. Our team will provide you with a detailed timeline after a thorough inspection at your property.

Q3: Do you offer foundation inspections?

Yes, we provide comprehensive foundation inspections to assess the condition of your home's foundation and identify any potential problems before they become serious.

Q4: How do I know if my foundation needs repair?

Signs of foundation problems can include cracks in walls or floors, doors and windows that stick or don't close properly, uneven floors, and gaps between walls and floors. We recommend scheduling an inspection if you notice any of these symptoms.

Q5: What types of foundation repair services do you offer?

We specialize in a variety of foundation repair techniques, including foundation piers, helical piers, slab jacking, mud jacking, foundation underpinning, and crack repair, tailored to the specific needs of your home in San Antonio.
