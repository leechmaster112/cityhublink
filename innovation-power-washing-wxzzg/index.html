Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Pompton Lakes area and surrounding communities in New Jersey, including but not limited to Glen Rock, Ramsey, Wayne, and Kearny. Please contact us for quotes outside these areas.

How long does a typical power washing job take?

The duration depends on the size of the area, the level of dirt and debris, and the specific services requested. A standard driveway might take 1-2 hours, while a full house exterior or deck cleaning could take several hours. We will provide a more accurate estimate after assessing your property.

Do you offer any guarantees on your work?

While results can vary based on the surface and conditions, we stand behind the quality of our workmanship. We typically offer a satisfaction guarantee on our cleaning services, subject to re-application fees for any missed spots if brought to our attention promptly.

Is your equipment and cleaning process safe for all surfaces?

We use appropriate pressure settings and techniques for various surfaces. However, it's crucial for you to inform us of any specific surface types (like delicate wood, stained concrete, or certain types of siding) beforehand. We will adjust our methods accordingly. Using the wrong pressure or technique on certain surfaces can cause damage.

Do you handle mold and mildew treatment?

Yes, our pressure washing services effectively remove surface mold and mildew. For extensive or deeply embedded mold/mildew issues, especially in areas prone to moisture, we recommend consulting with a specialized remediation service. We can discuss this further during a consultation.
