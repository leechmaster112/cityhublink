Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)?

A: Scalp Micropigmentation is a non-surgical cosmetic procedure that involves depositing pigment onto the scalp to create the appearance of a fuller head of hair. It's particularly effective for individuals experiencing hair thinning or a receding hairline.

Q: How does the SMP procedure work?

A: Our process involves carefully placing tiny dots of pigment onto the scalp using specialized micro-needles. This creates realistic hair follicle impressions, blending seamlessly with existing hair for a natural look.

Q: How long do SMP results typically last?

A: SMP results are considered permanent, although they may fade over time due to natural skin changes and sun exposure. Top-up sessions are usually recommended after 1-3 years to maintain optimal color and definition.

Q: Is the SMP procedure painful?

A: While everyone experiences pain differently, most clients find the procedure manageable with topical anesthesia. We ensure your comfort throughout the treatment process.

Q: What is the aftercare for SMP?

A: Proper aftercare is crucial for optimal healing and results. We provide detailed instructions including avoiding direct sun exposure, sweating, and certain activities for several weeks post-treatment to ensure the best outcome.
