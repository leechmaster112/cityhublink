Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do I need to book an appointment at Stay Local Tattoo and Piercing Wheat Ridge?

We highly recommend booking an appointment to ensure the artist you prefer is available for your desired time. Walk-ins may be accommodated if the studio is not busy, but scheduling ahead is appreciated.

Q2: What styles of tattoos do your Wheat Ridge artists specialize in?

Our artists at Stay Local Tattoo and Piercing Wheat Ridge are skilled in various styles including realism, watercolor, neo-traditional, American, geometric, tribal, and many others. Discuss your specific style with us during your consultation.

Q3: How long does a typical tattoo or piercing session take at your Wheat Ridge studio?

The duration depends on the size, complexity, and type of design or piercing. Your artist will provide an estimated time during your consultation. First sessions often require multiple visits, while touch-ups can often be completed in one or two shorter appointments.

Q4: Do you offer piercing for locations other than ears at the Wheat Ridge location?

Yes, our Wheat Ridge studio offers professional piercing services for various body parts, including dermal anchors, nose, eyebrows, lips, and more. Consult with our staff to discuss the options available.

Q5: What should I do before and after getting a tattoo or piercing at Stay Local Tattoo and Piercing Wheat Ridge?

Before your appointment, avoid blood thinners (like ibuprofen/aspirin, alcohol, and nicotine) for 24-48 hours. Aftercare instructions will be provided by your artist. Generally, this involves gentle cleaning and applying a recommended ointment or moisturizer to keep the area healing properly.
