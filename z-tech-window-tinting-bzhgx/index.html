Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of window tint do you offer at your Countryside shop?

We offer a variety of high-performance window films, including ceramic, carbon, and dyed tints. Each type provides different levels of heat rejection, UV protection, and privacy. We can help you choose the best option for your vehicle during your consultation at our location.

2. How long does a window tint installation take at your 55th St location?

Most standard vehicle tinting services are completed within 2 to 4 hours at our shop. The exact time can vary depending on the vehicle type and the specific service chosen.

3. Is window tinting at your facility legal in Illinois?

Yes, we ensure all our tint installations comply with Illinois state laws regarding visible light transmission (VLT). Our experts will inform you of the legal limits for your specific vehicle during your appointment.

4. Do you provide a warranty on your tint work?

Absolutely. We stand behind our craftsmanship and the materials we use. Our window tinting services include a warranty that covers issues like fading, peeling, and bubbling. Specific warranty details are provided upon service completion.

5. How do I care for my newly tinted windows after leaving your shop?

We recommend waiting approximately 3 to 5 days before rolling down your windows to allow the film to cure completely. Clean your tinted windows with a soft, non-abrasive cloth and ammonia-free cleaner to maintain their clarity and finish.
