Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How long does it typically take to build a custom pool at your Richmond location?

Generally, a standard residential pool project can take anywhere from 6 to 10 weeks from the time we break ground until the pool is filled with water and ready for use. We provide detailed project timelines during your initial consultation.

A: Yes, we understand that building a pool is a significant investment. We offer various financing options to help make your dream pool project more manageable. Our team can discuss flexible payment plans tailored to your budget during your visit to our Richmond showroom or over the phone.

Q3: What types of pool shapes and styles do you construct for homeowners on McCrary Road?

A: Precision Pools and Spas specializes in a wide variety of custom pool shapes and styles designed to complement any Richmond home. A: Absolutely. Extending your swimming season is a common goal for Richmond homeowners. We consider factors like your desired water temperature, usage patterns, and local climate when recommending solutions.
