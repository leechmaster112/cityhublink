Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is calisthenics?

A1: Calisthenics is a form of exercise that uses your body weight as resistance. It includes push-ups, pull-ups, squats, lunges, and other bodyweight movements to build strength, endurance, and flexibility.

Q4: What should I bring to my first session?
