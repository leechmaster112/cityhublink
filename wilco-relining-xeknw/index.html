Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is pipe relining?

A: Pipe relining, also known as trenchless pipe rehabilitation, is a process where a flexible liner is inserted into your existing damaged pipe. Once in place, it's cured using UV light (or sometimes steam) to create a seamless, durable pipe within your pipe. This restores flow, repairs cracks, and prevents future blockages.

Q2: How does the UV curing technology work?

A: We use specialized equipment to insert a resin-saturated liner into your pipe. This liner is then exposed to intense UV light, typically delivered by a robotic arm via a camera inspection system. The UV light instantly hardens the resin, creating a strong, glass-like pipe within your existing pipe structure.

Q3: Is the relining process suitable for all types of pipe damage?

A: Wilco Relining Sydney specializes in addressing a range of pipe issues including cracks, root intrusion, corrosion, misalignment, and minor blockages. A thorough internal inspection is always conducted first to determine if relining is the most appropriate and effective solution for your specific pipe condition.

Q4: How long does a typical relining project take in Sydney?

A: The duration of a relining project depends on the pipe's size, length, and complexity. However, due to the trenchless nature of the process, most relining jobs in Sydney can be completed within a single day, minimizing disruption to your property.

Q5: Is the process environmentally friendly?

A: Yes, pipe relining is considered an eco-friendly solution. It avoids the need for excavation, reducing soil disturbance and the removal of existing material. Furthermore, the materials used in modern relining are often designed to be durable and resistant, potentially reducing the need for future interventions and associated waste.
