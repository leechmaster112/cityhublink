Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Unlike traditional treatments, TMS therapy does not involve surgery or anesthesia, and it typically has fewer side effects than medication. Patients can resume their normal activities immediately after each session.

Frequently Asked Questions about TMS Therapy at TMS of the Carolinas, Fayetteville
Q1: What is TMS therapy and how does it work?

A1: TMS therapy is a non-invasive medical treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. It is FDA-cleared for the treatment of major depressive disorder in adults who have not achieved satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medications. The magnetic pulses are thought to help reawaken dormant brain cells and improve communication between brain regions.

Q2: Is TMS therapy painful?

A2: Most patients find TMS therapy to be well-tolerated. You may experience a mild tapping or thumping sensation at the treatment site on your head, and some patients report a mild headache after treatment, which usually subsides quickly. We take steps to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

Q3: What is the typical treatment schedule for TMS therapy?

A3: A typical course of TMS therapy involves daily treatments, five days a week, for approximately 4 to 6 weeks. Each session lasts about 20 to 40 minutes. Your treatment plan will be personalized based on your individual needs and response to therapy.

Q4: Are there any side effects associated with TMS therapy?

A4: The most common side effect is a mild headache at the treatment site, which typically resolves on its own or with over-the-counter pain relievers. Other less common side effects can include scalp discomfort and facial muscle twitching during treatment. Serious side effects are rare. TMS therapy is not associated with the systemic side effects often seen with antidepressant medications.

Q5: Who is a good candidate for TMS therapy at your Fayetteville location?

A5: TMS therapy is generally recommended for adults diagnosed with major depressive disorder who have not responded adequately to one or more antidepressant medications. If you are in Fayetteville and have been struggling with depression, we encourage you to contact us for a consultation to determine if TMS therapy is the right option for you.
