Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of non-surgical treatments does Bella Luce Med Spa offer at our NW Expressway location?

A1: Bella Luce Med Spa at 3209 NW Expressway specializes in a wide range of non-surgical aesthetic treatments. These include, but are not limited to: Botox® and Dysport® injections for reducing dynamic wrinkles, dermal fillers (such as Juvederm®, Restylane®, and Volbella®) for adding volume and contouring, facial rejuvenation therapies like microneedling with radiofrequency (SkinMedica® RF), chemical peels for skin resurfacing, laser hair removal (e.g., GentleYAG®), and body contouring treatments (like Cellutone®). We also offer specialized skin care consultations and personalized skincare product recommendations.

Q2: Are the treatments at Bella Luce Med Spa safe?

A2: Safety is our paramount concern at Bella Luce Med Spa. Our experienced practitioners are thoroughly trained and certified to perform procedures using FDA-approved products and equipment. We adhere strictly to industry best practices and maintain a high standard of hygiene and sanitation. A comprehensive consultation is conducted before any treatment to ensure it is appropriate for you and to discuss potential risks and benefits. We are fully equipped to handle any concerns that may arise during your visit.

Q3: How long does a typical treatment session take?

A3: The duration of your treatment depends on the specific procedure. For example, a Botox® or Dysport® session might take only 15-30 minutes, while dermal filler treatments could range from 30 minutes to an hour. More extensive procedures like laser hair removal or a full facial treatment might require a longer appointment time. Our team will provide you with a clear timeline during your consultation at Bella Luce Med Spa.

Q4: Is there any downtime after my treatment?

A4: Downtime varies depending on the treatment performed. Some procedures, like Botox®, typically involve minimal downtime, with you able to return to your normal activities immediately, though you might experience slight redness or swelling. Dermal fillers might cause minor swelling or bruising that usually subsides within a few days. Treatments involving lasers or microneedling may require more recovery time, with redness, swelling, or peeling possible. Our staff will discuss expected recovery and aftercare instructions thoroughly before your treatment at 3209 NW Expressway.

Q5: How often should I schedule maintenance for treatments like Botox® or fillers?

A5: The longevity of results from Botox® and dermal fillers varies based on the individual and the specific product used. Generally, Botox® and Dysport® effects last around 3-4 months, while some dermal fillers can last 6 months or longer. To maintain optimal results and prolong the benefits, touch-up treatments are often scheduled every few months. Our team at Bella Luce Med Spa will advise you on the best schedule for your personal goals and lifestyle, based on your specific treatment outcomes.
