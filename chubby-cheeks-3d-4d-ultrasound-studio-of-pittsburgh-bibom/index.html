Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: When is the best time to have a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A: For optimal image quality, we recommend scheduling your 3D/4D ultrasound appointment between weeks 13 and 26 of pregnancy at our Pittsburgh location.

Q2: How long does a typical 3D/4D ultrasound appointment take at Chubby Cheeks Pittsburgh?

A: A standard 3D/4D ultrasound session at our Pittsburgh studio usually takes about 45 minutes to an hour from arrival until departure.

Q3: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for me and my baby?

A: Yes. The sound wave technology used for 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks Pittsburgh is the same as used in standard 2D prenatal care ultrasounds. It is considered safe for both mother and baby when performed by trained professionals.

Q4: Can I bring my partner and other family members to the Pittsburgh appointment?

A: Absolutely! We encourage bringing your partner and other family members to share in this special experience at our Pittsburgh studio. We provide a comfortable waiting area.

Q5: What happens if we don't get clear images during our Pittsburgh ultrasound?

A: Factors like the mother's weight, amniotic fluid levels, and baby's position can sometimes affect image clarity. We ask that you drink plenty of fluids before your Pittsburgh appointment to help optimize visibility. If clear images cannot be obtained on the first visit, we will schedule a complimentary return visit at no additional charge.
