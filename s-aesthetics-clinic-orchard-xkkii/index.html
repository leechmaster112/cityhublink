Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of aesthetic treatments are offered at S Aesthetics Clinic Orchard?

A1: S Aesthetics Clinic Orchard provides a range of professional aesthetic services, including but not limited to Botox and Dysport injections for wrinkle reduction, dermal fillers (such as hyaluronic acid) for volume restoration and contouring, chemical peels for skin rejuvenation, and other non-invasive skin care treatments tailored to enhance your natural beauty.

Q2: How long does a typical consultation at S Aesthetics Clinic Orchard take?

A2: A standard consultation at our clinic usually takes around 30 to 45 minutes. During this time, our expert practitioners will discuss your aesthetic goals, assess your skin condition, answer your questions, and recommend suitable treatment options specific to your needs.

Q3: Are the aesthetic procedures performed at S Aesthetics Clinic Orchard painful?

A3: Modern aesthetic procedures are designed to be comfortable. While some minor discomfort or a sensation of pressure might be felt during injections, our practitioners utilise advanced techniques and may apply topical anaesthesia or numbing creams to minimise any potential discomfort. Most clients report tolerable sensations throughout the treatment.

Q4: What is the recovery time like after aesthetic treatments at S Aesthetics Clinic Orchard?

A4: Recovery time varies depending on the specific treatment performed. Procedures like Botox and Dysport typically involve minimal downtime, with most clients able to return to their normal activities immediately. Dermal fillers might involve slight swelling or bruising, usually resolving within a few days. Our team will provide you with detailed post-treatment care instructions during your consultation.
