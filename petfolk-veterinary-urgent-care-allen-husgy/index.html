Core Services

Our veterinary services include:

Wellness Examinations: Regular check-ups are crucial for maintaining your pet's health. Our veterinarians conduct thorough physical examinations to assess your pet's overall condition, recommend appropriate vaccinations, and screen for potential health issues.

Dental Care: We provide comprehensive dental services, including teeth cleaning, extractions, and oral health guidance to ensure your pet's mouth remains healthy and free from disease.

Laboratory Testing: Our on-site laboratory allows for quick and accurate diagnostic testing, enabling us to provide timely results and prompt treatment decisions.

Imaging Services: We utilize advanced imaging technology, including digital radiography, to diagnose and treat various conditions effectively.

Emergency Care: Our urgent care facility is equipped to handle after-hours emergencies, providing immediate attention when your pet needs it most.
