Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is chiropractic care?

A: Chiropractic care is a healthcare profession focused on the relationship between the spine and nervous system. Our practitioners at Alpine Chiropractic use manual adjustments and other techniques to restore proper alignment of the spine, thereby removing interference to the nervous system and allowing the body to heal itself naturally.

Q2: How long does a typical chiropractic visit take?

A: Your first visit to Alpine Chiropractic typically lasts about 60-90 minutes. This allows ample time for your initial consultation, health history intake, examination, and the development of your personalized treatment plan. Subsequent adjustment visits are usually shorter, typically lasting 15-30 minutes.

Q3: Is chiropractic care safe?

A: Chiropractic care is widely recognized as a safe and effective treatment for musculoskeletal conditions, particularly those involving the spine. Our practitioners at Alpine Chiropractic are highly trained professionals who prioritize patient safety. We utilize gentle, evidence-based techniques specifically tailored to your condition and individual needs.

Q4: Do I need a referral to see a chiropractor?

A: In Virginia Beach, you can typically schedule an appointment directly with a chiropractor like those at Alpine Chiropractic without needing a referral from a medical doctor. However, we encourage patients to discuss chiropractic care with their primary care physician if they have underlying health conditions.

Q5: How soon can I expect to feel better after starting chiropractic care?

A: The timeline for feeling better varies depending on the nature and duration of your condition, as well as your body's response to treatment. Many of our Virginia Beach patients report a reduction in pain and improved mobility within a few days to a week of beginning their care plan. Consistency with your treatment plan is key to achieving lasting results.
