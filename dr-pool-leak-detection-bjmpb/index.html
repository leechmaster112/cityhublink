Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How does Dr. Pool Leak Detection find leaks in Orlando pools?

We use advanced electronic detection methods, primarily the electronic pressure gauge (EPG) system. This technology measures the flow of water through the return line while the pool pump is running. By pressurizing the system and carefully monitoring pressure changes, our technicians can accurately locate the exact source of the leak without damaging your pool structure.

Q2: Is my pool safe during the leak detection process?

Absolutely. Our non-invasive techniques are designed to be safe for all types of pools and do not require draining the water or entering the pool structure. We follow strict safety protocols to ensure a safe environment for our technicians and your property.

Q3: How long does a typical leak detection job take in Orlando?

The duration depends on the pool size, complexity, and the location of the leak. Generally, a thorough leak detection inspection for an average-sized pool in the Orlando area can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. We always provide a clear timeline during our initial assessment.

Q4: What is the cost of leak detection for a pool in Orlando?

Leak detection is typically a one-time diagnostic fee focused solely on finding the leak. We provide a clear, upfront cost estimate before starting any work. Repair costs will vary based on the location and severity of the leak found, which we will discuss thoroughly once the source is identified.

Q5: Can Dr. Pool Leak Detection help prevent future pool leaks in Orlando?

While we specialize in detection, we can provide valuable advice on preventative maintenance based on our experience with common Orlando pool issues. Regularly monitoring water levels and addressing any slight drops promptly can help prevent minor leaks from becoming major problems. We recommend periodic inspections, especially during seasonal changes common in Orlando.
