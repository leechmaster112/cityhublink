Water Heaters Today

Water Heaters Today is a specialized, family-owned and operated business dedicated to providing expert water heater services throughout the Las Vegas Valley. With a focus solely on water heaters, their team offers professional installation, repair, and maintenance for both residential and commercial clients. They serve a wide range of communities, including Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and Boulder City, as well as surrounding areas like Summerlin, Spring Valley, and Paradise. The company handles all types of water heater issues, from emergency repairs to complete system replacements. Their technicians are skilled in diagnosing and fixing common problems such as a lack of hot water, leaking tanks, or faulty components on all makes and models. Understanding the urgency of these situations, they offer 24/7 emergency services to ensure clients are not left without hot water for long. In addition to repairs, Water Heaters Today specializes in the installation of new water heaters. They work with various types of systems, including traditional gas and electric tank models as well as modern, energy-efficient tankless water heaters. Their experts help customers choose the best unit for their specific needs and ensure every installation is performed safely and up to code. They also provide routine maintenance, like tank flushing, which is crucial for extending the lifespan of a water heater in areas with hard water like Las Vegas.
