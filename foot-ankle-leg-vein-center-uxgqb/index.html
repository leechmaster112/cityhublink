Foot, Ankle & Leg Vein Center

Foot, Ankle & Leg Vein Center is a trusted podiatry clinic in Boca Raton, FL, dedicated to advanced care for foot, ankle, and leg conditions. Our board-certified podiatrists, Dr. Jodi Schoenhaus, DPM, and Dr. Jason Gold, DPM, provide personalized treatments for issues such as heel pain, plantar fasciitis, bunions, Achilles tendonitis, diabetic foot concerns, arthritis, warts, nail fungus, sprains, and strains.
