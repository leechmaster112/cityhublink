### Frequently Asked Questions

What is pressure washing?

Pressure washing is a cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to remove dirt, mold, mildew, and other debris from surfaces. It is an effective way to restore the appearance of your property’s exterior.

What surfaces do you clean?

We clean a variety of surfaces, including siding, brick, stucco, driveways, sidewalks, decks, patios, gutters, and more. Our team can handle both residential and commercial properties.

How long does a typical pressure washing job take?

The duration of a pressure washing job depends on the size of the area and the level of dirt or buildup. A typical residential job can take anywhere from 1 to 4 hours, while larger commercial projects may take longer.

Do you offer any guarantees on your work?

Yes, we stand behind the quality of our work and offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with our service, we will return to address any concerns at no additional charge.

Is your team insured and experienced?

Absolutely. Our team is fully insured and trained professionals with years of experience in pressure washing and exterior cleaning. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities.
