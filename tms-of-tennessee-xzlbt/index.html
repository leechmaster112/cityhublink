What is NeuroStar TMS Therapy?

NeuroStar TMS Therapy is an advanced, non-invasive treatment for depression. It uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate specific areas of the brain responsible for mood regulation, without the need for surgery, anesthesia, or electrical currents passing through the scalp. This therapy is FDA-approved and is particularly beneficial for individuals who have not found sufficient relief from traditional antidepressant medications.

How Does NeuroStar TMS Therapy Work?

During a NeuroStar TMS session, our trained technicians will comfortably position you in the TMS chair. The NeuroStar device generates a magnetic field that passes through the skull and induces a current in a targeted region of your brain – typically the left prefrontal cortex, which is associated with mood control. You will hear a clicking sound and feel a tapping sensation on your head during the procedure. Each session typically lasts about 20-40 minutes and is performed while you are awake and alert, with no sedation required. The treatment is well-tolerated, and most patients report minimal, if any, discomfort.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about TMS of Tennessee Franklin

Question 1: Does TMS therapy require anesthesia?

Answer: No, NeuroStar TMS Therapy does not require anesthesia or sedation. Patients remain awake and alert during the entire treatment session.

Question 2: How long does a typical TMS session last?

Answer: A standard NeuroStar TMS session typically lasts between 20 to 40 minutes. Most patients find they can return to their normal daily activities immediately after a session.

Question 3: Is TMS therapy covered by insurance?

Answer: Insurance coverage for TMS therapy varies by plan and provider. We recommend contacting your insurance provider directly to inquire about coverage. Our team at TMS of Tennessee Franklin can also assist you in understanding your benefits and exploring payment options.

Question 4: How many TMS sessions are typically needed?

Answer: Most patients undergo approximately 20-30 sessions, typically scheduled five days a week for about four to six weeks. Our team will develop a personalized treatment plan based on your specific needs.

Question 5: Are there any side effects associated with TMS therapy?

Answer: NeuroStar TMS Therapy is generally well-tolerated. The most common side effects are mild headache or scalp discomfort, which typically diminish as treatment progresses. There is no risk of seizure associated with NeuroStar TMS due to its built-in safety mechanisms.
