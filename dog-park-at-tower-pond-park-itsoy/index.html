Dog Park at Tower Pond Park

The Dog Park at Tower Pond Park is one of Albuquerque's fenced off-leash areas, located on the city's west side with separate sections for large and small dogs. Maintained by the city's parks department, it offers a secure spot for daily exercise and socializing.
