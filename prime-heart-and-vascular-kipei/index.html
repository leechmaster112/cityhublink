Cardiovascular Care at Our Allen Center

Cardiovascular diseases encompass a wide range of conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels. At our Allen facility, we offer thorough evaluations and management strategies for conditions such as coronary artery disease, heart rhythm problems (arrhythmias), heart valve issues, heart failure, and peripheral artery disease. Our cardiologists conduct detailed assessments, including comprehensive physical examinations, in-depth discussions about your medical history and lifestyle, and a variety of specialized tests.

Tests performed at our Allen location may include, but are not limited to:

Echocardiograms (ultrasound of the heart) Electrocardiograms (ECG or EKG) Stress testing Coronary artery calcium scoring Cardiac CT scans Cardiac MRI

These diagnostic tools allow us to precisely identify cardiovascular issues and develop tailored treatment plans specific to each patient's needs.

Vascular Care Services

Our vascular specialists focus on the health of your blood vessels, including arteries and veins outside the heart. Common conditions addressed at our Allen practice include peripheral artery disease, carotid artery disease, aortic aneurysm, varicose veins, deep vein thrombosis (DVT), and chronic venous insufficiency.

We utilize both diagnostic and therapeutic approaches. Diagnostic services might involve ultrasound examinations of the arteries and veins to assess blood flow and vessel health. Based on the findings, our vascular team may recommend lifestyle changes, medications, or minimally invasive procedures performed right here in our Allen facility.

Our Allen location offers advanced vascular interventions, including procedures like:

Atherectomy (removal of plaque) Angioplasty and stenting Varicose vein treatments (laser ablation, sclerotherapy) Thrombolysis or thrombectomy (removal of blood clots)

Whenever necessary, our team collaborates with other specialists for comprehensive patient care.

Preventive Care and Patient Education

Preventing cardiovascular problems is often more effective than treating them. We emphasize proactive health measures at our Allen practice. Our physicians work closely with patients to assess their individual risk factors for heart disease and vascular problems. This includes evaluating factors like age, family history, smoking status, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and diabetes.

We provide personalized guidance on lifestyle modifications, such as adopting a heart-healthy diet, engaging in regular physical activity, managing stress, and quitting smoking. Patient education is a cornerstone of our approach at 981 State Hwy 121 #3100, Allen, TX 75013, empowering individuals with the knowledge needed to make informed decisions about their heart health.
