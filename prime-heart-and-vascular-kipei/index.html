Location

Prime Heart & Vascular, located at 981 State Hwy 121 #3100, Allen, TX 75013, is dedicated to providing expert cardiology and vascular care right here in our community. Our Allen location serves patients seeking specialized diagnosis, treatment, and management for heart and vascular conditions. We understand the importance of accessible, high-quality healthcare, and we are committed to being a trusted resource for cardiovascular health in Allen.

Cardiovascular diseases encompass a wide range of conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels. These can include coronary artery disease, heart rhythm problems (arrhythmias), heart attacks, heart failure, and various vascular issues like peripheral artery disease and aneurysms. At our Allen practice, we focus on the full spectrum of heart and vascular care, tailored to meet the specific needs of our patients living locally.

Our team of experienced cardiologists and vascular specialists is equipped to handle everything from routine check-ups and preventative screenings to the diagnosis and management of complex cardiovascular conditions. We utilize state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and evidence-based treatment protocols to ensure the highest standard of care for our patients residing in Allen and the surrounding immediate area.

Comprehensive Cardiology Services at our Allen location include:

Cardiac Risk Assessment: Evaluating your risk for heart disease through detailed history, physical examination, and risk factor analysis. Electrocardiogram (ECG/EKG): A fundamental test that records the electrical activity of the heart to detect arrhythmias, heart attacks, and other abnormalities. Echocardiogram: Utilizing ultrasound technology to create moving images of the heart, allowing us to assess its structure and function. Stress Testing: Monitoring heart function during physical exertion to identify potential problems not apparent at rest. Cardiac Catheterization & Angiography: Procedures used to diagnose and treat coronary artery disease and other heart conditions. Management of Heart Failure: Developing personalized plans to manage symptoms and improve quality of life for patients with heart failure. Arrhythmia Management: Diagnosing and treating irregular heartbeats, including the possibility of implanting devices like pacemakers or defibrillators.

Vascular Care Services available at our Allen facility address issues related to the blood vessels outside the heart. These services include:

Diagnosis of Vascular Diseases: Identifying conditions such as peripheral artery disease, carotid artery disease, and chronic venous insufficiency. Non-Invasive Vascular Testing: Utilizing techniques like Doppler ultrasound and vascular imaging to assess blood flow and vessel health. Management of Venous Conditions: Providing care for varicose veins, spider veins, and other venous disorders. Interventional Procedures: Offering minimally invasive treatments for vascular issues when appropriate.

We believe that proactive cardiovascular care is essential for maintaining overall health and well-being. Our Allen practice emphasizes patient education, empowering individuals to understand their heart health and make informed decisions about their lifestyle and treatment options.
