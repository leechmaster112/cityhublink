FAQ: Q: What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing for my home's exterior? A: Pressure washing uses high-force water streams to blast away stubborn dirt, mold, and mildew, ideal for hard surfaces like concrete driveways. Soft washing, on the other hand, utilizes low-pressure water combined with specialized cleaning solutions to gently but effectively remove organic growth and stains from more delicate surfaces like vinyl siding and roof shingles, preventing damage. Q: Will pressure washing damage my house siding? A: Not with OC Surface Care. We carefully assess your siding material and use the appropriate pressure and technique. For most siding, especially vinyl or painted surfaces, we recommend and utilize our soft washing method to ensure a thorough clean without any risk of damage. Q: How long does a typical pressure washing or soft washing service take? A: The duration varies depending on the size of your property and the specific areas being cleaned. However, most residential exterior cleaning projects are completed within a few hours, allowing you to enjoy your refreshed home without significant disruption. Q: Can you clean my concrete driveway and patio? A: Absolutely. Concrete surfaces are perfect candidates for our professional pressure washing services. We can effectively remove oil stains, dirt buildup, and algae to restore the clean appearance of your driveway and patio areas. Q: What kind of cleaning solutions do you use for soft washing? A: We use eco-friendly and biodegradable cleaning solutions specifically formulated to break down organic matter like mold, mildew, algae, and other stubborn stains. These solutions are safe for your landscaping and the environment.
