Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of dental services do you offer at your Saratoga Springs location?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services including preventative care (cleanings, exams, X-rays), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures), endodontics (root canals), periodontics (gum disease treatment), and cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers).

Q: Are payment plans available for dental services in Saratoga Springs?

Yes, we understand that dental care can be an investment, and we offer various payment options to help make treatment accessible. We can discuss financing plans and insurance options during your visit to help manage the cost of your care.

Q: How often should I schedule dental checkups in Saratoga Springs?

We generally recommend scheduling regular dental checkups and cleanings every six months for most patients in our community. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs, which our dentist will determine during your examination.

Q: Do you accept dental insurance for patients from Saratoga Springs?

Yes, Stonehaven Dental accepts most major dental insurance plans serving the Saratoga Springs area. We will work with your insurance provider to maximize your benefits and help you understand your coverage.

Q: What should I bring to my first dental appointment in Saratoga Springs?

For your first visit, please bring your dental insurance card, a list of any medications you take, your photo ID, and your previous dental records if you have them. We will also ask you to complete some new patient forms beforehand on our website or in the office.
