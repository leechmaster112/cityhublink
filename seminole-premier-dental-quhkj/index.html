Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: What types of dental insurance do you accept?

A: Seminole Premier Dental is committed to working with most major dental insurance plans. We recommend contacting our office before your visit to confirm your specific coverage details and ensure a smooth claims process.

Q2: Do you offer payment plans or financing?

A: Yes, we understand financial considerations are important. We offer flexible payment options and can discuss financing arrangements to help make quality dental care accessible. Please inquire during your consultation.

Q3: How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

A: Generally, we recommend bi-annual dental check-ups and cleanings for most patients. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs, so our team will advise you accordingly during your examination.

Q4: What should I do in case of a dental emergency outside regular hours?

A: While we strive to accommodate emergencies when possible during our hours, for after-hours situations, please contact our office during business hours, and we will provide guidance or direct you to appropriate care.

Q5: Do you provide dental care for children?

A: Absolutely! Our team is experienced in providing gentle and effective dental care for children of all ages. We focus on creating a positive experience to help establish lifelong good oral hygiene habits.
