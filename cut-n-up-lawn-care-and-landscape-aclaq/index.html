Seasonal Considerations for Your Alabaster Lawn

The climate in Alabaster provides specific challenges and opportunities for lawn care throughout the year. Our services are adapted to these seasonal variations to keep your property looking its best:

* Spring: Spring is a crucial time for lawn recovery after winter dormancy. We focus on reseeding bare spots, fertilizing to promote vigorous growth, and controlling weeds before they become established. Thorough lawn aeration may also be recommended to improve root development and water absorption.

* Summer: Consistent, professional lawn care is essential during the hot Alabama months. Our services ensure your lawn receives adequate attention to combat heat stress and maintain its color and density. Regular mowing and weed control are key priorities.

* Fall: Fall is ideal for lawn preparation for the upcoming winter. We focus on fertilization to strengthen roots, addressing any remaining weed issues, and potentially overseeding cool-season grass varieties to jumpstart spring growth.

* Winter: While grass growth slows, we remain available for essential services like clearing snow and ice from walkways and driveways (subject to service availability and conditions) and addressing any winter damage.
