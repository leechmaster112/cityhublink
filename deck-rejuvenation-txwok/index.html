Our Core Service: The Deck Rejuvenation Process

At Deck Rejuvenation, our flagship offering is a meticulous process designed to breathe new life into aged, damaged, or neglected timber decks. This isn't just a wash and a wax; it's a multi-stage treatment that restores the deck's integrity and appearance:

1. Thorough Deck Assessment:

We begin with a detailed inspection of your deck. Our experienced team identifies the specific type of timber, assesses the extent of wear and tear, checks for structural issues, and determines the most appropriate course of action. This initial evaluation is crucial for tailoring our treatment to your deck's unique needs.

2. Deep Cleaning & Stain Removal:

Dirt, grime, moss, algae, and old, faded stains can significantly detract from your deck's appearance and hinder the absorption of new treatments. We utilise effective yet environmentally conscious cleaning methods to thoroughly remove these contaminants, leaving your deck clean and primed for rejuvenation.

3. Deck Repair & Sanding (Where Necessary):

Small cracks, splinters, and minor damage are common issues that can compromise a deck's appearance and structural integrity. Our skilled team performs necessary repairs and, where appropriate, light sanding to smooth surfaces and prepare them for the next stages.

4. Wood Brightening & Staining:

This is the heart of our rejuvenation process. We apply a wood brightening agent to lift the natural fibres of the timber, enhancing its texture and colour. Following this, we carefully apply a premium quality stain designed to protect the wood from UV damage, moisture, and general wear, while providing a beautiful, long-lasting colour finish that complements your home's aesthetic.

5. Protective Sealant Application:

To lock in the colour, protect against future weathering, and maintain the deck's structural soundness, we apply a durable, weather-resistant sealant. This final layer ensures your investment is protected and extends the time between required maintenance treatments.
