Frequently Asked Questions

What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Riverview location?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic at 6332 US-301, Riverview, FL 33578, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a specialized coil is placed gently against your head. The treatment is administered by our trained staff in a relaxing environment, and you can return to your daily activities immediately afterward.

Who is a good candidate for TMS treatment at your center?

A good candidate for TMS at our Riverview clinic is typically an adult who has been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and has not experienced satisfactory improvement from one or more antidepressant medications. Dr. Melissa Fickey will conduct a comprehensive evaluation at our location to determine if TMS is the right treatment option for you.
