Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of routine veterinary care do you offer at your Oviedo location?

We provide comprehensive routine care including annual physical examinations, core vaccinations, parasite prevention (fleas, ticks, heartworm), dental cleanings, microchip registration and updates, and wellness planning tailored to your pet's age, breed, and lifestyle.

2. Do you offer after-hours emergency veterinary care for pets in Oviedo?

While we focus on providing excellent urgent care during our regular business hours, we can assist with after-hours emergencies. We can often accommodate walk-in urgent cases outside of regular hours or provide guidance on contacting emergency veterinary services if needed.

3. What payment methods do you accept for services at your Oviedo clinic?

We accept various payment methods including cash, major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), and pet insurance. Our front desk staff can provide details on accepted payment methods and help coordinate with insurance providers.

4. Can I schedule appointments for routine care at your Oviedo location?

Yes, we strongly recommend scheduling appointments for routine care to ensure the best experience for both you and your pet. Walk-ins are welcome when space is available, but calling ahead to schedule a specific time for wellness visits, vaccinations, or dental care is appreciated.

5. What urgent care services are available for pets at your Oviedo facility?

Our urgent care services include but are not limited to treating minor injuries, managing sudden illnesses, addressing digestive upsets, handling minor wounds or lacerations, managing ear infections, treating eye problems, and providing care for pets showing signs of distress outside of regular business hours. Our experienced team is equipped to handle a wide range of urgent pet health concerns.
