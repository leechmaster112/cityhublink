Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of surgeries does Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit perform?

A: Our facility specializes in a comprehensive range of surgical procedures, including routine spay and neuter surgeries, soft tissue surgery (such as tumor removals and foreign body extraction), orthopedic procedures (like fracture repair), and ophthalmic surgeries. We focus on providing expert surgical care for a variety of conditions affecting pets.

Q2: How do I prepare my pet for surgery at Dr. Kelly's?

A: Preparation instructions will be provided by our team after the surgical consultation. Generally, this includes fast-fasting your pet overnight before the procedure, withholding any medications not deemed essential by us, and completing any required pre-anesthetic blood work. Our staff will provide detailed instructions tailored to your pet's specific surgery.

Q3: What is the process for scheduling a surgical consultation?

A: You can schedule a surgical consultation by calling our office at (602) 641-4861. During the consultation, our surgeon will examine your pet, discuss the recommended procedure, potential risks, recovery process, and costs. You can also reach us through our website at https://www.drkellysvet.com/.

Q4: What should I expect during the post-operative care period?

A: Post-operative care instructions will be provided after your pet's surgery. These typically include information on pain management, activity restrictions, wound care, and follow-up appointments. We will outline a clear recovery plan designed to ensure your pet's comfortable and safe healing process at home.

Q5: Does Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit handle emergency surgical cases?

A: While we focus primarily on scheduled surgical procedures, we are prepared to handle certain emergency surgical cases that require immediate attention. If your pet experiences a sudden, life-threatening condition necessitating emergency surgery, please call our office at (602) 641-4861 immediately. Our team will assess the situation and determine the appropriate course of action.
