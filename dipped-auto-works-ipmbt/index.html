Q4: What's the difference between a car wrap and paint protection film?

A4: While both use similar vinyl film technology, their purposes differ. A car wrap (or vinyl wrap) is primarily for aesthetic enhancement, changing the vehicle's color or adding designs. A Paint Protection Film (PPF) is functional, acting as a clear shield to protect the original paint from damage. PPF is typically applied to vulnerable front sections, while wraps can cover the entire vehicle. You can even combine both – using PPF underneath a wrap for maximum protection.
