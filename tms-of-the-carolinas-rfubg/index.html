Location

At our Raleigh practice, we understand that your journey to better mental health requires specialized care. TMS therapy is a cutting-edge treatment that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, specifically targeting areas associated with mood regulation. This advanced technique is designed to help improve symptoms of depression and potentially other conditions, offering a drug-free alternative for many.

Our team at TMS of the Carolinas is committed to delivering personalized care. We begin with a thorough evaluation to determine if TMS is an appropriate treatment option for you. The TMS procedure itself is typically well-tolerated and does not require sedation. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Does TMS therapy require anesthesia or medication? A: No, TMS therapy is typically performed without any sedation or anesthesia. You will remain awake and alert during your sessions at our Raleigh location.

Q2: How long does a typical TMS session take? A: A standard TMS session at our Raleigh practice usually lasts between 25 to 45 minutes. Most patients find they can resume their normal activities immediately after a session.

Q3: Is TMS therapy covered by insurance? A: Insurance coverage for TMS therapy varies. We recommend contacting your insurance provider directly or calling our Raleigh office at (984) 375-3133 to discuss your specific benefits.

Q4: How many TMS sessions will I need? A: The number of sessions required varies depending on individual needs. Most patients at our Raleigh location receive sessions five days a week for about four to six weeks.

Q5: What are the potential side effects of TMS therapy? A: TMS is generally well-tolerated. The most common side effects are mild headache or scalp discomfort during treatment, which our team at our Raleigh location works to minimize.
