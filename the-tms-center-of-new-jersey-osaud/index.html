The TMS Center of New Jersey

The TMS Center of New Jersey is a specialized mental health clinic in Tinton Falls offering NeuroStar® Advanced Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy) therapy for depression, OCD, and anxiety. This FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment uses targeted magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, providing relief for patients who have not responded to medication or psychotherapy. Treatments are performed while patients remain awake, with no anesthesia or downtime, allowing a quick return to daily activities. Led by experienced clinicians, the center delivers personalized care focused on safe, effective, drug-free mental health treatment.

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. It is particularly effective for individuals who have not responded adequately to traditional antidepressant medications. The TMS Center of New Jersey in Tinton Falls is at the forefront of this innovative therapy, offering a safe and well-tolerated alternative for conditions such as major depressive disorder.
