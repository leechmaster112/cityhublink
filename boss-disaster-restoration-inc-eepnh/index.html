Boss Disaster Restoration Inc.

Boss Disaster Restoration Inc. is a 24/7 emergency water damage restoration and disaster recovery company based in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina. Founded in 1993, this locally owned and operated restoration specialist provides fast, reliable response services to homeowners and businesses throughout the Charleston Tri-County area, including Berkeley, Charleston, and Dorchester counties. Boss Disaster Restoration offers comprehensive disaster recovery solutions including water extraction and drying, flood damage restoration, fire and smoke restoration, storm damage repair, mold remediation, contents cleaning and pack-out, and structural drying services. Their certified technicians use advanced equipment to mitigate damage quickly, prevent secondary issues like mold growth, and assist customers through the insurance claims process. Open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, the company emphasizes rapid emergency response, professional workmanship, and clear communication to help clients recover from water, fire, storm, and mold emergencies with minimal disruption.
