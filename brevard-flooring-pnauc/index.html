Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: What types of flooring does Brevard Flooring install?

A1: Brevard Flooring specializes in the installation of a wide range of flooring materials, including hardwood, laminate, vinyl, tile, and carpet.

Q2: Do you offer free estimates for flooring projects?

A2: Yes, we provide complimentary estimates for all flooring projects to help you plan your budget effectively.

Q3: How long does a typical flooring installation take?

A3: The duration of a flooring installation varies depending on the type of flooring, the size of the area, and any necessary subfloor preparation. We will provide a more precise timeline during your consultation.

Q4: Can Brevard Flooring help with subfloor repair or preparation?

A4: Absolutely. We offer subfloor assessment and necessary repairs or preparation to ensure a stable and smooth foundation for your new flooring.

Q5: What is the process for selecting and ordering flooring?

A5: We guide you through the selection process, offering expert advice. Once you've chosen your flooring, we handle the ordering and ensure timely delivery for your installation.
