Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is paint protection film and how does it work?

Paint protection film is a durable, transparent urethane layer applied to the exterior painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as a resilient barrier that absorbs impacts from road debris, rock chips, and minor scratches, preventing them from damaging the underlying factory paint. The high-quality films we install at our Henderson location even feature self-healing technology, where minor swirl marks and scratches disappear with exposure to heat.
