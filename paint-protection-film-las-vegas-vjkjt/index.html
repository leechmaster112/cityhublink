Frequently Asked Questions

What is paint protection film and how does it work?

Paint protection film is a clear, durable urethane material applied to the exterior painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as a sacrificial layer, absorbing impacts from road debris, rock chips, and minor abrasions to prevent damage to the underlying factory paint. The film is self-healing, meaning minor swirl marks and scratches disappear with heat, keeping your vehicle's appearance flawless.

How do I care for my vehicle after the film is applied at your shop?

After installation at our Henderson facility, we provide detailed care instructions. Generally, you should wait at least 24 hours before washing the vehicle and one week before using a high-pressure washer. We recommend using gentle, ammonia-free cleaners and avoiding abrasive waxes or polishes on the film. The film is very low maintenance and designed for long-term durability.
