Frequently Asked Questions

What is paint protection film and how does it protect my car?

Paint protection film is a durable, transparent urethane layer applied to the exterior painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as an invisible shield, protecting the factory paint from rock chips, bug acids, road salt, minor scratches, and UV damage. This helps preserve the resale value and appearance of your car.

How do I care for my vehicle after the film is applied at your shop?

After installation, we provide you with specific care instructions. Generally, we recommend waiting at least one week before washing your car to allow the film to fully cure. After that, you can wash it normally using a mild automotive soap. We also advise against using abrasive cleaners or automatic car washes with harsh brushes.
