Our Services in Boston

As specialists in criminal defense, our services encompass a wide range of legal issues faced by individuals within the Boston community:

DUI/DWI Defense: We handle cases involving driving under the influence, from first-time offenses to repeat violations, challenging breathalyzer results, field sobriety tests, and any contributing factors. Misdemeanor Crimes: Our firm represents clients charged with offenses such as assault and battery, disorderly conduct, drug possession, theft, and other minor crimes that can nonetheless have significant consequences. Felony Defense: We provide robust representation for serious crimes including burglary, robbery, drug trafficking, firearms offenses, white-collar crimes, and violent felonies, utilizing all available legal defenses and procedural tools.
