Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how does it work at your South Tampa location?

TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our clinic at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106, we use a specialized device to deliver these focused pulses to the prefrontal cortex, an area of the brain involved in mood regulation. This stimulation helps to activate and restore normal neural activity, which can lead to a significant improvement in symptoms of depression.

2. Who is a candidate for TMS treatment at your wellness center?

Ideal candidates for TMS at our South Tampa location are adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication. A consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our office is essential to determine if TMS is the right treatment option for your specific situation.

3. What does a typical TMS treatment session involve at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106?

Each session at our Tampa clinic is designed for your comfort. You will be seated in a relaxing treatment chair. After an initial mapping session to determine the correct placement and dosage of magnetic energy, each subsequent treatment lasts approximately 18-20 minutes. You remain awake and alert throughout the process and can resume your normal daily activities immediately afterward, such as driving yourself home or back to work.
