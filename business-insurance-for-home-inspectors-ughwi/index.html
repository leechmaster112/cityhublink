Business Insurance For Home Inspectors

Home inspections move fast—tight deadlines, nervous buyers, demanding agents, and properties that don’t always match the listing photos. Nationwide Business Insurance offers home inspector insurance built specifically for the way you work, helping protect your business when a missed defect, property damage, or trip-and-fall turns into a claim. Whether you focus on residential homes, investment properties, or light commercial inspections, Nationwide Business Insurance helps you meet lender and broker requirements, satisfy certificate requests, and keep your schedule full. With industry-focused risk control resources and responsive claims support, you can show up to every inspection knowing your work, your gear, and your reputation are backed by coverage designed for home inspectors—not just generic business policies.
