FAQ

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables office?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our Coral Gables location at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, patients sit comfortably in a chair while a magnetic coil is placed gently against their scalp. Treatment sessions typically last around 20 minutes, and patients can immediately resume their normal daily activities afterward.

2. Who is a good candidate for TMS treatment at your clinic?

Ideal candidates for TMS at our clinic are adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication. A consultation with Dr. Fickey at our Coral Gables office is necessary to determine if TMS is the right treatment option for you.

3. What other services does Dr. Melissa Fickey provide at this location?

In addition to TMS therapy, Dr. Fickey provides comprehensive psychiatric care, including detailed diagnostic evaluations, personalized medication management, and individual psychotherapy sessions. All services are conducted at our clinic located at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, Coral Gables, FL 33134.

4. How do I schedule an appointment at the Coral Gables wellness center?

Scheduling an appointment is simple. You can call our office directly at (305) 686-5150 to speak with our staff. We will guide you through the process and set up your initial consultation at our Suite 711 location.

5. Do you accept insurance for treatments at this location?

Our office works with many major insurance providers for TMS therapy and other psychiatric services. Our administrative team at the Coral Gables clinic will verify your benefits and provide a clear explanation of coverage and any out-of-pocket costs before you begin your treatment.
