Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What specific vein conditions do you treat at your Union location?

We specialize in treating spider veins, varicose veins, chronic venous insufficiency, superficial venous reflux disease, and other vascular conditions affecting the legs and feet.

Q2: How long does recovery take after vein treatment at your Union center?

Recovery time varies depending on the procedure performed. Most patients can return to normal activities within a few days, with full recovery typically completed within 1-2 weeks.

Q3: Do you accept insurance for vein treatment services?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. Our staff will verify your coverage and discuss any potential out-of-pocket costs during your consultation.

Q4: What is the success rate for your vein treatments?

Our treatments have a high success rate, with most patients experiencing significant improvement in symptoms and appearance. Individual results may vary.

Q5: How often should I get check-ups for vein health at your Union center?

We recommend annual check-ups for vein health, or more frequently if you have existing venous conditions or risk factors.
