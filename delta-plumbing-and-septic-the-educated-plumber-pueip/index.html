Q2: How often should a septic tank be pumped for a home in McDonough?

The frequency of septic tank pumping depends on several factors specific to your McDonough home, such as the size of the tank, the number of occupants, water usage habits, and the amount of solids generated. Generally, most home septic systems require pumping every 3 to 5 years. We recommend consulting with us for a personalized pumping schedule based on your system.

Q4: What should I do if I suspect a problem with my septic system?

If you suspect an issue with your septic system in McDonough, it's best to contact us for a professional inspection. Signs of potential problems include slow drains, gurgling sounds in pipes, sewage odors indoors or near the drain field, pooling water or soggy soil over the drain field, and unusually high water bills. Early detection is key to preventing costly damage.
