Little Zoo Garden Capybara Cafe Bangkok

Little Zoo Garden Capybara cafe is a family-friendly animal cafe and mini-zoo located in Bangkok, Thailand, where visitors can interact with a variety of exotic and domestic animals in a relaxed setting, including capybaras, goats, rabbits, hedgehogs, dogs like Chihuahuas and corgis, cats, meerkats, deer, wallabies, birds such as cockatiels and cockatoos, tortoises, nutria, ducks, turtles, musk rats, bamboo rats, and more. It's designed as a petting and feeding garden at our capybara cafe, offering hands-on experiences like feeding, petting, and holding animals, making it popular for families, kids, and animal lovers. The venue combines outdoor and indoor areas with a cafe for drinks and snacks, allowing unlimited stay once inside.

Location

Welcome to Little Zoo Garden Capybara Cafe Bangkok, a unique animal cafe and mini-zoo experience located at 276 On Nut 17 Alley, Lane 6, Suan Luang, Bangkok 10250. We offer a family-friendly environment where you can enjoy delicious drinks and snacks while interacting with our adorable resident animals, including our famous capybaras. Our space is designed for unforgettable close encounters, photo opportunities, and educational fun for visitors of all ages.

For bookings and inquiries, please contact us at 092 435 1115 or visit our website at https://littlezoogarden.com to plan your visit to our animal cafe.

What are your opening hours?

Our opening hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We recommend checking our website for any special holiday schedules.

Do I need to make a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we highly recommend making a reservation by calling 092 435 1115 to guarantee your spot, especially on weekends.

What animals can we meet at your cafe?

Guests can meet and interact with our friendly capybaras, as well as a variety of other animals in our mini-zoo.

Is there an entrance fee?

Yes, we have an entrance fee which includes a drink. Please call us for the most current pricing and package details.

Is the cafe suitable for young children?

Absolutely! Our animal cafe is a family-friendly destination designed to be a safe and enjoyable experience for children and adults alike.
