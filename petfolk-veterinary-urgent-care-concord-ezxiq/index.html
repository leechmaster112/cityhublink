Location

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Concord

Welcome to Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care in Concord, your dedicated partner for comprehensive pet wellness and expert care right here in our community. Located at 2425 Herrons Nest Pl NW Suite 100, we are proud to be Petfolk's first Concord location, bringing our commitment to excellence in veterinary medicine directly to you.

Founded and led by a team of highly skilled and experienced veterinarians, our mission is simple: to provide the highest standard of care for your beloved companions. We understand that pets are integral members of the family, and we treat them with the compassion, respect, and thoroughness they deserve.

Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with modern diagnostic tools and treatment options to ensure your pet receives accurate assessments and effective care. We pride ourselves on offering a wide range of services designed to keep your furry friends healthy, happy, and thriving throughout their lives.

From routine wellness examinations and preventative care like vaccinations and parasite control to comprehensive dental services and meticulous grooming, our experienced team is here to support your pet's lifelong health journey. We also offer advanced diagnostics, including in-house laboratory testing, allowing us to quickly and accurately diagnose potential health concerns.

As part of our commitment to comprehensive care, we provide thorough surgical services, addressing everything from routine spay and neuter procedures to more complex orthopedic or soft tissue surgeries. Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care is uniquely positioned to offer urgent care services, providing prompt attention for unexpected illnesses or injuries when your regular schedule doesn't permit a visit.

Our dedicated team is focused on building strong client-pet relationships based on trust and open communication. We take the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and develop personalized treatment plans tailored specifically to your pet's unique needs.

For more information about our services or to schedule an appointment, please visit our website or contact us directly.

We primarily focus on dogs and cats, providing expert care for these common household companions.

Our regular business hours are designed to be convenient for pet owners in Concord. Please visit our website or call our office directly to find our current operating hours.
