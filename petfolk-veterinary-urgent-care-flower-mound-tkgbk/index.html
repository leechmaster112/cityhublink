Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What makes your Flower Mound clinic different from other veterinarians in the area?

Our Flower Mound location stands out through our commitment to comprehensive care with on-site diagnostics, experienced specialists, and a focus on building long-term relationships with both pet owners and their animals. We pride ourselves on providing personalized treatment plans tailored to each pet's specific needs.

Q2: Do you offer emergency services after hours?

Yes, our Flower Mound clinic offers emergency services during extended hours. While we don't have a dedicated 24/7 emergency room, our team is available to address urgent concerns after regular business hours. For true emergencies outside our operating hours, we can recommend nearby emergency facilities.

Q3: Can you perform dental procedures for pets?

Absolutely! Our Flower Mound veterinarians are fully equipped to perform comprehensive dental care including teeth cleaning, extractions, and oral surgery. Regular dental care is an essential part of maintaining your pet's overall health and preventing serious health issues.

Q4: How do I prepare for a routine check-up appointment?

For a routine wellness visit to our Flower Mound clinic, please bring your pet's complete medical history including vaccination records and any previous test results. We recommend bringing a recent stool sample if your pet has had diarrhea or unusual bowel movements. No special preparation is needed for most annual check-ups.

Q5: Do you offer pet boarding services at your Flower Mound location?

No, our Flower Mound facility specializes in veterinary care and does not offer boarding services. However, we can provide boarding information and recommendations for trusted local facilities if you need temporary housing for your pet.
