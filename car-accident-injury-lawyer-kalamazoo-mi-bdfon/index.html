How Michigan No-Fault Works After a Kalamazoo Crash Michigan uses a “no-fault” auto insurance system, which means that after most crashes, your own auto policy’s Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage is the first place to look for payment of medical bills and some wage loss, regardless of who caused the collision. Recent reforms allow drivers to choose different PIP medical limits—such as unlimited, $500,000, $250,000, or in some cases $50,000—each with different protections and risks. Because of these changes, many Kalamazoo drivers now have lower PIP limits than in the past, and insurance companies use that to minimize what they pay. When injuries are serious—such as fractures, surgeries, traumatic brain injuries, or long-term disability—you may also have a claim against the at-fault driver for pain and suffering, excess wage loss, and future medical expenses. This “third-party” claim is separate from your no-fault benefits and can be worth significantly more than the bills you see today. A knowledgeable Kalamazoo car accident attorney can review your coverage, the police report, and your medical records to identify every possible claim. What To Do After a Car Accident in Kalamazoo If you were hurt in a crash anywhere in Kalamazoo or Kalamazoo County, these steps can protect both your health and your legal rights: Get medical treatment immediately. Go to the ER, urgent care, or your doctor and follow up with any specialists they recommend. Gaps in treatment are one of the first things insurers use to deny claims. Report the crash. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Kalamazoo?

After ensuring safety, call 911 if needed, exchange information with other drivers, document the scene (photos, witness contact info), and seek medical attention even for minor injuries.

Q: How long do I have to file a claim after my Kalamazoo car accident?

In Michigan, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims, including car accidents, is typically 2 years from the date of the accident. It's crucial to consult a lawyer promptly.

Q: What types of compensation can I seek from a Kalamazoo car accident injury lawyer?

We can help you pursue compensation for medical bills, lost wages, property damage, pain and suffering, emotional distress, and other related losses.

Q: Do I have to pay legal fees if I don't win my Kalamazoo case?

Most car accident injury cases we handle operate on a contingency fee basis. This means you only pay attorney's fees if we successfully settle or win your case.

Q: Can you help me with a Kalamazoo car accident case where the other driver has no insurance?

Yes, we can explore options such as filing a claim under your own uninsured motorist coverage or seeking compensation from other potentially liable parties.
