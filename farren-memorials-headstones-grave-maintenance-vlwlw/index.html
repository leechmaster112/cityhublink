Location

Farren Memorials - Headstones & Grave Maintenance

Located at 5 Carleton Road, Marino, Dublin 3 (D03 DA58), Farren Memorials has been a trusted name in providing respectful and professional memorial services to the local community for generations. As a family-owned and operated business, we take pride in our deep-rooted connection to the Marino area and our commitment to honouring the memory of loved ones with dignity and care.

Our primary focus at Farren Memorials is the provision of high-quality headstones and the meticulous maintenance of gravesites. We understand that losing a loved one is a deeply personal and often difficult time, and we strive to make the process of selecting and caring for a memorial as smooth and respectful as possible.

When it comes to headstones, we offer a wide range of options to suit different tastes, budgets, and memorial preferences. From traditional granite markers to more contemporary designs, we work closely with families to find the perfect tribute that honours their individual wishes and provides a lasting resting place. Our team at our Marino location ensures that every headstone is sourced, selected, and placed with the utmost care and precision.

Furthermore, Farren Memorials provides essential grave maintenance services for families residing locally. We understand that maintaining a grave can be a challenging task for some, and our team is here to assist. Our maintenance services include regular grass cutting, flower arrangement (if desired), and general upkeep to ensure the grave site remains neat and respectful. This allows families to remember their loved ones without the worry of upkeep.

Operating from our base at 5 Carleton Road, Marino, we are dedicated to serving the residents of Dublin 3 and the immediate surrounding areas with personalised and reliable service. Our family-oriented approach means you can trust us with a task that is both personal and significant.

Whether you are looking to purchase a new headstone, select an existing one, or require ongoing grave maintenance, Farren Memorials is your dedicated partner. We combine professionalism with a deep sense of respect for the families we serve.

For more information about our headstone selection or grave maintenance services, please visit our website or contact us directly.

A2: You can book our grave maintenance services at any time. We recommend contacting us well in advance if you have specific dates in mind for flower arrangements or if you require maintenance around sensitive dates like anniversaries.

Q3: Do you provide headstone cleaning services?

A3: Yes, we offer headstone cleaning services for clients residing locally. Our team ensures your memorial is cleaned thoroughly and respectfully maintained.

A4: Absolutely. Our showroom at 5 Carleton Road, Marino displays a selection of our headstone designs and memorials. You are welcome to visit us during our operating hours to view our products and discuss your options.

Q5: What is included in your grave maintenance package?

A5: Our standard grave maintenance package includes regular grass cutting, trimming of surrounding plants, and general site upkeep. We can customise the package based on your specific requirements.
