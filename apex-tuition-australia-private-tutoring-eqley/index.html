Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects do you tutor?

We offer comprehensive tutoring across a wide range of subjects, including Mathematics (from basic numeracy to advanced calculus), English, Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics), Humanities (History, Geography, Economics), Languages (including preparation for HSC/ATAR), and preparation for standardised tests like the HSC,ATAR, and university entrance exams.

How do I book a session?

Booking is simple! You can contact us via phone at 0405 532 487 or reach out through our website. We'll discuss your requirements, find a suitable tutor, and arrange a convenient time for your sessions, whether in-home or online.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Absolutely! We believe in finding the right tutor-student match. We recommend a trial session to ensure compatibility and allow you to experience our teaching style before committing. This helps us tailor the learning experience from the very beginning.

How qualified are your tutors?

Our tutors are highly qualified professionals, typically holding tertiary qualifications in their respective subject areas, many with teaching experience at school or university levels. They undergo a thorough screening process to ensure they are the best match for our students' needs.

Can I choose my tutor?

Yes, we encourage students to have a say in tutor selection. We provide tutor profiles outlining their specialisations and experience, allowing you to make an informed choice. We also facilitate meetings between students and potential tutors to ensure the best possible match.
