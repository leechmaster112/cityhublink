Location

The SoundLab Music School & Store, located right here in the heart of St Kilda, under the iconic Astor Theatre, is your premier destination for musical discovery and development. We are dedicated to helping students of all ages and abilities turn up their musical ability, offering a comprehensive range of services right from our St Kilda location at 1 Chapel St.

At The SoundLab, our mission is simple: to nurture talent and foster a love for music within our local community. We provide expert music tuition in a variety of disciplines, including guitar, bass, drums, keyboard, vocals, and more. Our experienced instructors are passionate musicians committed to delivering high-quality, engaging lessons tailored to individual needs and goals. Whether you're a complete beginner picking up an instrument for the first time or an experienced player looking to refine your skills, our dedicated team at 1 Chapel St is here to support your musical journey.

Beyond our music school offerings, The SoundLab functions as a full-service music store. We pride ourselves on offering a wide selection of quality instruments and audio equipment suitable for various genres and skill levels. From entry-level guitars and keyboards to professional-grade drum kits and amplifiers, we have everything you need to start or enhance your musical pursuits. Our knowledgeable staff can provide expert advice on choosing the right gear and assist with any technical questions you might have.

Our store also serves as a hub for ongoing musical exploration. We offer instrument repairs and maintenance services to keep your gear sounding its best. We stay current with the latest trends and technologies in music production, performance, and sound engineering, ensuring our store reflects the dynamic nature of the musical world. All our services, from lessons to instrument sales and repairs, are conveniently accessed from our single location at 1 Chapel St, St Kilda VIC 3182.

Discover your sound and elevate your musical journey with The SoundLab Music School & Store. Visit us at https://thesoundlab.com.au/ or call us at +61 3 9510 4455 to learn more about our classes or browse our extensive inventory.
