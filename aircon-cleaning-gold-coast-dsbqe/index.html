Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get my aircon cleaned in New York?

A: For most residential units in New York, we recommend professional cleaning at least once a year. However, if you have allergies, live in a particularly dusty area, or notice reduced airflow or strange odors, more frequent cleaning may be beneficial.

Q2: What does your aircon cleaning process involve?

A: Our process typically includes a visual inspection, cleaning of internal components like the evaporator and condenser coils, drain lines, fan blades, and ductwork (if applicable), and a final thorough check to ensure optimal performance and cleanliness.

Q3: How long does an average aircon cleaning take?

A: The duration depends on the size of the unit, its condition, and access. A standard residential unit cleaning usually takes between 1 to 3 hours. We will provide a more accurate estimate once we inspect your specific system.

Q4: Do I need to be home during the cleaning service?

A: While we prefer access to the outdoor unit and indoor unit access panel, we often can complete the cleaning without requiring someone to be home. However, for your safety and to ensure smooth access, it's helpful if an adult can meet us at the appointment time.

Q5: Will cleaning my aircon help reduce my energy bills?

A: Yes, a dirty aircon works harder to achieve desired temperatures, consuming more energy. Regular cleaning helps restore your system's efficiency, potentially leading to noticeable savings on your utility bills throughout the heating and cooling seasons in New York.
