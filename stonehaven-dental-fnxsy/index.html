Stonehaven Dental

Our Spanish Fork, Utah, practice is led by two doctors and a hard-working team that loves taking care of patients and making them feel comfortable and cared for. We are conveniently located off U.S. 6, on North 920 E, near VASA Fitness. In addition to having Spanish-speaking team members, we offer numerous general dentistry and specialty services, including cosmetic dentistry, clear aligners, dental implants, sedation and orthodontics. Stonehaven Dental was founded as a single practice by Dr. Bret Tobler and his two sons nearly 30 years ago. Today, Stonehaven Dental operates multiple practices in Utah.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What services do you offer at Stonehaven Dental?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services including preventative care (cleanings, exams, X-rays), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures), and cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening).

Q: How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

Generally, we recommend scheduling regular dental check-ups every six months for optimal oral health. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs.

Q: Do you accept new patients?

Absolutely! We welcome new patients from the Spanish Fork area. You can schedule your initial consultation by contacting our office.

Q: What payment options do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans and offer various payment options for uninsured services. Please contact our office for specific details.

Q: What should I do if I have a dental emergency outside of office hours?

While we strive to accommodate all patients, please call our office during business hours. For after-hours emergencies, please contact our office and follow the instructions provided.
