Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein problems do you treat at your Fairfield location?

We specialize in treating a variety of venous conditions, including prominent varicose veins, spider veins, reticulated veins, and symptoms related to chronic venous insufficiency, such as leg pain, swelling, heaviness, and skin changes.

Q2: Are the treatments performed at your 55 Walls Drive clinic painful?

Modern vein treatments are designed to be comfortable. We use local anesthesia and advanced techniques to minimize discomfort during procedures. Most patients report only a mild sensation, if any, during their treatment at our Fairfield center.

Q3: How long does a typical vein treatment appointment take?

The duration of your visit depends on the specific procedure. Initial consultations and diagnostic ultrasound may take longer, typically 30-60 minutes. The actual treatment procedure itself usually ranges from 15 minutes to a few hours, depending on the complexity and extent of the veins being treated.

Q4: What is the recovery time after vein treatment at your clinic?

Recovery is typically quick with modern techniques. Most patients can resume light activities and daily routines immediately after their procedure at our 55 Walls Drive location. We may recommend avoiding strenuous activities and wearing compression stockings for a specified period to optimize healing.

Q5: Do I need insurance for vein treatment services?

Insurance coverage for vein treatment varies. While some procedures are medically necessary and covered by insurance, others may be considered cosmetic. We will review your benefits during your consultation at our Fairfield office and help you understand potential costs and payment options available specifically through our practice.
