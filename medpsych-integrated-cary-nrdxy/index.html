Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) Therapy at MedPsych Integrated - Cary

MedPsych Integrated - Cary offers Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy as a cutting-edge treatment option. TMS is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms associated with certain mental health conditions. This therapy is particularly effective for individuals struggling with treatment-resistant depression, anxiety disorders, and other conditions where traditional treatments may have been less successful.

Unlike some other interventions, TMS does not require surgery, anesthesia, or invasive procedures. It is generally well-tolerated, with minimal side effects, making it an attractive option for many seeking relief from debilitating mental health symptoms. Our Cary-based practitioners are experienced in administering TMS safely and effectively, tailoring the treatment protocols to each patient's specific needs.
