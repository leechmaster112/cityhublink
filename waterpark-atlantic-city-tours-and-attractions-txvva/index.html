Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are the current operating hours for Waterpark Atlantic City?

A: Our hours vary depending on the season. Please visit our official website at https://waterparkatlanticcity.org for the most up-to-date information.

Q2: How can I purchase tickets or season passes?

A: You can purchase tickets and passes online via our website https://waterparkatlanticcity.org. We also offer tickets at the gate, though online purchases are recommended for convenience and potential discounts.

Q3: Are there any age restrictions for the attractions?

A: While most of our attractions are suitable for a wide range of ages, some rides have specific height or age requirements for safety. Please check the details for each attraction or ask our staff upon arrival for guidance.

Q4: What is the best ride to experience first?

A: For first-time visitors, we recommend starting with our popular water slide for an adrenaline rush, followed by a relaxing dip in the lazy river. This allows you to experience the excitement and then unwind before exploring the rest of the park.

Q5: Can I bring my own food and beverages?

A: Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted to maintain the quality of our concessions and ensure a consistent experience for all guests. We offer a variety of food and drink options within the park.
