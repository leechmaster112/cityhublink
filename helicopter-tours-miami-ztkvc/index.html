Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Where do the helicopter tours depart from?

All helicopter tours operated by Helicopter Tours Miami depart from our base located at 25 SE 2nd Ave, Miami, FL, 33131.

Q2: How long does a typical helicopter tour flight last?

A standard introductory flight with Helicopter Tours Miami typically lasts around 20-30 minutes, offering a perfect balance of flight time and scenic views.

Q3: Are the helicopters safe?

Absolutely. Helicopter Tours Miami uses modern, well-maintained helicopters and employs highly trained, certified pilots and crew members who prioritize your safety at all times.

Q4: Can I take photos or videos during the flight?

Yes, you are welcome to take personal photos and videos during your flight. We also offer professional aerial photography and videography services for an additional fee.

Q5: What should I wear or bring on the tour?

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. Please bring sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses as flights are often conducted during daylight hours. A light jacket is also advisable as the temperature inside the helicopter can be cooler than outside.
