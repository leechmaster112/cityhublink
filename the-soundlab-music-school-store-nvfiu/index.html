Location

Welcome to The SoundLab Music School & Store, your premier destination for musical education and instrument acquisition right here in the heart of St Kilda. Located directly under the iconic Astor Theatre at 1 Chapel St, St Kilda VIC 3182, we are dedicated to nurturing musical talent and providing everything enthusiasts and professionals need to create beautiful sound.

The SoundLab Music School offers expert tuition for musicians of all ages and abilities. Our experienced instructors provide personalized lessons covering a wide range of instruments including piano, guitar, bass, drums, and various wind and brass instruments. We also cater to voice students, offering techniques for both performance and everyday singing. Whether you're embarking on your first musical journey or looking to refine your existing skills, our dedicated teachers are committed to helping you turn up your musical ability right here in St Kilda.

For those needing instruments to begin their studies, we provide a comprehensive instrument hire service. From beginner acoustic guitars and ukuleles to quality electric basses and drums, you can rent the tools you need without a large upfront investment. We understand that finding the right feel and fit is important, and our knowledgeable staff are on hand to assist you in selecting the best option for your sound.

Below the fourth floor of the Astor Theatre, our Music Store offers a vast selection of new and used instruments, amplifiers, recording equipment, and all the essential accessories musicians require. We stock popular brands and cater to various genres, ensuring you can find the specific gear needed for your musical style. From guitars and basses to keyboards, microphones, and drum kits, our store is your one-stop shop for all things musical in St Kilda.

We pride ourselves on being more than just a music school and store; we are a community hub for musicians in St Kilda. Come experience the vibrant atmosphere, connect with fellow music lovers, and let SoundLab be the place where your passion for music takes shape.
