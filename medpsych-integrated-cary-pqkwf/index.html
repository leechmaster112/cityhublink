Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is TMS therapy?

A: Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, specifically targeting areas associated with depression and other mental health conditions.

Q2: Is TMS therapy safe?

A: TMS is generally considered a safe treatment option. At our Cary location, our team follows strict protocols to ensure patient safety throughout the procedure.

Q3: How long does a typical TMS session last?

A: A standard TMS session at our Cary clinic typically lasts between 25 to 45 minutes. The exact duration can vary depending on the individual treatment plan.

Q4: Do I need medication to undergo TMS therapy?

A: TMS therapy does not require ingesting medication. However, our Cary team will discuss your overall treatment approach during your consultation.

Q5: How many TMS sessions will I need?

A: The number of TMS sessions required varies for each patient. Our team at MedPsych Integrated - Cary will determine the appropriate treatment plan based on your specific needs.
