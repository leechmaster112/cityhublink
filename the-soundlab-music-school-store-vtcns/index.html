Location

The SoundLab Music School & Store, located at 1 Chapel St, St Kilda VIC 3182, stands as a vibrant hub for musical exploration and education right under the iconic Astor Theatre. We are dedicated to nurturing musical talent and passion within our St Kilda community, offering comprehensive music education alongside a wide selection of quality instruments and accessories.

Our mission is simple: Let us help you turn up your musical ability. Whether you're a complete beginner picking up an instrument for the first time, or an experienced musician looking to refine your skills, our expert instructors provide personalised lessons tailored to your goals and learning style. We cover a diverse range of musical avenues, ensuring there's something for everyone eager to create beautiful sounds.

At The SoundLab, our St Kilda store is more than just a place to buy instruments. It's a destination for music enthusiasts seeking everything they need to practice, perform, and enjoy. We stock a comprehensive range of acoustic and electric guitars, bass guitars, keyboards, drums, ukuleles, and a variety of other popular instruments suitable for all ages and genres. Alongside the main instruments, we offer a selection of amplifiers, headphones, pickups, cables, effects pedals, sheet music, method books, and accessories to ensure your musical journey is well-equipped.

Our experienced teaching team comprises passionate musicians dedicated to fostering a positive and encouraging learning environment. We offer lessons in various popular styles including:

Guitar Lessons (Acoustic, Electric, Classical) Bass Lessons Drum Lessons Keyboard/Piano Lessons Ukulele Lessons And more, tailored to individual requests

Located in the heart of St Kilda, our store and teaching space provide a welcoming atmosphere for music lovers. We pride ourselves on being a local resource for musical excellence, supporting aspiring musicians and dedicated players alike right here in our neighbourhood.
