Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment issues do you handle for employees in Toronto?

A1: We primarily handle issues related to termination and dismissal, including unfair dismissal, wrongful termination, and constructive dismissal. We also advise on matters concerning workplace harassment, discrimination, and violations of the Ontario Employment Standards Act.

Q2: How long do I have to file a complaint after being dismissed?

A2: Time limits are crucial. Generally, for claims under the ESA (like unpaid wages), you typically have two years from the date the violation occurred. For common law claims like wrongful dismissal, the limitation period is generally two years from the date of termination. It's essential to consult us promptly after an issue arises.

Q3: Do I need a lawyer to negotiate a severance package?

A3: While negotiation is possible without legal counsel, having an employment lawyer experienced in Toronto law significantly strengthens your position. We can assess the reasonableness of any proposed severance package based on factors like your age, length of service, role, and the employer's size, ensuring you receive fair compensation.

Q4: Can you help me if my employer retaliated against me for complaining about something?

A4: Yes. Retaliation for asserting your rights is illegal under Ontario law. If you believe you were penalized, disciplined, or dismissed in response to a complaint or lawsuit, we can advise on your options and help you pursue legal recourse against both the original issue and the retaliation.
