One Emergency Dentist Liverpool

One Emergency Dentist Liverpool is a trusted dental clinic located at 656 Longmoor Lane, Fazakerley, Liverpool, L10 9LA, United Kingdom. Dedicated to delivering urgent and comprehensive dental care around the clock, the clinic operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, providing Liverpool residents with reliable access to emergency dental treatment at any time, including weekends, bank holidays, and even Christmas Day. With an outstanding 4.9-star rating on Google Reviews, One Emergency Dentist Liverpool has earned a strong reputation as one of the most dependable emergency dental practices in the city. The dental clinic is home to a team of more than 20 skilled dentists who collectively bring over 80 years of combined experience to patient care. The team includes Dr. Mustafa, an Invisalign provider specialising in smile makeovers and restorative dentistry; Dr. Amir, a cosmetic dentist experienced in veneers and teeth whitening; Dr. Wakkas, a surgical dentist proficient in oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and dental implants; and Dr. Zain, an emergency dentist focused on dental trauma and pain management. Together, they serve over 980 happy patients each month, delivering everything from urgent pain relief to complete smile transformations. One of the key advantages of this Liverpool dental clinic is its no-registration policy. Patients do not need to be registered to receive treatment, and walk-in appointments are warmly welcomed. In many cases, patients can be seen in as little as 15 minutes, which is especially important during dental emergencies when immediate attention is critical. The clinic also offers a discounted emergency consultation at just £20, reduced from the regular price of £50, making urgent dental care more accessible and affordable for everyone. The range of emergency dental services available includes treatment for toothache, broken or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, dental abscesses, trauma, wisdom tooth pain, lost implants or veneers, root canal treatment, and tooth extraction. Patients experiencing any form of dental pain can arrange a same-day appointment to receive prompt relief and expert care tailored to their specific needs. Beyond emergency services, One Emergency Dentist Liverpool offers an extensive selection of cosmetic dentistry treatments designed to enhance and restore patients' smiles. These include professional teeth whitening, custom porcelain veneers, composite bonding, and Invisalign clear aligners. The clinic's smile gallery features impressive before-and-after cases that demonstrate the quality of results achieved through treatments such as composite bonding for damaged teeth and porcelain veneers for full smile restorations. Flexible payment options, including 0% interest financing on cosmetic treatments, help patients manage costs with ease. The clinic serves a wide range of areas across Liverpool, including Aigburth, Allerton, Anfield, Childwall, Croxteth, Dingle, Everton, Fairfield, Kensington, Mossley Hill, Old Swan, Speke, Wavertree, Kirkdale, Knotty Ash, and West Derby, ensuring that residents throughout the city have convenient access to high-quality dental care. Conveniently situated just minutes from Liverpool city centre, the practice operates Monday through Sunday from 07:00 to 23:00, with emergency services available beyond standard hours. One Emergency Dentist Liverpool prides itself on creating a welcoming, judgement-free environment, particularly for nervous patients or those who have not visited a dentist in a long time. The team is committed to making dentistry more accessible, less intimidating, and as comfortable as possible for every patient. To book an appointment, patients can call 07751 757747, use the online booking system, or submit a request through the clinic's website contact form.
