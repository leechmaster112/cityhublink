Q2: How long does a typical foundation repair project take?

Q3: How can I tell if my foundation needs repair?

Signs of potential foundation problems in San Antonio homes include interior and exterior cracks in walls and floors, doors and windows that stick or no longer close properly, uneven floors, and gaps between walls and floors or ceilings. A professional inspection is essential for an accurate diagnosis.
