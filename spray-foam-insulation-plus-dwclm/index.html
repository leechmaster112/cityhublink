Our Core Service: Spray Foam Insulation

At Spray Foam Insulation Plus, our expertise lies in the precise application of two-component polyurethane spray foam. This material expands rapidly upon contact with air, filling every crack, crevice, and cavity within walls, attics, crawl spaces, and other designated areas. This comprehensive sealing action creates an airtight barrier, significantly reducing drafts, minimizing energy loss, and preventing the infiltration of dust, pollen, and other airborne particles.

There are two main types of spray foam insulation we utilize:

1. Open-Cell Spray Foam: This type is typically used in interior applications, such as insulating attic floors, wall cavities (especially in conditioned attics), and crawl spaces. It provides excellent thermal insulation and is known for its sound-dampening properties, which can be particularly beneficial for homes near busy streets or for apartments seeking quieter environments within the 7600 4th Ave S area.

2. Closed-Cell Spray Foam: This denser, more rigid foam offers superior insulating value (R-value) and acts as a vapor barrier. It is ideal for applications requiring both insulation and structural strength, such as new construction, basement and crawlspace insulation, roof decking reinforcement, and areas prone to high moisture. Closed-cell foam also provides a degree of fire resistance and can contribute to the structural integrity of a building here in Minneapolis.

Common Applications in the Minneapolis Area

We tailor our spray foam solutions to address the specific needs of properties in this region:

• Attic Insulation: Properly insulating the attic floor is often the most cost-effective way to improve a home's energy efficiency in Minneapolis's colder climate. Spray foam creates an airtight seal, preventing heated air from escaping through the roof.

• Wall Cavity Insulation: Ensuring walls are fully and correctly insulated is crucial for maintaining comfortable indoor temperatures year-round. Spray foam fills gaps and irregularities often missed by traditional fiberglass or cellulose insulation, providing a uniform, high-R-value barrier.

• Basement and Crawl Space Encapsulation: Sealing these often overlooked areas is vital for preventing moisture intrusion, controlling humidity, and reducing heating and cooling costs. Our spray foam creates an effective moisture barrier and air seal, transforming these spaces into conditioned living or storage areas.

• Foundation and Rim Joist Areas: The perimeter of the house, including foundation walls and rim joists (the top plates of the walls resting on the rim joist), are notorious for air leaks. Spray foam effectively seals these areas, enhancing overall energy performance.

• New Construction and Retrofits: We work with both new builds and existing structures throughout the Minneapolis area, ensuring that insulation is correctly integrated from the start or effectively retrofitted for maximum benefit.
