Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is considered a wrongful death in West Palm Beach?

A: A wrongful death in West Palm Beach occurs when the death of a person is caused by the wrongful act, neglect, or failure of another party. This can include car accidents, workplace accidents, medical malpractice, defective products, and other incidents.

Q: How long do I have to file a wrongful death claim in Florida?

A: In Florida, the statute of limitations for a wrongful death claim is generally two years from the date of the deceased's death. It is crucial to consult with an attorney promptly.

Q: Do I need a lawyer for my West Palm Beach wrongful death case?

A: Yes, it is highly recommended to have an experienced West Palm Beach wrongful death attorney. They can investigate the case, identify liable parties, gather evidence, and navigate the complex legal system to maximize your chances of receiving fair compensation.

Q: What types of compensation can be sought?

A: Potential compensation includes medical expenses, funeral and burial costs, lost income, loss of companionship and support, and pain and suffering.

Q: How much will it cost to hire a West Palm Beach wrongful death attorney?

A: Most wrongful death attorneys, including those at our West Palm Beach office, work on a contingency fee basis. This means you only pay if we win your case, and our fee is a percentage of the settlement or court award.
