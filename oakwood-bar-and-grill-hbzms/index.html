Frequently Asked Questions

What type of American food do you offer?

At Oakwood Bar and Grill, we focus on classic American dishes prepared with quality ingredients. Our menu features hearty staples like our signature Lamb Chops, alongside burgers, sandwiches, salads, and other comfort foods presented with a modern touch.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, we highly recommend making a reservation for an optimal dining experience, especially during peak hours or for larger parties. You can reserve your table by calling (313) 451-8970 or through our website.

What are your operating hours?

We are typically open for lunch from 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM and dinner from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week. Please call (313) 451-8970 for the most current hours, as they may vary slightly.

Is there a dress code?

Oakwood Bar and Grill maintains a smart casual dress code. Collared shirts and dress pants for gentlemen, and dresses or skirts for ladies are appropriate. Casual wear like jeans and sneakers are generally acceptable for lunch.

Can I make a catering request?

Absolutely! We offer catering services for groups of 20 or more for both on-site and off-site events. Please contact our restaurant directly at (313) 451-8970 to discuss your catering needs and receive a customized quote.
