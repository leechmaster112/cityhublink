Ontario Eviction Services

Ontario Eviction Services is a licensed paralegal practice that represents landlords through the Landlord and Tenant Board process in Ontario. The firm handles eviction and possession matters from start to finish, including notice preparation and service, application filing, hearing representation, orders, and enforcement. Common matters include rent arrears recovery through the N4 notice and L1 application, lease termination, and possession recovery, along with the full range of notice types under the Residential Tenancies Act. Pricing is flat fee and transparent, with the scope documented clearly in the engagement letter so landlords know the cost before work begins. Ontario Eviction Services acts for landlords only and is built around landlord economics, from individual owners with a single rental to property management firms with many filings to process. The practice serves Toronto first and supports landlords across Ontario. The goal is to move matters through the Board efficiently, protect the owner rights, and recover possession or arrears with as little delay as possible.
