Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of carpets do you clean?

We clean a wide variety of carpet types, including but not limited to Berber, shag, wool, nylon, polyester, and olefin. Our techniques and equipment are versatile enough to handle different textures and materials effectively.

Q2: How long does the cleaning process take?

The time required depends on the size of the area and the level of soiling. Generally, a standard living room and hallway take 1-2 hours. We always provide a specific timeframe during our initial assessment.

Q3: Are your cleaning products safe for homes with children and pets?

Absolutely. We exclusively use eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning solutions that are safe around children and pets. Our products are designed to be effective yet gentle on your family and the environment.

Q4: Will my carpets be completely dry before I can walk on them?

Yes. We use advanced extraction methods and powerful fans to ensure your carpets are dried thoroughly and quickly, usually within 4-6 hours, allowing you to walk on them comfortably.

Q5: Do you offer stain removal services?

Yes, we specialize in stain removal. We offer both preventative advice and treatment for common stains like spills, ink, wine, and more. The success of stain removal often depends on the type and age of the stain.
