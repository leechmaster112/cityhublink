Bill Witt City Park Dog Park

Bill Witt City Park on Yorktown Blvd features Corpus Christi's dedicated off-leash dog park, a secure fenced setting divided into separate sections for small and large dogs. Practical amenities include double gates, water stations, shaded benches, leash hooks, and waste stations with complimentary bags.
