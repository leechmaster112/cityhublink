Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of transmission services do you offer at your Springfield location?

A1: Ace Transmission Service at 2610 W. Kearney St in Springfield offers a full range of transmission services, including inspections, fluid flushes, transmission repairs, rebuilds, and replacements.

Q2: How often should I get a transmission service for my vehicle?

A2: While recommendations vary by vehicle make and model, we generally recommend transmission inspections every 30,000 to 50,000 miles or annually. A full transmission service like a fluid flush may be recommended every 50,000 to 100,000 miles.

Q3: What is the difference between transmission repair and a transmission rebuild?

A3: Transmission repair addresses specific issues like leaks or component failures. A rebuild involves disassembling the transmission, replacing worn parts, and refurbishing it to restore it to near-new condition, often providing better performance than simple repairs.

Q4: How long does a typical transmission repair or rebuild take?

A4: The duration depends on the complexity of the issue. Minor repairs might take a few days, while a full rebuild can take several weeks. We will provide you with an estimated timeline once the diagnosis is complete.

Q5: Do you use original equipment manufacturer (OEM) parts or remanufactured parts?

A5: We use a combination of high-quality OEM parts and remanufactured components, carefully selected for compatibility and reliability. We will discuss part choices with you and explain the benefits of each option.
