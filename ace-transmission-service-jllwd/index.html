Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What transmission services do you offer at your Springfield location?

Q: How long does a typical transmission repair take at your shop?

A: The duration of transmission repairs depends on the specific issue and required parts. Our team will provide you with an accurate estimate after diagnosis. Complex repairs may require several days to ensure quality workmanship.

Q: Do you service all makes and models of vehicles in Springfield?

A: Yes, our skilled technicians are experienced with a wide range of transmission types found in various makes and models commonly serviced by Springfield residents.

Q: What should I do if my transmission is slipping?

A: Transmission slipping can indicate several potential issues. We recommend scheduling a diagnostic check at our Springfield location as soon as you notice this symptom to prevent further damage.

Q: How often should I get a transmission fluid change?

A: It's generally recommended to change transmission fluid and filters according to your vehicle manufacturer's specifications, typically every 50,000 miles or 5-7 years, whichever comes first. Regular checks at Ace Transmission Service can help maintain your transmission's health.
