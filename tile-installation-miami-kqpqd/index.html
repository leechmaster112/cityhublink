The timeline depends on the size of the area, the complexity of the design, and the type of tile being used. We provide a detailed estimate after your initial consultation at our Miami location.

Yes, we offer design consultation to help you choose the right tile and layout for your space. We can provide samples and guidance to ensure you make the best choice for your Miami-area home or business.

Absolutely! We understand the importance of choosing durable and appropriate tiles for Miami's environment. Our team can advise on factors like slip resistance, moisture resistance, and aesthetic appeal to help you select the perfect tile for your specific needs.
