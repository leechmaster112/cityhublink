Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of DUI cases do you handle?

A1: We handle all types of DUI/DWI cases in Vancouver, including first-time offenses, repeat offenses, breathalyzer issues, field sobriety test failures, and cases involving blood alcohol content (BAC) over the legal limit.

Q2: How can an attorney help with a criminal charge?

A2: An attorney can investigate the circumstances of your case, identify potential legal defenses, advise you on the best course of action, negotiate with the prosecutor if appropriate, and represent you effectively in court to protect your rights and minimize potential consequences.

Q3: Do I need a lawyer for a minor traffic violation?

A3: While seemingly minor, traffic violations can sometimes lead to points on your license or other penalties. An attorney can advise you on the potential consequences and help you navigate the process, ensuring your rights are protected.

Q4: What should I do if I'm arrested?

A4: If you are arrested, it is crucial to exercise your right to remain silent and avoid speaking to law enforcement without consulting with a criminal defense attorney. Contact our office immediately at (360) 828-1440 to discuss your situation.

Q5: How long does a DUI case typically take?

A5: The timeline for a DUI case can vary significantly depending on factors like the specifics of the case, potential defenses, plea negotiations, and court scheduling. We will keep you informed throughout the process and work to resolve your case in a timely manner.
