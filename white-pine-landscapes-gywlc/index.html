White Pine Landscapes

White Pine Landscapes is a full-service landscaping company based in Spring Hill, TN, providing professional landscape maintenance, installations, and hardscaping for residential and commercial properties. We focus on creating and maintaining outdoor spaces that are clean, functional, and built to last. Our services include routine landscape maintenance, full landscape installs, and custom hardscaping such as patios, walkways, and retaining walls. White Pine Landscapes proudly serves Spring Hill, Columbia, Murfreesboro, Nashville, Franklin, and Brentwood with dependable service and attention to detail. Whether you need ongoing maintenance or a complete landscape upgrade, our team is committed to quality workmanship and long-term results.

Location

At White Pine Landscapes, we take pride in being a full-service landscaping company dedicated exclusively to enhancing the beauty and functionality of properties right here in our Spring Hill community. Located at 1839 Lasea Rd, Spring Hill, TN 37174, our team is committed to providing exceptional landscaping services tailored to the unique needs of our local clients. We understand the importance of a well-maintained landscape, and we strive to deliver professional, reliable, and creative solutions that transform outdoor spaces into inviting extensions of your home or business.

Our expertise spans the entire lifecycle of a landscape project, from initial design and consultation to installation and ongoing maintenance. We believe that a beautiful landscape is not just about aesthetics; it's about creating a harmonious blend of nature and functionality that enhances your quality of life. Whether you're looking to establish a lush, green lawn, design a custom garden, or maintain a sophisticated landscape, our dedicated team at White Pine Landscapes is here to bring your vision to life.

Our Services Include:

Landscape Design: We begin every project with a thorough consultation to understand your goals, preferences, and the specific characteristics of your property. Our designers work closely with you to create a customized landscape plan that incorporates native plants, efficient irrigation, and sustainable practices, ensuring your landscape is both beautiful and environmentally responsible.

Landscape Installation: Once your design is finalized, our skilled team handles the complete installation process. From soil preparation and hardscaping to planting and finishing touches, we ensure that your landscape is built to last and meets the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.

Landscape Maintenance: To keep your landscape looking its best year-round, we offer comprehensive maintenance services. This includes regular mowing, pruning, fertilizing, mulching, and seasonal cleanups. Our proactive approach helps prevent issues before they arise, saving you time and effort while maintaining the health and beauty of your landscape.

Seasonal Landscape Care: Spring Hill's climate demands specific care at different times of the year. Our team provides tailored seasonal services, including spring cleanups, summer water management, fall planting, and winter protection, ensuring your landscape thrives throughout the year.

Tree and Shrub Care: Healthy trees and shrubs are the backbone of a vibrant landscape. We offer expert tree trimming, pruning, and removal services, as well as planting and care for shrubs and groundcovers. Our team is trained to handle all tree care needs safely and effectively.

Hardscaping: Enhance your outdoor living spaces with beautiful hardscaping features. We design and install durable and attractive elements such as patios, walkways, retaining walls, and decorative stone features that complement your landscape and provide functional areas for relaxation and entertainment.
