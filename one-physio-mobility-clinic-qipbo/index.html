Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

What conditions do you treat?

We treat a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, including back and neck pain, sports injuries, joint pain (knee, shoulder, hip), post-surgical recovery, arthritis, pelvic pain, and mobility limitations. We also offer specialized care for women's health issues and pediatric needs.

How do I book an appointment?

You can easily book your appointment online via our website https://1physioandmobility.ca/. Alternatively, you can call our clinic directly at (403) 984-1669. Walk-in appointments may also be available depending on therapist availability.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your initial assessment will involve a thorough discussion of your medical history and the reason for your visit. This will be followed by a comprehensive physical examination, including movement analysis and specific tests to assess your condition. Based on our findings, we will develop a personalized treatment plan and discuss it with you.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept a variety of extended health benefits plans. Please contact our clinic to verify your specific coverage details and understand any potential out-of-pocket costs before your appointment.

How long does treatment typically take?

The duration of treatment varies significantly depending on the nature and severity of your condition, your overall health, and your commitment to the prescribed home exercise program. Your physiotherapist will provide you with a realistic timeline during your assessment.
