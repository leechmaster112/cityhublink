Ruff Range Dog Park

Ruff Range Dog Park is a 5-acre fenced off-leash area within B.F. Phillips Community Park in Frisco, Texas, with separate sections for small dogs under 35 pounds and large dogs over. Amenities include water stations, dog wash stations, shade structures, agility equipment, seating, and pavilions, with walking trails and disc golf nearby. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is Ruff Range Dog Park suitable for dogs of all sizes?

A: While our 5-acre fenced area at 5335 4th Army Dr, Frisco, TX 75034 is large enough for plenty of running, we do not currently offer separate areas for small and large dogs. All dogs share the same expansive space.

Q: What are the rules for using the off-leash area?

A: To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone at Ruff Range Dog Park, all dogs must remain under voice control and within sight. Owners are responsible for cleaning up after their pets. Please refer to the park's signage at 5335 4th Army Dr for a complete list of rules.

Q: Can I bring my reactive dog to Ruff Range Dog Park?

A: Ruff Range Dog Park is an off-leash facility, which means dogs are expected to be social and comfortable interacting with other dogs. If your dog is reactive, it may be best to consider other options or visit during less busy times, always keeping your dog on a leash until you are safely inside the fenced area.

Q: Are there any amenities for dog owners at Ruff Range Dog Park?

A: Our primary focus is providing a large, secure off-leash space at 5335 4th Army Dr, Frisco, TX 75034. While we don't have specific human amenities like benches or water fountains, the expansive grassy fields offer plenty of room for dogs to play and owners to observe.

Q: How do I find Ruff Range Dog Park within B.F. Phillips Community Park?

A: Ruff Range Dog Park is located within B.F. Phillips Community Park at 5335 4th Army Dr, Frisco, TX 75034. Look for the designated fenced area designed for off-leash canine fun.
