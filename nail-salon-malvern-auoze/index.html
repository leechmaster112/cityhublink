Welcome to Nail Salon Malvern, your premier destination for exceptional nail care right here in Malvern. Located at 215 Lancaster Ave c9, Malvern, PA 19355, our intimate and welcoming salon is dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain beautiful, healthy nails.

We take immense pride in offering top-tier nail care services meticulously tailored to your unique preferences and needs. At Nail Salon Malvern, we understand that beautiful nails are more than just aesthetics; they are a form of self-expression and care. We offer a comprehensive range of services designed to pamper your hands and feet:

Manicures: Our classic manicures include thorough cleaning, cuticle care, nail shaping, and a flawless polish application. For those seeking extra pampering, we offer deluxe manicures which may include warm paraffin wax treatment.

Pedicures: Indulge your feet with our range of pedicure services. From basic shaping and filing to deep cleansing and nourishing treatments, our pedicures leave your feet feeling soft, smooth, and revitalized. We also provide polish application for toes.

Nail Extensions: Enhance the length and shape of your natural nails with our professional nail extension services. We utilize high-quality systems to create durable, natural-looking extensions that allow you to wear your favorite styles for weeks.

Basic Nail Care: We also offer essential nail care services like nail filing, cuticle trimming, and polish removal, ensuring your nails always look their best between full service visits.

Relax in our tranquil and clean environment while our expert team attends to your nail needs. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of hygiene and sanitation to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all our valued clients from the Malvern community.
